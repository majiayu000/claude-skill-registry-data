# Legal Operations

Choose one primary mode and read only its workflow. Add modes only when the matter requires their distinct artifacts.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `legal-research-workflows` | Organize issues, jurisdiction, authority hierarchy, procedural posture, caveats, and counsel questions. | [Legal research](references/workflows/legal-research-workflows.md) |
| `contract-review-operations` | Extract clauses, obligations, dates, operational effects, risks, and negotiation questions. | [Contract review](references/workflows/contract-review-operations.md) |
| `records-retention-operations` | Map records, authority, schedules, holds, disposition, approvals, and audit trails. | [Records retention](references/workflows/records-retention-operations.md) |

Do not load sibling workflows or artifacts prospectively. Preserve source text and uncertainty; stop before legal conclusions, signatures, disposition, negotiation, or filing actions reserved for counsel or authorized owners.
