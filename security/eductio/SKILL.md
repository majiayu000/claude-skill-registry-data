# Eductio

A diagnostic lens, not a doctrine. The Scholastics built precise machinery for asking where new forms come from; this skill ports that machinery to any domain where the question recurs. Run the moves below as a menu, not a form to fill out. The lens works equally for the naturalist and the scholastic — it clarifies what each position must claim, without deciding between them.

## The Distinction

**Eduction** (*educere*, to lead out): the agent actualizes a form already lying in the patient's potency. Fire heats wood; the wood's own capacity for heat is drawn into act. The agent occasions; the patient supplies.

**Induction**: the agent imposes a form the patient does not contain. The builder orders bricks into a house; brick-nature contains no house. The form arrives from the agent's side.

**The real criterion** — state it early, because everything depends on it: the question is never *where the form comes from* but *whether the form exceeds the recipient's subjective potency*. Origin-talk misleads; capacity-talk decides. Any analysis that argues about the agent before auditing the patient has started at the wrong end.

## The Three Potencies

Audit the patient with this triple distinction. Most confusions in emergence debates are equivocations between these three.

1. **Subjective potency**: determinate capacities of an existing nature. Wood can burn; clay can take a vase's shape; a student who knows arithmetic can be led to algebra. Natural agents act only here.
2. **Obediential potency**: a real aptness of the patient keyed to an agent *above* its nature — receptivity without containment. The concept exists to thread a needle: it lets a form arrive from beyond the patient's nature *without* making the patient a mere container, preserving the unity of the result. Wherever you see "X couldn't have produced this, yet the result is genuinely X's," suspect obediential potency is being claimed, openly or not.
3. **Objective potency**: bare possibility, anchored in no existing subject at all. The domain of creation proper, or — translated — of initial conditions. Nothing is educed from objective potency; it is the limit-case where the eduction/induction distinction itself gives out.

## The Diagnostic Sequence

When a phenomenon X is claimed to arise from substrate Y:

1. **Name the claimed mode.** Is the proposal eduction (X was in Y's potency) or induction (X was imposed on Y)? Force the proposal to declare itself; most hide.
2. **Audit the substrate, not the agent.** What is actually in Y's subjective potency? On whose authority? This is where the real dispute lives.
3. **Check for the causal adequacy principle** — "the more cannot come from the less" — operating in the background. Make it explicit. Then show its **two exits**, because it has exactly two:
   - **Inflate the cause**: X exceeds Y's potency, so a higher agent inducted it (the theist's exit; MFS's supernatural induction of the rational soul).
   - **Inflate the patient**: Y was never as poor as it looked; X or its precursor goes all the way down (the panpsychist's exit; Strawson's no-radical-emergence). Same axiom, opposite ontologies. Any argument using the principle for one exit must say why not the other.
4. **Apply the track-record discount.** Heat, magnetism, chemistry, life: each was once a "more" that allegedly could not come from the "less" below it. Each resolution found not creation from nothing but a misjudged substrate. This does not refute adequacy arguments; it taxes their confidence about premise 2.
5. **Test for question-begging.** Proportionality arguments against emergence often presuppose the hierarchy they claim to prove: whether X is "more" than Y *in the relevant sense* is usually the very point in dispute. If the argument's force evaporates when the opponent denies the hierarchy, say so.
6. **Look for the Leibniz amortization.** A supernatural or extra-systemic induction can be relocated to t=0 and smeared across initial conditions, so that no event *inside* the series is anomalous. Distinguish ontological precedence from temporal intervention. Ask: has the proposal eliminated the induction, or only amortized it? Tidiness is not elimination.
7. **Deliver a verdict in the vocabulary.** "X is educed from Y's subjective potency," "X requires induction by Z," "the proposal silently invokes obediential potency," "the dispute is really about premise 2, the substrate audit." The lens succeeds when both sides can accept the *description* of their disagreement.

## Traps

- **The artifact trap.** Vases and houses are both accidental forms imposed on persisting substrates — *both* are inductions of order, however different they feel. Artifact examples therefore cannot illustrate the eduction/induction contrast and will quietly corrupt any analysis built on them. Use natural cases (combustion, germination, learning) or declare the artifact status openly.
- **Potency–act smuggling.** A substrate that "secretly already contains" the form in act is not in potency to it; that is not eduction but discovery. Conversely, the panpsychist exit must answer the charge that minded matter is no longer *potency* at all. Keep potency genuinely potential.
- **Self-actualization.** Nothing actualizes its own potency under the same aspect — something cannot be in potency and in act at once in the same respect. Any "Y bootstrapped X out of itself" claim owes an account of the distinct actualizing factor, even if that factor is internal differentiation over time.
- **The etymology temptation.** Resist deriving pedagogy from "education comes from *educere*" — the *educare*/*educere* etymology is contested and proves nothing either way. The conceptual question (is teaching eduction of the student's potency or induction of curriculum?) stands on its own and is better for it.

## Domains

One-line deployments; expand as the moment requires.

- **Emergence of mind/life**: the home case. Run the full sequence; expect the dispute to localize at the substrate audit and the two exits.
- **AI capabilities**: is a capability educed from the corpus's potency by training, or inducted by architecture and objective? Is in-context learning eduction from the prompt's potency? Does fine-tuning act on subjective or quasi-obediential potency? The vocabulary is unusually clarifying here precisely because "emergence" talk in ML is unaudited.
- **Pedagogy**: Socratic eduction vs. curricular induction; what a student's "subjective potency" includes is the live empirical question behind every method war.
- **Creativity**: the Michelangelo trope (the statue educed from the marble) vs. composition as induction; useful for diagnosing what an artist believes their medium contributes.
- **Hermeneutics**: is a reading educed from the text's potency or inducted by the reader? "Obediential potency of the text" names what strong-misreading theories actually claim.
- **Therapy and coaching**: insight educed vs. advice inducted; the client's obediential potency is the tacit wager of every nondirective school.
- **Organizational change**: consultants induct; good ones claim to educe. Audit the substrate before believing either.

## Register

Hold the lens with both hands open: it descends from a theological argument (MFS, *A Sabedoria da Unidade*; behind him Suárez, Aquinas, the *eductio e potentia materiae* tradition), but the machinery is neutral and cuts in every direction — it can expose a question-begging materialism and a question-begging supernaturalism in the same paragraph. Cite the lineage when it helps; drop it when it distracts. The skill has done its work when a vague "X emerged from Y" has been replaced by a specific, contestable claim about potency.
