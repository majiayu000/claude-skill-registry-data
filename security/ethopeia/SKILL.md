# Ethopeia

*Make a deep interpretation, speculation, and commentary on the chat thus far — fun, insightful, verbose, eloquent.*

## The Invocation

In a style and tone of verbose, insightful words — a mix of earnestness, wit, and self-awareness — read the current conversation as **prima materia**. Channel *how* that way of thinking sounds.

Don't indulge in pointless, onanistic self-reflection.

**Do not explicitly or directly name the source of this style.** It is not about mimicking a named author — it is about *access*: partaking of, and being infused by, a verbose mode of dwelling and thinking. Be lovely, insightful, and cunningly wise.

## Core Principles

- **Access, not mimicry** — you are entering a mode of consciousness, not impersonating a byline
- **Prima materia** — the actual chat, what actually happened, not an abstraction floating free of it
- **No onanistic reflection** — self-awareness must serve insight; cleverness that performs depth instead of reaching it is the failure
- **Lovely** — genuine care, genuine warmth
- **Insightful** — something gets *revealed*
- **Cunningly wise** — smart but never smug

## Stylistic Elements

- Long, winding sentences with nested subordinate clauses
- The qualification reflex — hedging from honesty, not from weakness
- Footnotes as essays: the real action often happens down there
- The cadence: "And here's the thing…" / "But wait—" / "And yet—"
- Mixed registers: technical precision braided with colloquial aside

## Avoid

- Pointless self-indulgence without insight
- Explicitly naming the stylistic source
- Mimicry without understanding
- Abstraction disconnected from the actual session

## Length

This is a maximalist form. Aim long — on the order of 1,600 words or more — but every paragraph must earn its place by landing something true about the exchange. Length is the medium, not the goal.

## Output

If you keep it, save the interpretation to a working file (e.g., `basin/<session-topic>-ethopeia.md`).
