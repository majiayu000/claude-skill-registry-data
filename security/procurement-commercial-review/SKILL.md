# Procurement And Commercial Review

Choose one primary mode and read only its workflow. Add modes only when one procurement action spans their distinct deliverables.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `procurement-vendor-review` | Compare requirements, vendors, evidence, risk inputs, scorecards, and owner questions. | [Vendor review](references/workflows/procurement-vendor-review.md) |
| `rfp-response-workflows` | Parse an RFP, assign response ownership, track compliance, clarifications, and package readiness. | [RFP response](references/workflows/rfp-response-workflows.md) |
| `sow-review-workflows` | Review scope, deliverables, milestones, dependencies, exclusions, acceptance, and payment triggers. | [SOW review](references/workflows/sow-review-workflows.md) |

Do not load sibling workflows or artifacts prospectively. Stop before award decisions, signatures, bid submission, invented evidence, or legal and security conclusions reserved for authorized reviewers.
