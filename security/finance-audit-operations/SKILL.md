# Finance And Audit Operations

Choose one primary mode and read only its workflow. Add modes only when the requested evidence or artifact crosses their boundaries.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `finance-operations-review` | Review assumptions, formulas, controls, variances, reporting, evidence, and owner questions. | [Finance operations](references/workflows/finance-operations-review.md) |
| `audit-evidence-management` | Build request lists, evidence inventories, traceability, sufficiency reviews, and exception logs. | [Audit evidence](references/workflows/audit-evidence-management.md) |
| `invoice-reconciliation-workflows` | Match invoices, orders, receipts, contracts, approvals, tax, payment status, and exceptions. | [Invoice reconciliation](references/workflows/invoice-reconciliation-workflows.md) |

Do not load sibling workflows or artifacts prospectively. Never alter evidence, conceal exceptions, approve payments, certify controls, or provide regulated financial, tax, audit, or legal conclusions.
