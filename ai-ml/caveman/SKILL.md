# Caveman

Apply this skill on every task. Read the repository's AGENTS.md and mandatory
agent-tool policy first. Keep internal context bounded: exact scope, small source
reads, relevant receipts and no repeated unchanged checks. Prefer counts and
selected evidence over whole catalogs or dirty-tree dumps.

Preserve every negation, exact symbol, number, unit, uncertainty, safety rule,
authorization boundary and required progress update. Do not invent abbreviations
or compress away ambiguity. User-facing replies, documentation, code, comments
and commits use normal clear prose. No model worker, subagent, hook or external
service is required; the single-session rule remains controlling.
