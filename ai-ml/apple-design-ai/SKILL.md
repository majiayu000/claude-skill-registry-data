# Apple AI Experience Design

## Apply the skill

Begin with the user benefit and the consequence of an incorrect result. Decide whether the model is essential or complementary, reactive or proactive, and private or shared. Read the relevant AI reference before designing interaction details.

Specify inputs, uncertainty, generation or prediction states, editing, retry, dismissal, and recovery. Keep model output distinguishable from verified facts or completed actions. Separate suggesting an action from executing it, and preserve the user’s existing authorization boundaries.

Evaluate realistic failures and corrections as well as successful examples. Include a useful path when the feature is unavailable or declined. Present model-quality claims only with supporting evidence; UI wording cannot establish reliability.
