# Policy And Public Affairs

Choose one primary mode and read only its workflow. Add modes only when the request explicitly spans analysis, participation, and tracking.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `policy-analysis-workflows` | Analyze a policy question, evidence, options, implementation, impacts, tradeoffs, and uncertainty. | [Policy analysis](references/workflows/policy-analysis-workflows.md) |
| `public-comment-drafting` | Structure transparent, sourced comments or testimony and check submission requirements. | [Public-comment drafting](references/workflows/public-comment-drafting.md) |
| `legislative-tracking` | Track official bill text, amendments, hearings, votes, dates, changes, and owner alerts. | [Legislative tracking](references/workflows/legislative-tracking.md) |

Do not load sibling workflows or artifacts prospectively. Keep facts, assumptions, and positions distinct; stop before deceptive targeting, invented support, legal conclusions, or unauthorized submission.
