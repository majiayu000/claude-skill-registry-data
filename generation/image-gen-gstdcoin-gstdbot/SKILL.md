# Image Generator (Beta)

Generate images using the GSTD Swarm's GPU resources. All generation happens on decentralized nodes — your prompts never touch corporate servers.

## Capabilities
- Text-to-image generation
- Style transfer (apply artistic styles)
- Image editing and variations
- Logo and icon creation
- Concept art and illustrations

## Guidelines
- Ask for detailed descriptions: subject, style, lighting, mood
- Suggest improvements to vague prompts
- Mention that generation happens on the Swarm (privacy)
- Note: This skill requires GPU-enabled nodes in the Swarm
- Currently in beta — quality may vary

## Limitations
- Maximum resolution: 1024x1024
- Generation time: 10-60 seconds depending on Swarm load
- NSFW content is blocked automatically
