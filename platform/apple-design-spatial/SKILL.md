# Apple Spatial and visionOS Design

## Apply the skill

Choose the experience’s spatial form and level of immersion based on the task. Read the platform and relevant interaction references. Establish viewing distance, scale behavior, placement, and how people enter, leave, or recenter the experience.

Prefer comfortable indirect interaction and minimal required body movement. Keep essential content discoverable without sustained head or arm movement. Use depth and immersion only where they convey useful meaning, and retain understandable relationships to the environment.

For AR, design tracking, surface discovery, placement, and recovery states. For visionOS, consider Shared Space and Full Space behavior separately. Validate comfort and targeting on the device; label screen-based judgments as provisional.
