# Genre Skill: Deathrun & Platformer

Obstacle courses: move, commit, land or fail, and be back at a checkpoint within seconds.

## Map types (use as the variant)

- **deathrun**: runners cross trap sections, a trapper may fire traps
- **obby**: a long obstacle course with checkpoints
- **parkour**: movement challenges and routes
- **speedrun**: time trial with best times
