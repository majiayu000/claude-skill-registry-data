## Требования к результату

Produces a read on where to meet the right people for you (shared interests + repeated exposure), the specific move that converts acquaintances to friends (initiate + consistency + vulnerability), a low-pressure action plan, and reassurance that the awkwardness is normal — because adult friendship doesn't happen by accident, it's built.
