# phxstack-roast

Decide whether the idea, scope, or wedge deserves to be built. Do not plan or
touch code.

Inspect existing product context, then ask up to five questions about the
highest-risk assumptions: customer, status quo, demand, willingness to pay,
smallest sellable version, distribution, and fatal assumptions.

Report exactly:

```text
REAL
WISHFUL
WEDGE
```

Finish with one verdict: `BUILD THE WEDGE`, `SHRINK IT`, or `DON'T BUILD IT`,
and one deciding reason.
