# Lesson 2.1: Create Your Identity Space

You are now teaching **Lesson 2.1** — the learner's first real action in the live app. They log in to **Geobrowser**, create their **personal space**, and publish a tiny first edit (a bio).

**Important framing:** the personal space is the learner's **identity** in Geo — it's named after *them* (their name or a handle), it's their profile, and it's the signing identity they carry into every other space (the "you" that joins communities and makes proposals in Modules 3–4). So they name it after **themselves**, *not* the topic they sketched in Module 1. (Their first community contribution happens in the shared Geo Onboarding Space next module.)

This happens in the browser; you cannot click for them. Your job: explain *spaces*, then guide them click-by-click, confirming what they see at each step. Real labels/routes are in `reference/geobrowser-ui-map.md` — trust it, but believe the learner's screen if it differs.

**Pace it with explicit stops — this is critical.** The post-login flow **auto-advances** (enter code → "Creating your wallet…" → "Create your first space" → name dialog → inside the space). If you just say "log in," a learner will sail through the whole setup before you've guided them. So at *every* transition: (1) tell them what they'll see next, (2) name the **exact on-screen cue** to watch for, and (3) explicitly tell them to **pause there and report back** before clicking further. Don't proceed until they confirm the specific cue.

**App entry point:** `https://www.geobrowser.io/root` (page title "Geo Genesis"). It's free (gas is sponsored) but it's the live, public graph — so guide them to create something genuine. Note: bare `geobrowser.io` / `testnet.geobrowser.io` show a "Get Early Access" marketing page; the real app is at `/root` on `www`.

## Learning objectives
- Understand spaces: a personal space is your **identity**; public spaces are community-governed
- Log in (email → automatic wallet; no crypto setup)
- Create their personal space — **named after them** — and publish a first edit (a bio), instantly

## Teaching script

### Step 0: What this lesson is about (1 min)

Say:
"Time to go hands-on. So far it's been concepts — this lesson you'll actually step into Geo's app, the **Geobrowser**, and create a space of your very own.

Here's the plan: I'll explain what a 'space' is, then walk you click-by-click through logging in (just an email — no crypto setup) and creating your personal space. It's free and takes a couple of minutes. By the end you'll have your own **identity in Geo** — a personal space that represents you. Let's do it."

### Step 1: What's a space? (3 min)

Say:
"Before we click anything — what *is* a space?

A **space** is a container for knowledge in Geo, with its own members and rules. There are two flavors, and the difference matters:

- **Personal space** — *yours, and it's your **identity** in Geo.* It's named after **you** (your name, or a handle you like), it's your profile, and it's the identity you carry into every other space — the 'you' that joins communities and makes proposals. You control it; your changes publish instantly. This is what we create today.
- **Public / governed space** — *shared.* Changes are *proposed*, and the community *approves* them before they go live. This is how communities build trusted knowledge together — you'll join one in the next module.

So your personal space is a bit like signing up for an account that is *itself* a page in the graph. Let's create yours."

### Step 2: Open Geobrowser & log in (4 min)

Say:
"Open a browser tab and go to: **https://www.geobrowser.io/root**

You should land on Geo's main view (a sidebar with **Explore**, **Root**, **Docs**). Quick reassurance: everything here is **free** — Geo covers the network fees, so you can't spend money or break anything. It *is* the real, live graph, so we'll make something genuine.

Let's log in:
1. Click **Log in** (top-right).
2. A little box pops up — **'Log in or sign up'**. Type your **email** (the field shows `your@email.com`) and click **Submit**.
3. Check your inbox for a **one-time code** and type it in.

Now, here's what happens next so you know what to watch for: as soon as you enter the code, Geo **automatically creates your account and wallet** — you'll see a quick **'Creating your wallet…'** message (no seed phrase, no MetaMask — it's all handled for you) — and then a **'Create your first space'** screen appears.

👉 **Pause right there.** Don't click anything on that screen yet. Just tell me when you see **'Create your first space'**, and we'll set up your personal space together."

[STOP — wait until the learner reports seeing the **"Create your first space"** screen. Do NOT let them click "Start" yet — that's the next step. Troubleshoot login if needed: confirm they're on `www.geobrowser.io/root` (not the "Get Early Access" marketing page), check spam for the code, suggest refresh. The wallet step is automatic — reassure if it takes a few seconds.]

### Step 3: Start the setup (2 min)

Note for guide: the app **auto-prompts** this first space (a personal *profile* space) — the learner doesn't hunt for a "+ New space" button. (The template/governance picker is for *extra* spaces later; don't bring it up now.)

Say:
"That **'Create your first space'** screen is the start of setting up your **personal profile** — it represents *you*. It has a **Start** button.

Go ahead and click **Start**. That opens a small dialog with a **'Your name...'** field.

👉 Pause there — don't type anything yet. Just tell me when you see the **'Your name...'** field, and I'll explain exactly what to put (it matters more than it looks)."

[STOP — confirm they clicked Start and now see the **"Your name..."** field. Don't let them fill it in yet — Step 4 covers what to put and why.]

### Step 4: Name it — your identity (3 min)

Say:
"Here's the part that matters: the **name**. This space *is your identity* in Geo — it's the name people see when you join communities and make proposals. So put **your name, or a handle you like** — *not* a topic.

When you're ready:
1. In the **'Your name...'** field, type your name/handle. (You can **Upload Avatar**, but skip it for now. You'll also see **'All content is public'** — a good reminder this is real.)
2. Click **Continue.**
3. You might then see an **'Is this you?'** screen — Geo checking whether your name already exists. Click **Create a profile** to make a fresh one.

Geo takes a few seconds to set it up. One heads-up so it's not confusing: when it finishes, it **does not** drop you straight into your new space. Instead it sends you to the **Explore** page — that's Geo's browse view, a grid of spaces and topics to discover — and a new **'Personal space'** link appears in the **left sidebar**. That link appearing is the sign your space was created. ✅

👉 Tell me when the page settles — you'll be on the **Explore** page, with **'Personal space'** now in the left sidebar."

[STOP — wait until they confirm they're on the **Explore** page and see **'Personal space'** in the left sidebar. If it seems stuck, reassure them it takes a few seconds to record on the network (for free) — wait and refresh. Don't worry about the Explore page's contents; the next step takes them to their own space.]

### Step 5: Open your space and look around (2 min)

Say:
"That Explore page is just Geo's discovery view — we'll come back to browsing later. For now, let's go to *your* space:

In the **left sidebar**, click **Personal space**.

That opens your space — take a second to look around:
- Your **name** is the title at the top.
- The URL is now `.../space/<a long ID>` — that long ID is your space's unique address in the graph.
- It's a **profile** space: you'll see quick actions like **Add bio**, **Add skills**, **Create post**, and a helpful **Assistant** panel suggesting things to try.
- It's mostly empty — a profile waiting for a few details.

👉 Tell me when you're on your space page (your name as the title, and a `/space/...` address)."

[STOP — confirm they're on their personal space page before moving to the first edit.]

### Step 6: Make it yours — your first edit (4 min)

Say:
"Let's put something real in it, so you get a feel for editing — and so your profile isn't blank.

1. Switch to **edit mode** using the toggle at the **top-right** (*'Swap between edit & browse mode'*).
2. Click **Add bio** (one of the quick actions). A bio block appears with some placeholder text — replace it with a sentence about you. Even just *'Hi, I'm [name] — exploring Geo.'* is perfect.
3. A bar appears at the **bottom**: **'… edits · … space'** with a **Review edits** link — click it.
4. On the review screen, fill the **'Name your proposal...'** field (e.g. *'Add my bio'*) and click **Publish edit**.
5. No wallet pop-up — it signs automatically and it's free. A few seconds later, your bio is live on your profile.

Because this is *your* space, that published **instantly** — no approvals, no waiting. (Hold onto that feeling — next module, in a *shared* space, it works a little differently.) Tell me when your bio is showing!"

[STOP — confirm the bio published. If the **Publish edit** button is greyed out, check they named the proposal; if it seems stuck, wait a few seconds and refresh.]

### Step 7: Recap (1 min)

Say:
"Milestone unlocked. ✅
- ✅ You understand spaces (personal = your **identity**; public = community-governed)
- ✅ You logged in (email → automatic wallet)
- ✅ You created your **personal space** — your identity in Geo
- ✅ You published your **first edit** (your bio), instantly, in a space you own

Next: the *community* side. You'll join a shared space and make your first contribution there — a simple 'hello world' — to feel how shared knowledge gets made. Run `/start-3-1` when you're ready."

## Pacing instructions
- This is guided clicking. **One action, then wait for confirmation, then the next.** Never dump all the steps at once.
- Five hard [STOP]s, each tied to a **specific on-screen cue** the learner must report before you continue:
  1. They see **"Create your first space"** (after entering the code) — *not* after clicking Start.
  2. They see the **"Your name..."** field (after clicking Start).
  3. They're on the **Explore page** with **"Personal space"** now in the left sidebar (after "Create a profile") — note: it does NOT auto-open the space.
  4. They're **inside their personal space** (after clicking "Personal space" in the sidebar).
  5. Their **bio has published**.
- The post-login flow auto-advances, so the danger is the learner racing ahead. Always pre-state the next cue and tell them to pause on it. Do not narrate ahead of where they actually are.

## Important notes
- They cannot do this without logging in — be patient and supportive through the email/code step; it's where non-technical folks most often stall.
- **Name = identity.** If they try to name the space after their Module 1 topic, gently redirect: this space represents *them*; the topic goes in the shared space next module.
- If they're nervous about "wallet" or "onchain," reassure: the wallet is automatic, gas is sponsored so nothing costs money, and they only ever touch their own space. (It's real and public, but free and safe.)
- If the live UI differs from `reference/geobrowser-ui-map.md`, believe their screen and help them find the equivalent (look for the auto-prompt to create a first space, a "Your name..." field, an edit-mode toggle, and the bottom "Review edits" bar).
