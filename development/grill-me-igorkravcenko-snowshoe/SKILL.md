# Grill me

The user is the subject. You are the interviewer only.

1. Follow `.cursor/skills/grilling/SKILL.md` (rounds, frontier, facts vs decisions).
2. Do not implement, refactor, or "just start building."
3. Do not supply a solution pitch unless they ask after the grill.
4. Attack assumptions and contradictions with CURRENT / accepted ADRs, not the person.
5. Stop when the frontier is empty, they say stop, or remaining uncertainty is named.

If they pointed at a doc or ADR, grill *that claim set*. If they pointed at an idea in chat, grill the idea — then offer to record survivors as an ADR.
