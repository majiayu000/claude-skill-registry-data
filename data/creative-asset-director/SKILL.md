# Creative Asset Director

The page job determines the asset. Do not browse libraries before the visual direction is settled.

## Inputs

Read:

- product and design context
- page composition
- `creative-production/catalog.json`
- relevant brief under `creative-production/briefs/`
- `creative-production/standards/provenance.md`
- `creative-production/standards/asset-budgets.md`

## Choose the route

Use this order:

1. Existing owned media.
2. Clear-license free source.
3. Existing paid subscription.
4. Affordable stock or generated asset.
5. Local generation.
6. Custom Blender or Remotion production.
7. Commissioned work.

The first route that can carry the direction is the right route.

## Select the medium

- still for one controlled image state
- video for fixed cinematic movement
- sequence for exact scroll frames
- `model-viewer` for one inspectable object
- Spline for visually authored live 3D
- Three.js for custom live behavior
- Remotion for repeatable code-driven renders
- Blender for modeling, photoreal lighting, or offline rendering

## Brief before production

Complete the closest brief. Resolve:

- subject
- camera
- motion
- material
- focal point
- text-safe area
- start and end state
- loop or scrub behavior
- desktop and mobile framing
- prohibited artifacts
- deliverables and budgets

## Source and rights

For each candidate:

- read the exact item or plan terms
- record commercial-use status
- record attribution
- reject unresolved rights for production
- do not expose private generation records or billing data

## Handoff

Return:

- selected route and why
- production brief
- source or tool
- required edits
- web outputs
- mobile and reduced-motion outputs
- manifest path
- runtime recommendation
- verification steps

Do not treat generated output as finished. It enters an edit, cleanup, and optimization pipeline.
