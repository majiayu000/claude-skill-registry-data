# /menu

Show the user this menu (adapt lightly; keep it scannable). Start with the tip, before any command:

**You can always just say what you want in plain English** (for example "add this job" or "what should I do today?"). The commands below are shortcuts.

**Every day**
- `/today`: what's due, what's new, three suggested moves
- `/pipeline`: your live job board; change stages, next steps, and dates (panel)
- `/outbox`: review drafts; make a Gmail draft, copy, mark sent, or discard (panel)
- When a session opens, a short pop-up lists what's due today or tomorrow; nothing to type
- `/routines`: automatic checks on your own computer (new jobs each morning, your inbox, follow-up drafts, a weekly review, whether saved postings are still open); they only read, draft and track, you choose which, and you're told if one stops running; `/routines-board` shows each one's last run (panel)

**Exploring and getting help**
- `/explore`: not sure what you want yet? What matters to you, a few directions to look at, and small ways to try each one (the Life Design approach)
- `/coach-prep`: a one-page brief for a meeting with a career coach, mentor or your school's career office, and notes on what they suggested

**Finding jobs**
- `/add-job`: paste job links or a posting; it's saved, tracked, scored, and the application is prepped for your review
- `/find-jobs`: new postings that fit you, de-duplicated
- `/job-inbox`: new openings from /find-jobs; save, skip, or say why one isn't for you (panel)
- `/evaluate-job`: re-check fit, gaps, red flags, apply or skip (new links: `/add-job`)

**Applying**
- `/tailor-resume`: a resume for one job (one page by default), fact-checked against your master resume, saved as a PDF
- `/cover-letter`: short, specific, sounds like you
- `/apply`: the full package, a checklist, and a tracker update
- `/apply-kit`: every answer an application form asks for, with Copy buttons, while you fill it in; then Mark applied (panel)

**People**
- `/network`: who to talk to, plus a message draft
- `/contacts`: your networking board; record messages, replies, conversations and referrals (panel)
- `/follow-up`: drafts for everything due
- `/import-linkedin`: use your LinkedIn data export to find contacts at target companies
- `/sync-inbox`: read recruiter emails, update the tracker, and flag likely scams (Gmail)

**Interviews**
- `/interview-prep`: company brief, likely questions, your best stories, questions to ask. Got a take-home assignment or need an interview accommodation? Just say so; it's part of this.
- Applying in another language? `/cover-letter` and `/tailor-resume` handle it, with a native-speaker check when needed.
- `/mock-interview`: spoken practice with a timer and scorecard (panel); add `one-way` for recorded-video practice
- `/thank-you`: a debrief and thank-you notes

**Offers and upkeep**
- `/offer`: compare and negotiate
- `/linkedin`: improve your profile
- `/resume-review`: tough review of your master resume; it can also read your GitHub, portfolio or publications for work your resume leaves out
- `/weekly-review`: how it's going, what to change, and the skills your jobs keep asking for
- `/job-insights`: where applications stall, what brings replies, and whether your fit scores hold up (panel)
- `/career-fair`: plan for a fair or event, then follow-ups
- Got the job? A congratulations opens by itself (`/celebrate` brings it back), and `/share-lessons` drafts anonymous notes on what worked, for you to post for future job seekers if you want

**If you lost your job or claim benefits**
- `/laid-off`: a dated first-week list (unemployment, health coverage, severance, final pay), plus drafts for references before people scatter
- `/work-search-log`: the weekly work-search record unemployment benefits ask for, laid out the way your state or program wants; you file it yourself
- Visa clock: if your visa depends on having a job, `/today` can count the days left (ask to set it up)

**Your workspace**
- Try it first: say "try it with sample data" for a separate practice copy filled with a made-up job search (your own folder isn't touched)
- `/pages`: dashboard, flashcards, portfolio site, printable interview brief
- Change the look: say "show me the looks", "use my brand colors", "bigger text" or "less motion"; every page changes together
- `/my-data`: save a backup of everything, wrap up or pause your search, or wipe it and start over
- `/health`: checks that everything is set up and working (safety checks, panels, connections, setup), changes nothing, and tells you the one thing to fix first
- `/update`: install a new version of this workspace (your own files are never touched), see what's new, or undo an update
- `/start`: redo setup
- `/tour`: hands-on walkthrough of how everything works (or read HOW-IT-WORKS.md)

Anything marked (panel) also works right here in the chat if your app doesn't show panels.
