# Enterprise Search

Use this skill when the main problem is finding or consolidating information across multiple sources.

## Workflow
- Define the exact question and artifact scope.
- Retrieve the smallest useful source set.
- Route to deep reading or quick research when needed.

## Deliverables
- A source-backed retrieval summary.
- A short note on coverage or missing context.

## Guardrails
- Do not blur retrieved evidence with inference.
- Do not dump raw search results without synthesis.
