# Slack By Salesforce

Use this skill when the output is meant for Slack or Slack-like team communication.

## Workflow
- Clarify whether the message is an announcement, reply, digest, or workflow update.
- Keep formatting scan-friendly.
- Route to related internal comms or GIF skills when helpful.

## Deliverables
- A Slack-ready message, digest, or workflow note.
- A shorter variant if needed.

## Guardrails
- Do not write email-length walls of text for Slack without a reason.
- Do not hide the ask or action item.
