# AIWF AEO And GEO

## Core Rule

Improve the usefulness, clarity, originality, and verifiability of content for people and machines. Do not promise inclusion, citations, rankings, or traffic, and do not treat unsupported AI-search hacks as requirements.

## Workflow

1. Define the audience, decision, entity, product facts, evidence, geography, and target question set.
2. Audit whether the site makes who, what, where, why, proof, limitations, dates, and authorship unambiguous.
3. Find information gaps where first-party experience, measurements, comparisons, examples, or primary data can add real value.
4. Build a question-to-evidence map and write direct answers supported by nearby context and accessible source links.
5. Keep claims consistent across pages, profiles, documentation, datasets, and public repositories.
6. Measure eligible pages, impressions, referrals, brand/entity queries, cited pages, conversions, and answer accuracy over defined windows.
7. Record observations separately from causes; answer systems are volatile and attribution is incomplete.

Read `references/source-register.json` before claiming that a file, schema, content format, or optimization is required by a search or answer platform.

## Guardrails

- Reject mass-produced answer pages, fabricated expertise, fake mentions, citation bait, hidden text, doorway pages, and copied summaries.
- Do not claim that `llms.txt`, tiny content chunks, special AI rewrites, or excessive structured data guarantees visibility.
- Use structured data only when it matches visible content and a supported vocabulary.
- Add `aiwf-web-seo` for rendering, canonicalization, robots, sitemaps, performance, and indexability.
- Add `aiwf-startup-marketing-growth` for positioning, distribution, funnel, or campaign planning.

## Output

Return prioritized question clusters, entity and evidence gaps, content changes, measurement plan, unsupported tactics rejected, and the next recheck date.
