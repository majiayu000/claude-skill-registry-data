# Social Listening

Mentions arrive whether you watch or not. This skill builds the watch: coverage of where you are talked about, a triage rubric, a response policy, and honest reporting.

## When to use
- Standing up monitoring for a person, brand, or product (mentions, reviews, communities).
- Responding to or escalating a mention; deciding public vs private replies.
- Reporting on reputation trends without fooling yourself.

## Method
1. **Inventory**: names, handles, products, misspellings and variants; owned channels vs third-party sources. List where discussion happens and known blind spots (logged-in-only platforms, DMs).
2. **Coverage setup**: RSS, APIs, saved searches, alerts, webmentions per source; define cadence (real-time for crises, daily digest otherwise).
3. **Triage rubric**: severity × reach × authenticity (is it spam/astroturf?). Route: ignore / monitor / reply / escalate / legal. False positives get tracked too.
4. **Response policy**: who may speak as the brand; tone rules; when to take it to DM; when to publish a statement. Draft + approve templates for common cases.
5. **Alert hygiene**: thresholds, dedupe, quiet hours; no alert storms; test alerts after setup.
6. **Evidence**: archive every actionable mention (screenshot + link) before responding — posts get deleted.
7. **Report**: weekly digest — notable mentions, trend notes, sentiment *with caveats*, actions taken. Separate signal from noise explicitly.

## Quality gates
- [ ] Coverage list written with blind spots acknowledged.
- [ ] Triage rubric + escalation path documented.
- [ ] Response templates approved; authority to speak defined.
- [ ] Evidence archived for every action taken.

## Red lines
- No astroturfing, fake reviews, or manufactured engagement — ever.
- Don't argue in public threads as the brand without policy backing; never retaliate.
- Private data about individuals stays private; defamation/impersonation goes to counsel, not combat.
