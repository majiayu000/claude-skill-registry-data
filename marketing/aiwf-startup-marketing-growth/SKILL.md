# AIWF Startup Marketing Growth

## Core Rule

Start with the customer problem, evidence, offer, constraint, and measurable business outcome. Do not prescribe a channel because it is popular, confuse activity with demand, or invent market evidence.

## Workflow

1. Establish product stage, buyer, user, geography, price, sales motion, proof, capacity, budget, and decision horizon.
2. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions. Turn the riskiest assumptions into small tests.
3. Define positioning, category, problem, differentiated promise, proof, objections, and one primary call to action.
4. Map the journey from discovery through activation, conversion, retention, referral, and expansion.
5. Select channels by audience access, buying intent, content fit, feedback speed, cost, and operating capacity.
6. Define each experiment with a hypothesis, audience, asset, spend/time cap, success metric, guardrail, and stop rule.
7. Report results using comparable windows and distinguish correlation, platform attribution, and causal evidence.

Read `references/source-register.json` before making current platform, advertising, disclosure, or measurement claims.

## Required Outputs

- concise market and customer assumptions
- positioning and offer statement
- prioritized channel and experiment backlog
- funnel stages with owner and metric
- budget or effort caps
- measurement plan and decision dates
- risks, evidence gaps, and next validation step

## Boundaries

- Add `aiwf-web-seo` for crawl/index/structured-data implementation and `aiwf-aeo-geo` for answer visibility.
- Add the Meta, Google Ads, or YouTube skill for platform-specific work.
- Add `aiwf-startup-finance-funding` for runway, capital, or financing decisions.
- Draft by default. Publishing, outreach, tracking changes, purchases, or campaign spend require explicit approval.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, scarcity, reviews, partnerships, customer counts, performance claims, or market size.
- Follow disclosure, privacy, consent, anti-spam, and platform policies. Escalate legal uncertainty rather than declaring compliance.

## Validation

Check that every recommendation names its evidence, audience, owner, cost, metric, and stop condition. Prefer a smaller testable plan over a large unmeasured campaign.
