# Marketing

Use this skill when the task is marketing-oriented rather than generic business writing.

## Workflow
- Clarify audience, channel, and objective.
- Choose the best output format for that channel.
- Route to brand, carousel, page, or research skills as needed.

## Deliverables
- A marketing-ready brief, asset plan, or draft.
- A concise rationale for channel fit.

## Guardrails
- Do not chase trendiness over brand coherence.
- Do not ignore conversion intent.
