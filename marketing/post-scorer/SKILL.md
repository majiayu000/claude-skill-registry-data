# Post Scorer

Grade drafts against what historically works — before you publish.

## Scoring rubric (100 pts)
1. **Hook strength (25)** — stops the scroll in first line; specific, curious, or bold.
2. **Clarity (15)** — one idea, grade 6-8 reading level, no fluff.
3. **Structure & format (15)** — platform-native length, line breaks, scannability.
4. **Value density (15)** — teaches/moves/provokes per line.
5. **Emotion/resonance (10)** — triggers feeling (controversy, relief, humor, identity).
6. **CTA / engagement driver (10)** — invites reply/save/share naturally.
7. **Voice consistency (10)** — matches the author's voice profile.

## Method
1. Ask for past top 5 and bottom 5 posts (or analyze provided history) to calibrate.
2. Score the draft per criterion with evidence; total = /100.
3. Verdict: >80 publish, 60-80 revise (list exact edits), <60 rework angle.
4. Rewrite once to show the fixed version.

## Output
Scorecard table + verdict + line-level edit list + improved rewrite.
