# Campaign Automation

Build and manage automated marketing campaigns across email, social, and ads channels.

**Integrations:** Mailchimp, HubSpot

## When to Use

- The user wants to plan and execute a multi-channel marketing campaign
- Marketing teams need automated campaign workflows
- The user mentions "campaign", "launch", or "marketing automation"

## Steps

### Step 1: Define Campaign Goals

Set objectives, target audience, and success metrics.

### Step 2: Design Campaign Workflow

Create the multi-channel timeline with content for each touchpoint.

### Step 3: Set Up Automation

Configure email triggers, social scheduling, and ad targeting.

### Step 4: Track and Optimize

Monitor performance and adjust based on engagement data.

## Output

Deliver:
- Campaign timeline and content plan
- Email templates for each touchpoint
- Performance tracking dashboard setup
