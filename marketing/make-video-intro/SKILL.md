## What this skill does

- Turns approved logo, name and sonic direction into compact motion identity.
- Keeps intros brief and tests readability at actual viewing size.

## How to use

Provide logo, exact title, colors, tone, runtime, ratio, sound direction and ending transition.

#### Example

```text
Create a 4-second 16:9 intro for FIELD NOTES; exact title, cream and forest green, paper-fold motion, page-turn sound.
```

## Workflow

1. Lock exact text, logo rules, palette, runtime, audio cue and exit frame.
2. Storyboard a single reveal, hold and transition; avoid a montage.
3. Generate title-safe key art with GPT Image 2 Medium/2K, then animate that precise first frame with Kling 3.0.
4. Inspect spelling, logo fidelity, title hold, motion timing, sound sync and clean exit.

## User-facing output

- Media type: One MP4 video
- Default quantity: 1
- Content per video: One short branded opening sequence
- Default layout: User-requested delivery format
- Model policy: GPT Image 2 Medium/2K for title-safe key art, then Kling 3.0 for precise-frame animation
- Downstream use: Channels, shows and branded series
