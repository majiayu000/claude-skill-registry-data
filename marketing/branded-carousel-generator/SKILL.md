# Branded Carousel Generator

Use this skill when the output should become a carousel for social platforms rather than a generic post.

## Workflow
- Extract one strong narrative and split it into a readable slide arc.
- Apply the active brand system to type, color, and CTA.
- Keep one idea per slide for mobile readability.

## Deliverables
- A slide-by-slide carousel outline.
- Visual treatment notes for each slide.

## Guardrails
- Do not split a paragraph into slides without restructuring.
- Do not ignore the user brand rules.
