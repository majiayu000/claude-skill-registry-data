# Email Sequences

Create and manage automated email outreach sequences for sales prospecting.

**Integrations:** Gmail, HubSpot

## When to Use

- The user wants to build an outreach sequence for prospecting
- Sales teams need personalized email templates at scale
- The user mentions "email sequence", "drip campaign", or "outreach"

## Steps

### Step 1: Define the Audience

Identify the target persona, industry, and pain points for the sequence.

### Step 2: Generate Email Templates

Create personalized email templates for each step in the sequence.

### Step 3: Set Cadence and Triggers

Configure timing between emails and engagement-based triggers.

### Step 4: Track Performance

Monitor open rates, reply rates, and meeting conversions.

## Output

Deliver:
- Complete email sequence with templates for each step
- Recommended cadence and send times
- Performance tracking setup
