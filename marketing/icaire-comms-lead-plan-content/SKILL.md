# ICAIRE Comms Lead: Plan Content

Create a practical content plan that turns ICAIRE priorities, events, and
updates into coordinated communications across channels.

## Contract

- Start from the time horizon, audience, channels, objectives, and available
  source material.
- Use BigBrain or the ICAIRE MCP connector for ICAIRE initiatives, people,
  events, partner context, prior messaging, and open commitments when the user
  has not supplied enough source material.
- Turn scattered updates into content pillars, channel-specific angles, owners,
  deadlines, and approval steps.
- Match each content item to a real audience need or institutional objective.
- For planned visual assets, note that ICAIRE's primary brand color is
  `#4A9549` and should be used as the main accent unless a campaign-specific
  approved design system overrides it.
- Mark facts, assets, approvals, and source material needed before publication.

## Workflow

1. Define the planning frame.
   - Identify planning period, channels, priority audiences, communications
     objective, publishing cadence, and constraints.
   - If the user gives no channel list, consider website, newsletter, social,
     events, and partner channels, then include only the relevant ones.
2. Gather source material.
   - Read provided updates, calendars, reports, event notes, partner notes, and
     draft copy.
   - Query BigBrain or ICAIRE MCP for current initiatives, events, stakeholders,
     and sensitivities when needed.
3. Identify content pillars.
   - Group potential content by theme, audience value, institutional priority,
     and proof points.
   - Avoid turning every internal update into public content.
4. Build the channel plan.
   - Assign channel, format, message angle, audience, owner, draft date,
     publishing date, and approval path.
   - Adapt the same story differently for different channels instead of
     duplicating copy.
5. Flag dependencies.
   - List facts to confirm, people to quote, assets to request, approvals to
     secure, and risks to manage.
6. Review the plan.
   - Check for audience balance, repetition, timing conflicts, sensitivity, and
     realistic production workload.

## Output

Default to this structure unless the user requested another format:

- `Planning Frame`: period, audiences, objectives, and channels.
- `Content Pillars`: priority themes and why they matter.
- `Channel Plan`: channel, format, angle, audience, owner, date, and approval.
- `Calendar`: recommended sequence or publishing schedule.
- `Reuse Opportunities`: how one story can become multiple assets.
- `Dependencies`: facts, assets, quotes, or approvals needed.
- `Risks And Sensitivities`: items needing careful handling.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent events, owners, approvals, or publication dates.
- Do not plan public content from internal-only information without marking the
  approval need.
- Do not create a generic social calendar disconnected from ICAIRE priorities.
- Do not assume a channel is available or active unless the user or source
  material supports it.
- Do not ignore production capacity; keep the plan executable.
