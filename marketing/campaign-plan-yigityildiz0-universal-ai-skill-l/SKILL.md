# Campaign planning

Adapted from Anthropic's Marketing campaign workflow; see [SOURCE.md](SOURCE.md).

Use this for a coordinated campaign or launch, not for one ordinary message or social post. Infer the product, audience, goal and timing from the request. Ask only for a missing choice that changes the plan materially; otherwise show a provisional assumption. Missing ad budget does not block an organic plan.

Build the smallest useful plan around objective, audience, message, channel and measurement. Use supplied brand voice and evidence. Tie supporting claims to real proof; never invent testimonials, results, credentials or audience research.

Choose channels based on reachability, intent, available time and actual budget. Create a compact calendar showing asset, channel, dependency, owner if known, and date. Landing-page readiness and tracking checks precede a proposed paid launch.

Define an outcome KPI, its calculation, data source and review interval. Label targets as targets, assumptions as assumptions and sourced historical data as historical. Do not present generic budget percentages, conversion benchmarks or production times as measured facts.

Deliver a brief and an actionable calendar, with budget scenarios only when relevant. Writing a plan does not authorize ad spending, publishing, emailing or accessing new accounts. Use existing connected tools only when their capabilities and authorization fit the task; do not assume Claude MCP placeholders exist in ChatGPT.
