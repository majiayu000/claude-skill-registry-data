# KDP Launch & Marketing

[OBJ] A launch is a sequenced velocity event, not an upload. Amazon's algorithm rewards sales velocity in the new-release window — every sale (organic or ad-driven) feeds organic rank. A sale is a sale.

## The organic flywheel

Ad-driven sales improve organic rank exactly like organic sales. The endgame: reach self-sustaining organic sales where ads become optional. But ads only amplify — **they cannot fix a bad book**. Cover, description, reviews, price, niche all come first. Fixing the product precedes spending on traffic.

## 30-day launch sequence (4 phases)

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|-------|------|-------|
| 1. Pre-launch | −14 to 0 | advance reader team assembled, review copies out, promo-site bookings made, listing finalized |
| 2. Launch week | 0–7 | price pulse ($0.99 or free-if-Select), maximum visibility push, mobilize every channel the same week — velocity concentration beats spread |
| 3. Consolidation | 8–21 | reviews land, start Sponsored Products, raise price into the 70% band, monitor keyword indexing |
| 4. Sustain | 22–30+ | tune ads, apply for remaining promo slots, decide series/fast-follow |

## Reviews — the ethical lines

- Legitimate: advance reader teams, editorial reviews, asking buyers honestly (no incentive manipulation).
- Prohibited: paid reviews, review swaps in bulk, family accounts, incentivized positive reviews — Amazon terminates accounts for these.
- Reviews are the conversion engine: under 10 reviews most ad spend underperforms. Prioritize honest review velocity in phase 1–2.

## Amazon advertising system

### Ad-type triage

| Type | Verdict |
|------|---------|
| Sponsored Products | the only type for most publishers — search results + product pages |
| Sponsored Brands | advanced only — needs 3+ titles/backlog |
| Lock-screen/interest ads | avoid — targeting too broad, ACOS routinely catastrophic |

Prefer advertising the paperback when both formats exist — higher margin absorbs ad cost; ebook sales ride along.

### The 4-campaign structure

1. **Auto campaign** — discovery engine; let Amazon find converting search terms.
2. **Manual keyword campaign** — your research keywords at controlled bids.
3. **Manual product/ASIN campaign** — target competitor book pages.
4. **Harvest cycle** — every 1–2 weeks: pull the search-term report, move converting terms from auto into manual with higher bids, negative-keyword the money-wasters.

### ACOS discipline

- ACOS = ad spend ÷ ad revenue. Break-even ACOS = your royalty margin percentage.
- Judge on 2+ weeks of data — Amazon attribution lags up to 72h and KENP/read-through revenue never shows in ACOS at all (series economics tolerate higher ACOS on book 1).
- Troubleshooting matrix: high impressions + no clicks = cover/title problem; clicks + no sales = description/price/reviews problem; no impressions = bids too low or targeting too narrow.

## Rules

1. [REQ] Velocity concentration: compress launch effort into one week rather than spreading over a month.
2. [REQ] Fix the product before spending on ads; ads amplify, they don't repair.
3. [REQ] Start with one auto + one manual Sponsored Products campaign; harvest search terms every 1–2 weeks.
4. [PROHIBIT] Paid/incentivized reviews or review manipulation — account-termination risk.
5. [REQ] Set ACOS targets from actual royalty math, including read-through for series.
