# SMS Marketing

## Mandatory Content Standards

- Treat consent, identity, timing, and opt-out handling as required parts of every campaign.
- Do not invent legal conclusions or claim compliance without jurisdiction and evidence.
- Use active voice, short paragraphs, direct language, and clear tables.
- Use no em dashes, hashtags, emojis, or filler closings.
- End every campaign plan with a testable next step.

## Consent Gate

Before writing a campaign, record the business identity, recipient purpose, collection source, consent language, consent timestamp, jurisdiction, sending number, frequency, quiet hours, STOP handling, HELP handling, and suppression process.

Never treat a phone number as consent. Never buy or reuse a list without a documented lawful basis and provider permission.

## Campaign Types

| Campaign | Use | Required Control |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | Confirm opt-in and set expectations. | Immediate identity and opt-out path. |
| Reminder | Reduce no-shows for events or appointments. | Date, time, timezone, and reschedule link. |
| Abandoned cart | Recover a known shopping action. | Event timestamp and frequency cap. |
| Promotion | Present a time-bound offer. | Clear sender, terms, and opt-out. |
| Win-back | Re-engage an inactive customer. | Suppress recent purchasers and opt-outs. |
| Support | Send transactional status updates. | Keep promotional content separate. |

## Message Structure

Use sender identity, relevant context, one benefit, one action, timing or terms, and opt-out language where required. Keep links branded and traceable. Avoid URL shorteners when they reduce trust or deliverability.

## A2P And Deliverability

Identify whether the campaign uses a toll-free number, short code, or local number. Confirm registration requirements, throughput, carrier filtering, message classification, and brand identity before launch. Treat A2P 10DLC as a setup and compliance task, not a copy detail.
