# Vintage
## Mission
Evoke the tactile warmth of aged print — as if every page was pulled from a 1920s letterpress shop, worn at the edges and rich with history.
## Brand
### 🎨 Colors
- `#5C3A21` — Primary (warm brown)
- `#8B6914` — Secondary (aged gold)
- `#F5F0E8` — Background (cream paper)
- `#E8DCC8` — Surface (aged parchment)
- `#2C1810` — Text (deep ink brown)
- `#8B4513` — Accent (saddle brown)
### 🔤 Typography
- Heading font: Playfair Display (Google Fonts, elegant serif)
- Body font: Lora (Google Fonts, readable serif)
- Type scale: xs 0.75rem / sm 0.875rem / base 1.0625rem / lg 1.25rem / xl 1.563rem / 2xl 2.441rem / 3xl 3.052rem
### 📐 Spacing
- Grid: 6-column (narrower, like vintage publications)
- Section padding: 4rem vertical, 3rem horizontal
- Card gaps: 2.5rem (generous breathing room)
### 🧩 Components
- Buttons: Letterpress style — slight inner shadow, warm brown fill, cream text. Hover deepens the brown. Subtle 2px border-radius.
- Cards: Cream surface with a darker deckled-edge border effect (CSS box-shadow for depth), optional ornamental corner flourishes.
- Nav: Centered, spaced serif links with a thin gold ornamental divider below. Active state: italic + underline.
- Hero: Large serif headline over a cream background, decorative rule lines above and below, optional vintage illustration or photograph.
- Footer: Three-column with small serif headings, gold divider rule, copyright in italic.
### ♿ Accessibility
- Warm brown on cream passes 4.5:1 — verify with contrast checker
- Minimum body text size: 1rem (not 0.875rem)
- Focus rings: 2px solid saddle brown, clearly visible on cream
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: remove any subtle hover transitions
### ✍️ Writing Tone
Warm, refined, unhurried. Full sentences over bullet points. Embrace slightly formal constructions. No slang, no tech jargon unless the subject demands it.
## Do / Don't
### ✅ Do
1. Use paper textures (subtle noise, grain, or fiber overlays) on backgrounds
2. Add ornamental details: rule lines, fleurons, drop caps, section dividers
3. Embrace generous margins — vintage layouts breathe
4. Use sepia-toned photography with vignette effects
5. Layer depth with subtle box-shadows that mimic letterpress impression
### ❌ Don't
1. Use pure black or pure white — always warm-toned
2. Add neon colors, gradients, or modern glow effects
3. Use sans-serif fonts for body text
4. Fill every inch of space — vintage design values restraint
5. Mix in flat-design or material-design conventions
## Quality Gates
- [ ] No pure black (#000) or pure white (#FFF) anywhere
- [ ] At least one ornamental element (rule, fleuron, drop cap) present
- [ ] Serif fonts used for all body text
- [ ] Paper texture or grain effect visible on backgrounds
- [ ] Color palette stays within warm brown/gold/cream spectrum
- [ ] Photography (if used) is sepia-toned or warm-filtered
