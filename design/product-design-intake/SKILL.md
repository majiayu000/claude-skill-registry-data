# Product Design Intake

## Purpose

Prevent invented users, policy, content, visual identity, and success metrics from driving implementation.

## When to Use

- A new page, flow, or component family lacks a trusted current brief.
- A redesign could change behavior, hierarchy, conversion, or user familiarity.

## Do

- Identify primary task, completion signal, consequence of failure, entry point, recovery path, and real content extremes.
- Capture device, locale, accessibility, security, performance, brand, and existing-pattern constraints.
- State assumptions and write testable acceptance criteria before choosing layout.

## Do Not

- Do not create fictional personas, choose patterns from a gallery, or use final hierarchy based on placeholder content.
- Do not treat a screenshot as a substitute for task and state decisions.

## Review Checklist

- [ ] Primary task and completion signal are one clear statement.
- [ ] Failure, recovery, content, accessibility, and device constraints are known.
- [ ] Brand direction and anti-direction are documented.

## Expected Output

1. One-page product brief.
2. Task-flow and state inventory.
3. Assumption register and acceptance criteria.
