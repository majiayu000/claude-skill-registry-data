# Frontend Design Quality Orchestrator

## Purpose

Turn broad interface requests into one bounded design pass with measurable closure.

## When to Use

- A page, feature, component family, or design system needs coordinated review.
- The dominant design risk is unclear or multiple specialist skills could apply.

## Do

- Run product intake, classify the dominant risk, and choose one primary plus at most three secondary skills.
- Record intent, constraints, rejected alternatives, evidence, acceptance criteria, and owner.
- Validate accessibility, responsive behavior, component states, and release evidence before closure.

## Do Not

- Do not load every design skill for one task or substitute decoration for task success.
- Do not approve attractive work with undefined loading, error, empty, keyboard, or responsive behavior.

## Review Checklist

- [ ] Primary user task, constraints, and dominant risk are explicit.
- [ ] Selected skills have bounded scope and evidence.
- [ ] Release blockers have an owner or are resolved.

## Expected Output

1. Diagnosis and selected skills.
2. Design decision record and smallest safe implementation plan.
3. Final scorecard, blockers, and release decision.
