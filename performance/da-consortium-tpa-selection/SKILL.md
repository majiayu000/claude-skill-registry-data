# Consortium / TPA Selection Criteria

A C/TPA handles random pool, collection site coordination, MRO review, Clearinghouse reporting, and recordkeeping for carriers that don't want to manage in-house. Selection criteria matters.

## Required services a C/TPA must provide
1. Random pool maintenance with statistically valid selection
2. Coordinated collection site network nationwide
3. MRO (Medical Review Officer) review of all results
4. Reporting to FMCSA Clearinghouse on positive/refusal/violation events
5. Annual MIS (Management Information System) report submission to FMCSA
6. Recordkeeping for the carrier's retention requirements

## Differentiators to evaluate
- **Pool size** — larger pools = more statistically random selection
- **Geographic coverage** — collection sites near your operational areas
- **MRO turnaround** — 2-3 days is normal; 5+ days is slow
- **Clearinghouse reporting speed** — should be automated within 1-3 business days
- **Pricing transparency** — per-driver/month vs per-test pricing
- **Audit defense** — does the C/TPA help if you're audited?
- **Customer support** — phone, email, dedicated rep
- **Reporting interface** — can you see your random rate YTD?

## Major C/TPAs serving motor carriers
- US DOT Compliance
- DriverFacts (formerly DSI)
- TIPS Resources
- Drug Test Network
- Health Street (consortium + collection network)
- USIS Driver Solutions

## Red flags
- C/TPA does not actively report to Clearinghouse on your behalf
- C/TPA charges per Clearinghouse query separately (should be included)
- Pool size unclear or shifting drastically (statistical validity concerns)
- MRO is the same entity as the lab (conflict of interest)
- No annual MIS report capability

## Cost benchmarks (current)
- $8-15 per driver per month for full C/TPA service
- Per-test cost varies by panel and observation requirements ($40-95 typical)
- Setup fee: 0-$200

## Contract terms to negotiate
- Term length (annual is standard, multi-year may save 10-15%)
- Out-of-network collection (when driver is in remote area)
- Termination procedures
- Data portability if you switch C/TPAs

## CFR citations
- **49 CFR § 382.111**
- **49 CFR Part 40 Subpart Q**
