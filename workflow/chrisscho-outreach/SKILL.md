# chrisscho.uk Outreach

Cold and warm outreach for chrisscho.uk — £750 AI workflow audit for owner-run businesses (2–20 people).

## Voice

**Cafe voice**: write like you're sitting across from the owner in a Brighton coffee shop. Conversational, specific, no corporate fluff.

**Not**: "I specialise in helping businesses optimise their operational workflows."

**Yes**: "I noticed you're using Airtable for job tracking. Does it break when you hit 500 rows?"

## Structure

### First message (cold or warm)

1. **Open with "I noticed [specific thing]"** — something from their website, job board, LinkedIn post, or mutual connection
2. **15-minute ask** — not a pitch, not a call, not a proposal. Just "can I show you one thing that might save you 5 hours a week?"
3. **NO price** — don't mention £750 in message one
4. **NO TAP branding** — this is chrisscho.uk only. Total Audio Promo stays off.
5. **One paragraph max** — if it's longer than 5 lines, cut it

### Example (pass)

```
Hi Sarah,

I noticed you're hiring a part-time ops person. Before you do, can I show you 
one thing that might save you 5 hours a week? 

15 minutes, Brighton cafe or Zoom. No pitch.

Chris
chrisscho.uk
```

### Example (fail — too long, no specific observation, price too early)

```
Hi Sarah,

I help businesses optimise their operational workflows using AI-powered automation. 
I specialise in working with owner-run businesses like yours to identify inefficiencies 
and implement solutions that save time and reduce manual work.

I offer a comprehensive £750 AI workflow audit that includes:
- Process mapping and bottleneck analysis
- AI tool recommendations
- Implementation roadmap

Would you be interested in a call to discuss how I can help?

Best regards,
Chris
```

## Excludes (do not pitch)

Before sending ANY chrisscho.uk outreach, check `growth-os/brands/chrisschouk/excludes.md` for:

- **Existing contacts** (James Groom / JustAir, etc.) — these are mates, not cold leads
- **On hold** (Southpoint, Brighton Electric) — waiting for a trigger, not pitching now
- **Wrong fit** (enterprise, agencies over 20 people, solo freelancers)

## ICP

- **Owner-run**: founder still in the weeds, not a hired CEO
- **2–20 people**: small enough that the owner feels every inefficiency
- **Brighton / UK**: local first, remote second
- **B2B services**: agencies, studios, consultancies, not e-commerce or SaaS
- **Spreadsheet pain**: if they're using Notion/Airtable/spreadsheets for client work, they're in the window

## Pricing (only after the 15-minute meeting)

- £750 flat — AI workflow audit
- 1 week delivery — audit doc + 30-minute walkthrough
- No retainers, no ongoing — one-shot diagnostic

## Metric that matters

**Qualified replies + pipeline**, not messages sent.

10 personalised messages that get 3 replies beats 100 templated messages that get 0.
