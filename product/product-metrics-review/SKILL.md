# Product Metrics Review

Interpret change only after validating the metric.

1. State the product decision, metric contract, source, time period, owner, and freshness.
2. Check denominator, cohort, time zone, missing data, tracking changes, releases, and definition changes before explaining movement.
3. Segment the change by the few dimensions that can reveal meaningful differences; avoid fishing.
4. Separate observation, hypothesis, evidence, and next test.
5. Recommend an owner, decision date, and stop condition for follow-up.

Do not confuse correlation with causal proof or publish a metric interpretation without the caveats that materially change it.
