# Competitive Brief

Compare alternatives against a real buyer or product decision, not a generic feature grid.

1. Define audience, decision, category, alternatives, evaluation criteria, and time horizon.
2. Research current primary sources first: product docs, pricing, release notes, public statements, and directly observable product behavior.
3. Separate verified facts, customer claims, analyst inference, and unknowns.
4. Compare positioning, target user, workflow fit, differentiation, constraints, pricing assumptions, and switching costs.
5. End with implications, counter-positioning, and questions to validate—not a claim that one product is universally best.

Do not scrape gated data, reproduce competitor marketing as fact, or use stale price/features without a dated source.
