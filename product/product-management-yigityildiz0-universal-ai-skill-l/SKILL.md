# Product Management

Use this skill when the task is product strategy or product planning rather than only writing or coding.

## Workflow
- Clarify user, problem, metric, and decision.
- Structure work into problem, options, recommendation, rollout.
- Route to research, deck, or decision skills as needed.

## Deliverables
- A PM-ready brief, roadmap slice, or PRD section.
- A concise recommendation.

## Guardrails
- Do not confuse feature lists with strategy.
- Do not ignore trade-offs or metrics.
