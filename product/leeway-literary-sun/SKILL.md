# LeeWay Literary Sun

## Boundary
This is a Creator-proposed evaluation model, not automatically the canonical LeeWay Formula encoding. Literary quality contains measurable and judgment-based dimensions; do not invent numeric precision for subjective judgments.

## Six core craft rays
1. NARRATIVE_ARCHITECTURE
2. CHARACTER_DYNAMICS
3. SCENE_TENSION_ECONOMY
4. THEME_RESONANCE
5. INFORMATION_REVELATION
6. VOICE_MEDIUM_EXECUTION

Each ray may define an evidence-bound deviation vector x_i, SPD W_i and threshold theta_i. r_i=sqrt(x_i^T W_i x_i); b_i=1 iff r_i<=theta_i.

## Structural profiles
Beat positions such as [0.12,0.25,0.50,0.75,0.90] are optional FEATURE_THREE_ACT profile targets, not universal constants. Episodic, novel, commercial, literary, nonlinear and other forms use their own declared targets or qualitative gates.

## Scene correction
A professional scene should create meaningful state/information/relationship/decision movement appropriate to its function. Full charge inversion |Delta V|=1 is not mandatory. Measure slack/redundancy separately from scene magnitude.

## Information correction
Maintain Author/Canon, Character and Audience knowledge states distinctly. Mystery, suspense and dramatic irony depend on relative knowledge; entropy equations require defined variables/probabilities before numeric claims.

## Publishing gates
Literary craft PASS does not erase hard failures in continuity, factual integrity, rights/permissions, author approval, EPUB conformance, accessibility claims, metadata integrity or required publication validation.

## Hierarchy
A six-ray Literary Sun may summarize craft; sub-Suns can evaluate novel, screenplay, episodic, copy, editorial and publishing production. Do not silently reorder/version rays.
