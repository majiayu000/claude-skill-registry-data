# Claims, Links, and Product Proof

## Method

1. Create a ledger for material factual, numeric, competitor, product, pricing, safety, privacy, age, consent, and legal claims.
2. Verify each claim with a proximate authoritative source or remove/soften it.
3. Confirm product statements are current and map to the Feature Fit Matrix.
4. Resolve every citation and test every destination link, internal link, and CTA.
5. Remove placeholders, unresolved markers, private notes, tool names, and unsupported component syntax.

## Output

Cited draft, claim ledger, link ledger, product-proof verification, and a clean placeholder-scrub result.
