# Stakeholder Update

Communicate decisions, evidence, risks, and asks without hiding uncertainty.

1. Identify audience, decision rights, cadence, and the few metrics or outcomes that matter.
2. Lead with outcome and change since the last update.
3. Report progress, evidence, risks/blockers, decisions needed, owners, and next checkpoints.
4. Use Green/Yellow/Red only with an explicit evidence basis and a concrete recovery or escalation action.
5. Separate confirmed facts, forecasts, and requests.

Do not pad updates with activity lists, invent status confidence, or send externally without approval.
