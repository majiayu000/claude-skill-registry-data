# Draft and Rewrite

## Method

1. Draft from the accepted outline, author packet, and product proof—not from generic memory.
2. Lead with reader reality and the answer; do not lead with features or SEO throat-clearing.
3. Cover all consensus topics, but add the planned unique contribution.
4. Keep product mentions proof-led, sparse, and useful to the decision at hand.
5. Critique version one for intent satisfaction, depth, specificity, authority, originality, voice, and sales pressure.
6. Produce a materially improved version two. A rewrite that changes only wording is not accepted.

## Output

Version-one critique, version-two draft, and a record of substantive changes.
