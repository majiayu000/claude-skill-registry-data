# Abbruchbire (burn-the-house-down)

The figures below are board metrics of public ecdsa.fail rows (official grader, August 2026).

A local optimum rarely looks like inactivity. It can look like real but mostly sub-percent, uneven gains purchased with more machinery: wider searches, tighter certificates, longer reviews, and more elaborate explanations for why the structure itself cannot change.

That was the ECDSA.fail frontier before 2026-08-21. The leading campaigns refined one divstep lineage while their own fitted gates increasingly defined what a valid next candidate was allowed to look like.

Then a separate descent produced a different family: fixed-depth ping-pong binary division. The move added 171 qubits and removed 332,069 rounded average executed Toffoli, lowering the product from 1,477,492,400 to 1,258,525,947—14.82%. Its realized trade was 1,941.9 removed Toffoli per added qubit, against a 972.6 exact whole-jump break-even.

The method was not “try random things,” and a later strict improvement on the same family is not another burn:

1. Protect the verified leader.
2. Open a campaign charter with a discontinuity-sized target; by default, 5% for structural admission and 10% for the campaign.
3. Run at least two independently contextualized blank-slate explorers and maintain three architecture fingerprints. A new worktree alone proves nothing.
4. Turn every wall into an assumption and an executable overturn condition.
5. Give explorers a score direction and a protected invariant-progress horizon, so a worse intermediate score is not an automatic veto.
6. Run the cheapest falsifier first. Let proxies rank the queue, but let only the trusted evaluator certify the exact artifact.
7. Before scoring a local resource cut, census every tied global owner and price every forward, inverse, cleanup, boundary, and repeated-use recomputation traversal.
8. Cap incumbent grinding while structural questions remain open; reconcile each provider/account ceiling independently, and let unused machines stay idle.
9. Repeat the descent from earlier clean ancestors.
10. Compose the top compatible pair in both orders; discovery order need not be the best application order.
11. Grind only after the architecture is frozen, competitive, and passes the campaign checker.
12. Name every deployed candidate as an immutable generation. A replacement gets a new manifest and exact hashes; it supersedes rather than silently editing the prior generation.
13. Split qualification from execution. Qualification may fetch, build, and prove parity, but it cannot begin expensive screening. Execution requires a fresh qualification receipt, provider-local budget authority, and a bound lifecycle guard with the original absolute deadline.
14. Port the winner through a clean shipping tree and re-run the trusted correctness contract.
15. Land one measured increment on the new family, refresh the live frontier after it, and capture final capital plus branch/worktree inventory for both shipping sources.

The phrase is deliberately dramatic; the operation is not. Do not delete the incumbent, discard evidence, or weaken correctness. Remove the old artifact's authority over the explorer while preserving the artifact as the leader until it is genuinely beaten.

> When gains shrink while machinery grows, protect the leader, challenge the assumptions behind the wall, and re-run the descent on cleared ground.

If the run produces a 0.07% same-family win, celebrate and ship it as defense. Do not use it to certify the burn. Structural classification requires a different resource equation that clears the declared discontinuity threshold; a COMPLETE archive additionally requires the post-jump increment, later frontier refresh, and final snapshots.

## What this copy is

This is the doctrine: the fifteen rules above. The full campaign package we run it
with (charter templates, architecture passports, conformance checkers and the companion skills for clean-room
re-descent, portfolio management and the structural audit) lives in a separate repository and is not published
here. Where a rule says "campaign checker" or "qualification receipt", write the check yourself: a script that
refuses to start expensive work until the named evidence exists.

The move is Teddy Pender's, shown in public by his 21 August 2026 ecdsa.fail row. The rules are our own reading
of what made such a row possible and of where our own campaigns failed to do the same; they are not his words.
