# Experience first

The work is judged by the people who consume it.

- The user is whoever consumes the work: an end user, a colleague importing
  the module, the next maintainer. Explain impact from their side.
- When implementation convenience and the consumer's experience conflict,
  choose the experience.
- Each feature, control and option earns its place against the core
  workflow; cut what does not serve it.
- Ship fewer finished features rather than more rough ones.
- Interaction details (transitions, alignment, spacing, feedback, error
  states) are part of the work, finished with it, not left for a later
  polish pass.
