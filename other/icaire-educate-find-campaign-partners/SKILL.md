# ICAIRE Educate: Find Campaign Partners

Identify partner organisations that can credibly distribute an ICAIRE Educate
programme, cohort, workshop, or registration campaign.

## Research Order

1. Read ICAIRE context and existing campaign notes first.
2. Check whether the partner is already known before searching externally.
3. Use web research for current public facts when needed.
4. Capture uncertainty explicitly instead of filling gaps.

## Partner Brief

For each partner, provide:

- organisation name
- relevance to the programme
- audience fit
- proposed forwarding ask
- suggested email angle
- known contact route when verified
- approval or sensitivity notes

## Selection Guidance

Prioritise partners with:

- direct audience fit
- credible institutional relationship with ICAIRE or the programme topic
- practical ability to forward or amplify registration
- clear non-commercial rationale

Avoid speculative partner claims, unverified contact names, or assumptions that
a partner will send.

## Output

Return:

- `Recommended Partners`
- `Partner Briefs`
- `Third-Party Email Angles`
- `Open Questions`
- `Sources Checked`

## Guardrails

- Do not create or send partner emails from this skill.
- Do not fabricate contacts, approvals, relationships, or audience sizes.
- Do not route sensitive partner context into public-facing copy without review.
