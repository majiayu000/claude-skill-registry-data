# Archives And Collections

Choose one primary mode and read only its workflow. Add another mode only when the request needs its distinct deliverable. Do not load sibling workflows or artifacts prospectively.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `accession-intake` | Prepares collection intake inventories, provenance and transfer evidence, condition notes, and accession questions without authorizing acquisition or changing ownership. | [accession-intake](references/workflows/accession-intake.md) |
| `collection-description` | Creates hierarchical collection descriptions, finding-aid drafts, and terminology decisions from supplied inventories while preserving provenance and original-order evidence. | [collection-description](references/workflows/collection-description.md) |
| `digital-preservation` | Plans fixity, format-risk review, storage independence, recovery verification, and reversible migration for digital collections without altering original assets. | [digital-preservation](references/workflows/digital-preservation.md) |
| `collection-access` | Reviews collection access requests against supplied donor terms, restrictions, privacy concerns, and institutional policy without authorizing release or resolving legal conflicts. | [collection-access](references/workflows/collection-access.md) |
| `exhibit-interpretation` | Writes source-backed exhibit narratives, object labels, and accessible interpretive alternatives from collection evidence without inventing provenance or granting publication clearance. | [exhibit-interpretation](references/workflows/exhibit-interpretation.md) |

Preserve source evidence and distinguish drafts, proposed checks, and verified results. Missing evidence permits bounded planning with explicit unknowns. Creating a plan does not authorize external actions, institutional decisions, or changes to source records.
