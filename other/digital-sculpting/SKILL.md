# LeeWay 3D Digital Sculpting

Build silhouette and primary masses before secondary anatomy/forms and tertiary surface detail. Choose multiresolution, voxel/remesh or dynamic-topology methods by downstream need. Preserve scale and reference. High-detail sculpt is normally SOURCE/HIGH geometry; game/runtime use requires retopology/baking unless the target runtime explicitly supports the density. Validate silhouette, landmarks, surface continuity and retopology/bake handoff.
