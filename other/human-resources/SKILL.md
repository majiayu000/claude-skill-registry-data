# Human Resources

Use this skill when the task is HR-adjacent rather than generic writing.

## Workflow
- Clarify audience, sensitivity, and decision context.
- Use structured rubrics and process steps.
- Keep the tone practical and respectful.

## Deliverables
- An HR-ready document or process brief.
- A clear rubric or action list.

## Guardrails
- Do not present compliance assumptions as universal legal truth.
- Do not erase nuance from sensitive people issues.
