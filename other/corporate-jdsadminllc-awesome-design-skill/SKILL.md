# Corporate
## Mission
Project trust, stability, and professionalism through a conservative blue-navy palette and formal, structured layouts.
## Brand
### 🎨 Colors
- `#0A1628` — Primary dark (navy background)
- `#1E3A5F` — Secondary dark (card backgrounds)
- `#0066CC` — Accent (corporate blue)
- `#FFFFFF` — Primary text on dark / background on light
- `#94A3B8` — Secondary text (slate)
- `#F1F5F9` — Light section background (alternating)
### 🔤 Typography
- Heading font: Libre Franklin (Google Fonts)
- Body font: Source Sans 3 (Google Fonts)
- Type scale: xs 0.75rem / sm 0.875rem / base 1rem / lg 1.125rem / xl 1.375rem / 2xl 1.75rem / 3xl 2.25rem
### 📐 Spacing
- Grid: 12-column, 2rem gap, max-width 1140px
- Section padding: 5rem top/bottom (alternating white/navy backgrounds)
- Card gap: 2rem
### 🧩 Components
- Buttons: 4px border-radius (nearly square), solid navy fill, white text, subtle darken on hover
- Cards: White with 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 8px radius, subtle shadow, icon-top layout
- Nav: Full-width navy bar, centered or left-aligned logo, dropdown menus for sub-sections
- Hero: Full-width navy background, centered heading, subhead, two CTAs (primary + outline), stock-photo or abstract background
- Footer: Dark navy with 4-column link grid, copyright line, social icons, "trust bar" with certifications
### ♿ Accessibility
- Minimum 4.5:1 contrast for all text (navy on white = 13:1, white on navy = 13:1)
- Focus rings: 2px solid `#0066CC` outline
- Reduced motion: remove hover lifts, keep opacity transitions
- Minimum font size 0.875rem for body
### ✍️ Writing Tone
- Formal and professional — use complete sentences, avoid contractions
- Third-person or institutional "we"; avoid first-person singular
- Labels: title case for navigation, sentence case for body CTAs
## Do / Don't
### ✅ Do
- Alternate between white and light-gray section backgrounds for rhythm
- Use data-heavy layouts: stat blocks, comparison tables, bulleted feature lists
- Include trust signals: client logos, certifications, case-study links
- Maintain conservative border-radius (4-8px max)
- Structure content with clear headings, subheadings, and supporting body
### ❌ Don't
- Use bright or neon accent colors — stay in the blue-navy range
- Add playful animations — hover lifts are the ceiling
- Use unconventional layouts — centered or two-column only
- Feature informal photography — use corporate stock or product shots
- Mix in trendy design elements (glassmorphism, neumorphism, brutalism)
## Quality Gates
- All text passes 4.5:1 contrast (target 7:1 for body text)
- Focus states visible on every interactive element
- Page respects prefers-reduced-motion
- No font smaller than 0.875rem
- Navigation works with keyboard (tab order logical)
- Trust signals present above the fold (client logos or certification)
