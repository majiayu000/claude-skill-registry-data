# Case Study Design Decisions

Make the decision legible to a skeptical non-designer. Read `references/decide-record.md`.

## Workflow

1. Identify the reader’s influence, priorities, vocabulary, and likely objection.
2. Select only decisions that changed user outcomes, system behavior, risk, cost, or delivery.
3. Complete a DECIDE record for each pivotal decision.
4. Connect design evidence, business consequences, technical constraints, and limitations.
5. Represent rejected alternatives fairly.
6. State what agreement enabled and what happened next.
7. Remove aesthetic rationale that does not affect use, comprehension, trust, or performance.

## Communication pattern

For each decision:

- set context before showing the artifact;
- name the problem and tension;
- explain the choice in the reader’s language;
- ground the rationale in users, business, research, and constraints;
- acknowledge limitations without weakening the argument;
- show the decision or learning that followed.

Do not hide uncertainty or pretend consensus existed. Distinguish the design recommendation from the final organizational decision.

## Output

Return a decision shortlist, completed DECIDE records, likely stakeholder questions, and recommended captions or callouts for the associated visuals.
