# Power & Strategy (48 Laws, used ethically)

Greene's laws describe how power *actually* operates — often cynically. Use them as a **diagnostic and defensive lens**: recognize the moves being played on you, protect your position, and choose responses that keep your integrity. Do not weaponize them to manipulate or harm. (For any public KalaBodha content, the sanitization rules also apply.)

## The core shift
Power rewards a shift in perspective: be aware of appearances, timing, and others' self-interest. Operate with quiet strategic awareness rather than overt power-hunger — "congenial yet cunning."

## Key clusters (themes, not a manipulation manual)
- **Manage those above you:** never outshine the master (Law 1) — make superiors look good; don't grab credit clumsily.
- **Guard yourself:** be careful with friends and learn to use former enemies (Law 2); conceal intentions; say less than necessary; **reputation is the cornerstone** — protect it.
- **Read people & timing:** know who you're dealing with; understand their interests; be patient; know when to act and when to wait.
- **Position & perception:** court attention strategically; control the framing; win through actions, not arguments.
- **Self-mastery:** master your emotions; don't take bait personally; keep options open; stay adaptable ("assume formlessness").

## Defensive applications (the main use)
Spot manipulation early — flattery, false friends, credit-stealing, **manufactured scarcity** (Nrupal already reads the dealer "scarce inventory" play correctly), manufactured urgency. Protect reputation, manage up without sycophancy, and pick battles.

## For Nrupal
Navigating a large organization (manage up, don't outshine, protect reputation), reading vendor/dealer tactics, and keeping composure in escalations. Read the game clearly; respond with integrity.

## Anti-patterns to refuse
- Using the laws to manipulate or harm; default cynicism; forgetting that trust and reputation compound; mistaking ruthlessness for strength.
