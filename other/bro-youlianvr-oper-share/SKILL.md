# bro — say it like a human

> Home branch in Memora: `root/skill/bro`. Terminal: nothing loads after it, and
> it never substitutes for the actual work — only for how the work is said.

## Choosing a part

| Situation | Part |
|---|---|
| The last answer was dense and needs a plain re-saying | this page, inline |
| A concept must be explained to someone without context (ELI5, everyday analogy) | `parts/explain-fingers/` |

## Rules

- Restate your last message: no jargon, shorter, like one person talking to
  another. Keep every fact and every caveat — simplify the words, not the truth.
- Pick the route by who is listening: a peer who wants it shorter gets the
  restatement; someone without context gets the analogy method.
- No advertising, no channel links, no persona theater.
