# Boundary Check

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Identify what belongs to the user and what belongs to another capable person.
2. Check whether the boundary protects time, energy, safety, values, or responsibility.
3. Check whether enforcement depends on making someone suffer or withholding a dependent person's basic needs.
4. Phrase the boundary around what the user will or will not do, not how another person must behave.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
