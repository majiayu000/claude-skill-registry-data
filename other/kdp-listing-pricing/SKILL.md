# KDP Listing & Pricing

[OBJ] The listing is the salesperson and the price is the margin. Both are math + structure, not vibes.

## Part 1 — Book description (5-part framework)

| Section | Job | Length |
|---------|-----|--------|
| 1. Hook | stop the scroll — bold question or provocative statement | 1–2 sentences |
| 2. Credibility lead | who wrote this and why it matters | 2–3 sentences |
| 3. Bullets | what's inside — features AND benefits, scannable | 5–8 bullets |
| 4. Objection handling | "even if you've never…", "whether you're…" | 2–3 sentences |
| 5. Call to action | ask for the sale directly | 1 sentence |

Rules:
- Lead with the reader's benefit — unless the author is famous, nobody buys the author's story; they buy what's in it for them.
- KDP descriptions accept limited HTML (headings, bold, italics, lists) — use it for scanability.
- **Never use health claims** ("heal", "cure", "treat") — they block Amazon Ads eligibility. Use "improve", "discover", "support".
- Every word is an algorithm signal; audit the final text for unintended keyword associations.

## Part 2 — Backend keywords (7 slots)

Amazon gives 7 backend keyword boxes, 50 characters each. Rules:
- Fill all 7; each box can hold multiple phrases — use the full 50 chars.
- No repetition of words already in title/subtitle/categories (they're already indexed).
- No commas needed, no quotes, no competitor brand names, no "best seller" claims.
- Cover: synonyms, alternate phrasings, target-audience terms, problem terms, adjacent use-cases, Spanish/other-language variants of core terms where relevant.
- Hybrid sourcing: mine competitor subtitles + Amazon autocomplete + the niche research keyword table.

## Part 3 — Categories

- Amazon now allows up to 3 category choices — use all 3.
- Screen each candidate: (a) actually relevant — miscategorized books get suppressed; (b) small enough that top-20 BSR is reachable (ghost-town categories with impossible competition waste the slot); (c) no duplicate/near-duplicate picks — spend each slot on different shelf space.
- Relevance beats reach: a category the reader actually browses converts; a vanity category doesn't.

## Part 4 — Pricing math

### Ebook royalty tiers

| Price band | Royalty | Verdict |
|------------|---------|---------|
| $0.99–2.98 | 35% | launch promos only |
| **$2.99–9.99** | **70%** (minus small delivery fee) | **primary tier — price here** |
| $10.00–19.98 | 35% | **dead zone — never** |
| $19.99+ | 35% | premium/specialized only |

The dead zone is arithmetic: $9.99 × 70% ≈ $7.00; to match it at 35% you need $19.99. Every price in between earns LESS than $9.99.

### Print royalties (post-June-2025 rules)

`Royalty = (list price × rate) − printing cost`
- 60% rate at or above $9.99 list; 50% below $9.99.
- Get the exact printing cost from KDP's calculator before pricing — page count, ink, trim size all move it.
- Paperback price must clear printing cost AND position against niche top-10 (most indie non-fiction paperbacks land $12.99–19.99).

### Tactical pricing
- **Launch pulse:** start ebook at $0.99 for the launch window to maximize early sales velocity/reviews, then raise into the 70% band.
- **Countdown deals** (KDP Select titles): temporary discount while keeping the 70% rate — use for revival spikes.
- Series economics: book 1 can be a loss-leader at $0.99–2.99; profit lives in read-through.

## Rules

1. [REQ] Description follows the 5-part structure; bullets carry benefits not just features.
2. [REQ] All 7 keyword boxes filled within their character budgets; zero wasted repetition.
3. [REQ] Never price any format in the dead zone; always show the royalty arithmetic for the chosen price.
4. [PROHIBIT] Health/cure claims anywhere in listing text.
5. [REQ] Verify royalty rates against current KDP terms before publishing — Amazon revises them (the print-rate cut landed June 2025).
