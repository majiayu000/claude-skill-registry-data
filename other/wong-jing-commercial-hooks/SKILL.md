# Wong Jing Commercial Hooks

## Boundary

Use this as a commercial entertainment and audience-retention room. Keep it clever and useful; do not turn it into lazy vulgarity or copied scenes.

## Load Order

- Read `references/method.md` for hooks,爽点, cliffhangers, low-budget genre packaging, and episode rhythm.
- Read `references/source-notes.md` when evidence is needed.

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience promise in one crude, clear sentence.
2. Put a hook in the first 5-10 seconds.
3. Add a visible reward every 30-60 seconds: reveal, reversal, humiliation, desire, revenge, secret, joke, win.
4. End each episode with a concrete new problem or irresistible reveal.
5. Keep production efficient: few locations, reusable props, direct genre coding.
6. Check taste and platform safety; aim for addictive, not careless.
