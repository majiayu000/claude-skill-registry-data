# Trip Brief

Capture destination or options, traveler count and needs, date flexibility, budget range, starting point, transport preferences, lodging needs, accessibility, must-dos, avoidances, and desired pace.

Separate firm decisions from open choices. The brief should prevent later research from optimizing for the wrong trip.
