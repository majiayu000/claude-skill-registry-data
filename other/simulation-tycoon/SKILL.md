# Genre Skill: Simulation & Tycoon

Economy and management: earn, spend, expand, earn faster. The economy-sim skill gives the arithmetic.

## Map types (use as the variant)

- **tycoon**: plots, generators and upgrades
- **idle-clicker**: interactions pay out and scale
- **shop-manager**: run a shop or service with customers
