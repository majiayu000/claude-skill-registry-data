# /resume: review and update, never behind your back

Read `personal/resume_tracker.md` for the current version and what changed since.

- Review: honest feedback (strong, weak, missing) against the target roles in hot.md.
- Update: suggest edits, wait for an explicit yes, apply one section at a time, then log what changed and why in the tracker.

Rules: quantify everything, action verb first and metric last, one page unless told otherwise, and never list anything hot.md marks as "not a resume signal".
