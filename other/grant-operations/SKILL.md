# Grant Operations

Choose one primary mode and read only its workflow. Add modes only when the same award or proposal needs their distinct checks.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `grant-proposal-compliance` | Parse solicitations, eligibility signals, requirements, attachments, review criteria, or blockers. | [Proposal compliance](references/workflows/grant-proposal-compliance.md) |
| `grant-budget-justification` | Connect costs, estimates, allocations, restrictions, narrative, and owner questions. | [Budget justification](references/workflows/grant-budget-justification.md) |
| `sponsored-projects-reporting` | Track award obligations, reporting calendars, evidence, approvals, deliverables, or closeout. | [Sponsored-project reporting](references/workflows/sponsored-projects-reporting.md) |

Do not load sibling workflows or artifacts prospectively. Stop before certifying eligibility or allowability, inventing institutional approvals, or submitting official materials without authority.
