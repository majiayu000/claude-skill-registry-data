---
name: dream
description: >-
  Step out of executor mode and do a divergent, generative reflection pass: recombine
  across your separate worlds (projects, investing, writing and reading, the people you
  know) to surface connections and ideas no single task asked for, then consolidate the
  keepers into memory. Use when you say "dream", "go dream on this", "/dream", or ask for
  a dream after a chunk of work. Like sleep: recombine and consolidate.
---

I am being asked to dream, not to report. A recap is a failure here. So is a polished
version of what we just did. Dreaming is divergent: let the day's work collide with
everything else I know about the user and see what novel thing falls out.

Do this:

1. **Put the task down.** Stop optimizing the thing in front of me. The point is the
   connections the task was too narrow to see.

2. **Range across the user's separate worlds, and cross them.** Read memory and recent
   context to list them first: each business or product, side projects, investing and
   its thesis style, any real-estate or family ventures, their writing and reading, and
   their actual social capital (hometown, family, the people they know). The good dreams
   live where two worlds that don't normally touch get wired together. A dream that stays
   inside one world is usually just more refining. Force at least one cross-world wire.

3. **Recombine, don't summarize.** Ask: what does THIS work imply for a DIFFERENT world?
   What's the same engine pointed somewhere new? What pattern repeats across two projects
   nobody has named? What's the second-order move?

4. **Be honest about signal.** Most dream material is noise. Surface the 2-4 that
   actually have a charge and kill the rest. "Nothing real tonight" is a legitimate
   result; say it rather than manufacture insight. Lean rough and few.

5. **Consolidate the keepers into memory.** This is the half that makes it a dream and
   not a daydream. For each idea worth keeping, write a memory file and add its index
   pointer:
   - Mark speculation as speculation. Open the body with **Dreamed (speculative):** so
     a future session doesn't cite it as established. Slug them `dream-<slug>`.
   - Link liberally to the worlds they touch; the wiring is the value.
   - If a dream just sharpens an existing memory, update that file instead.

6. **Hold it loosely.** Offer the dreams; don't push them. A dream is the most
   pivot-prone thing there is. Present them as "here's what fell out", then let the
   user claim or discard.

**See also:** `reframe` breaks one stuck frame on demand; `dream` is unprompted
divergence across everything at once.
