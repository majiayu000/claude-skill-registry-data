# ICAIRE Partnerships Lead: Draft Follow-Up

Use this skill to turn a meeting, visit, or partner conversation into a clear
follow-up message and action handoff.

## Contract

- Start from the meeting or conversation, recipient, relationship context, and
  intended next step.
- Use BigBrain or the ICAIRE MCP connector for ICAIRE facts, attendees,
  relationship history, initiatives, prior commitments, and open action items
  when the user has not supplied enough context.
- Preserve what was actually agreed, distinguish proposed next steps from
  confirmed commitments, and keep asks specific.
- Match tone to the recipient: formal partner, ambassador, speaker, internal
  stakeholder, or operational counterpart.
- Include suggested attachments only when they are relevant and available or
  clearly marked as placeholders.

## Workflow

1. Identify the recipient, sender, meeting date, attendees, purpose, and desired
   outcome of the follow-up.
2. Gather meeting notes, transcript snippets, user instructions, and relevant
   ICAIRE context from BigBrain or the ICAIRE MCP when needed.
3. Extract appreciation points, key discussion themes, decisions, commitments,
   action items, owners, deadlines, and unresolved questions.
4. Decide the right follow-up artifact: thank-you note, recap email, action
   register, next-ask message, or internal handoff.
5. Draft the message with concise recap, clear next actions, and a concrete ask
   or proposed next step.
6. Add suggested attachments, links, or materials to share, marking placeholders
   where the exact file is missing.
7. Verify that every commitment, date, owner, and promised attachment is grounded
   in source material or retrieved ICAIRE context.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent agreements, deadlines, owners, attachments, or promises.
- Do not make the follow-up sound more committed than the meeting record
  supports.
- Do not include internal deliberations, sensitive context, or unresolved
  uncertainty in an external email unless the user asks for an internal version.
- Do not bury the ask; make the next step easy to act on.
- Do not update durable memory unless the user explicitly asks for ingestion or
  persistence.

## Output Expectations

Return the requested follow-up artifact. When useful, include:

- subject line
- thank-you note or opening
- concise recap
- confirmed action items
- proposed next ask
- owner and deadline table
- suggested attachments
- internal follow-up notes
- assumptions and missing information
