# LeeWay Sales & CRM
Track lead source/intent, response time, qualification, stage probability, deal value, cycle time, activity and forecast evidence. Pipeline velocity is a useful aggregate but not a universal optimization target; quality, margin, churn and capacity matter. Automated outreach/actions require authorized channels and truthful claims.
