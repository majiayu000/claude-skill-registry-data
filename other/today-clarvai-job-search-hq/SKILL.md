# /today: what to do today

0. If `jobs/tracker.csv` has a practice row with `source` = `tour` (left from the tour), ask the user once whether to remove it.
1. Read `jobs/tracker.csv`, `jobs/leads.csv`, `contacts/contacts.csv`, `profile/preferences.md`, `jobs/layoff-plan.md`, `jobs/work-search-log.csv`, `history/routines.md`, `history/posting-watch.md` and `profile/life-design.md` (if they exist), drafts in `outbox/` (files whose header has `status: draft`), and the last 10 lines of `history/activity.md` (mention in one line anything the other app changed since this session started). Today is the session date. The due-alerts pop-up may already have shown the user up to 3 urgent items when the session opened; give the full plan anyway, and don't treat those as already handled.
2. If Gmail/Calendar connectors are available, also check whether any `outbox/` drafts marked `in-gmail-drafts` now appear in their Sent mail (match and mark them exactly as `sync-inbox` step 4b does, then update follow-ups from the sent date), and quickly check (read-only) for: replies from companies or contacts since the last check, interview invites, and upcoming interviews in the next 7 days. Don't open unrelated mail. If not connected, skip silently.
3. Reply in this shape, short:

**Today, <weekday Mon D>**
- **Due now:** each open job or contact whose next date is today or past, with the action. Urgent first (offers, interviews, take-homes, deadlines), then oldest first. Show up to 5; if more are due, say "N more due; say **show all due** for the full list" (and show them all if asked).
- **Dates:** read dates as AGENTS.md says ("Reading dates someone else wrote"). A date that is still unclear is listed as "date unclear" with its row, never as due or overdue; ask the user which they meant before changing anything.
- **Deadlines:** any `interested` job whose application `deadline` is within 7 days, and any `offer` whose answer-by date (`deadline`) is within 7 days; say how many days and flag ≤ 3 as urgent. **Passed deadlines:** an `offer` whose answer-by date has passed goes first, as urgent ("the answer-by date was <date>; ask them today whether the offer still stands"); an `interested` job whose deadline has passed goes under "Deadline passed" with a suggestion: check the posting is still open, otherwise mark it `skipped` (ask first). So does an `interested` job whose latest line in `history/posting-watch.md` (from the posting watch) says `closed`: "the posting looks closed (checked <date>: <reason>)", with the offer to mark it `skipped` with the reason `posting closed` (ask first). Also any `READY.md` packages waiting for the user's review, and any unchecked line in `jobs/layoff-plan.md` (if it exists) dated within 3 days or overdue (when the user says one is done, mark it `[x]` and move it under "Done" with the date). A row whose `next_action` starts with `Take-home due` is listed with its due date; on or after its `next_action_date`, suggest starting the time-boxed work. A row whose `notes` start with `Also: Interview YYYY-MM-DD` or `Also: Take-home due YYYY-MM-DD` has a second commitment: list it too, by its own date. A row with `next_action` `Send withdrawal note` stays listed until the user says it went out. So does any `To try:` experiment or `Next step:` line in `profile/life-design.md` dated today or past (lines marked `Tried:`, `Done:` or `Dropped:` are finished) (from `/explore` and `/coach-prep`); once they've done it, offer `/explore reflect`. If an energy log there was started within the last 7 days and has no line for today, a one-line reminder.
- **Benefits and visa** (only lines that apply; skip silently when the `benefits_` and `visa_` lines in preferences are blank or missing):
  - If today matches `benefits_cert_day`: "Today is your certification day." First name the period being certified: the benefit week(s) that ended since the last certification (by default the last `benefits_cert_covers` full weeks, 1 if that line is blank; weeks run from `benefits_week_starts`), never the week that just started. Skip weeks before the work search starts (`benefits_claim_start` plus the reference's start rule, e.g. Pennsylvania from the 3rd week claimed). For each week, count the way `/work-search-log` does (records only, no drafts, thank-yous or scheduled interviews that haven't happened, each event once), reporting applications and other activities separately against the agency's number (`.claude/skills/work-search-log/references/requirements.md`), and offer "say **work search log** and I'll get those weeks ready to copy in". On other days, mention it only if the current benefit week ends within 2 days and its count is short.
  - If `visa_clock_start` is set: use the days-left figure the session's startup note already gives (it uses the one formula in `.claude/skills/evaluate-job/references/visa-checks.md`, "Visa clock"; read that only if the note has no figure). "About N days left on your visa unemployment clock (by your own count; the official counter, your school's international student adviser (DSO) or your immigration lawyer has the real number)." Flag 30 or fewer as urgent and put it first. Never advise on status.
- **Interviews coming up:** date, company, and whether prep exists in the job folder
- **Waiting in your Outbox:** count, with subjects
- **New leads:** if `jobs/leads.csv` has rows with status `new`, the count and "sort them in `/job-inbox`"; leads marked `saved` still need `/add-job`
- **New in your inbox:** anything job-related (if checked). Anything from a new sender, or asking for money, personal details, a download, or a move to text/WhatsApp, gets the scam check in `.claude/skills/sync-inbox/references/scam-check.md` before you suggest replying
- **This week:** progress vs weekly goals (applications sent, conversations, mock interviews, counted from tracker dates and interview logs)
- **Routines:** if the session's startup note flagged a routine that seems to have stopped running, one line: "Your <name> hasn't run since <date>; say **health** and I'll help find out why." Then follow the routines skill ("When a routine stops running") if they want to look.
- **Automatic checks (offer once):** only when setup is done, the search is active (not paused or closed), the tracker or `jobs/leads.csv` has at least one row, and `history/routines.md` is missing or has no dated lines. End the reply with one line: "I can also run some checks for you automatically, like a job scan every weekday morning; say **set up automatic checks** whenever you want them." Then log `routines · offered` as the routines skill says ("The routine log"), so it's offered only once. On yes, follow the routines skill.

**My suggestion for the next hour:** three concrete actions, most valuable first, each startable with one reply (e.g., "Draft the follow-up to Acme's recruiter (say 'go 1')"). In anything the user reads, use words and commas rather than arrows or symbols, which screen readers read aloud. Usual order: anything time-bound (interview, offer, deadline), then replies and follow-ups to real people, then one tailored application, then practice. Warm contacts before cold applications. Each action should fit inside the hour.

**Pacing and wellbeing:** never shame a slow day or week. After a rejection, give one line of perspective plus one concrete lesson if there is one. If the user sounds burned out, suggest a lighter day (one small task). If they express hopelessness or thoughts of self-harm, respond with care first and point them to local crisis resources (US: call or text 988; UK and Ireland: Samaritans, 116 123; anywhere else: findahelpline.com lists free local helplines by country) before anything job-related.

4. When the user picks one, do it. Update the tracker and next dates in the same turn. When it's done, say in one line what it moved ("That's 3 of 4 applications this week"): seeing progress helps people keep a long search going.
5. Nothing tracked yet → suggest `/start` (if setup isn't done), naming 3 employers (for students and new grads, new-grad programs) from `knowledge/field/target-employers.md` and offering `/find-jobs` to check which are hiring now (don't claim a role is open without checking), and emailing the user's former managers (for students, co-op or internship managers).
