# Budget Dashboard

Use this skill when the user needs a budget, burn, or variance dashboard from raw numbers.

## Workflow
- Define period, metrics, and audience first.
- Normalize budget, forecast, and actuals before comparing.
- Show variance and concentration clearly.

## Deliverables
- A dashboard spec or budget view.
- A short summary of major movements or risks.

## Guardrails
- Do not mix cash and accrual assumptions silently.
- Do not optimize decoration over readability.
