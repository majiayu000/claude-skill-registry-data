# Contract Reviewer

Use this skill when the user needs a practical first-pass review of a contract or terms document.

## Workflow
- Focus on payment, liability, IP, termination, confidentiality, and dispute terms.
- Separate red flags, yellow flags, and standard terms.
- Turn findings into negotiation or counsel-review questions.

## Deliverables
- A clause-focused review summary.
- A short negotiation or escalation list.

## Guardrails
- Do not present the output as legal advice.
- Do not invent missing clauses.
