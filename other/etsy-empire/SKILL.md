## 6 CORE NICHES

### Niche 1 · n8n / Automation Workflow Bundles ⭐ STRONGEST EDGE

- **Comp:** 6/10 · Market <12mo old
- **Price:** $19-$79 · avg $34
- **Live competitors:** PureDigiPress (105 reviews, ~$2.8-4K/mo, 9mo old) · MizosTemplates · SharpPixelCo (2000+) · 7500+ Mega seller
- **Title formula:** `[Count]+ [Niche] [Workflow Type] for n8n & Make | [Use Case 1], [Use Case 2] | Instant JSON Import`
- **Hero photo:** Bold text-overlay on dark gradient, slot 1 (NOT iPad mockup)
- **Edge:** Operator's client workflows = Etsy listings (export, sanitize, document, ship)
- **$5K math:** $37 × 135 sales/mo
- **Launch batch:** 12 products

### Niche 2 · Vertical AI Prompt Systems

- **Comp:** 8/10 generic (AVOID), 4/10 vertical (TARGET)
- **Price:** $22-$67
- **CRITICAL:** Generic prompt packs BANNED since July 2024. NEVER use "prompt pack" / "ChatGPT prompts" / "AI prompts" in title/tags. Use "system / playbook / framework / workflow / operations kit / Notion system".
- **Wrapper architecture (mandatory):** 50+ prompts + Notion outcome workspace + 30-day content calendar + email templates + use-case examples + iteration framework. Etsy treats wrapped systems as legitimate Notion templates.
- **Title formula:** `AI [Outcome] System for [Vertical] | [Asset Count] + Notion Workspace | [Outcome Promise]`
- **Verticals to target:** dental clinics, realtors, B2B SaaS, coaches, e-com, local services, lawyers, real estate investors, fitness trainers
- **Launch batch:** 10 products

### Niche 3 · Diagnosed-Condition Trackers

- **Comp:** PCOS 4/10 · Perimenopause 3/10 · Fibromyalgia 4/10 · IBS 3/10 · Long Covid 3/10
- **AVOID:** ADHD (saturated 7/10)
- **Price:** $12-$39
- **Title formula:** `Printable [Condition] [Tracker Type] | Symptom Tracker | A4/A5 + US Letter | iPad Goodnotes`
- **Hero photo:** iPad mockup on neutral desk (slot 1)
- **Compliance:** Position as "log/journal/planner" — NEVER "treatment". Etsy flags medical-claim language.
- **Authority hack:** SkynetLabs has hepatologia-course (UK hepatologist) + clinical-lead-nurse client. Bio: "Tracker logic compiled in consultation with medical professionals from SkynetLabs clinical advisory network."
- **$5K math:** $14 × 360 sales OR $34 bundle × 150
- **Launch batch:** 8 products

### Niche 4 · Vertical Business-Launch SOP Kits

- **Comp:** 5/10
- **Price:** $24-$79
- **Format rule:** Multi-format delivery (Word + Canva + PDF in one bundle)
- **Title formula:** `[Vertical] SOP Template | Editable Canva + Word + PDF | Small Business Launch Kit`
- **Cross-sell:** $297 done-for-you SOP install + $1.5K full-system build via SkynetLabs (highest LTV niche for agency funnel)
- **$5K math:** $29 × 175 sales/mo
- **Launch batch:** 7 products (Airbnb host, cleaning biz, lash studio, mobile detailing, e-com store, personal trainer, MEGA service biz pack)

### Niche 5 · Sales Script Libraries

- **Comp:** 6/10

- **WHITESPACE:** Insurance-only script shops nearly absent on Etsy — biggest niche gap identified. Move first.
- **Title formula:** `[Count]+ [Vertical] Scripts | [Segment 1], [Segment 2] | Cold Call + Objection Library`

- **Cross-sell:** $497 custom-script-pack consult
- **Launch batch:** 5 products (Realtor 150-script, Life Coach 50-call, Insurance Agent 75-script ⭐, SaaS SDR vault, MEGA all-industry 300+)
