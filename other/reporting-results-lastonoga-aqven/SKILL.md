## MUST

- A report opens with the result on real data: the number, the change in percentage points against the
  baseline, and n. Synthetic tests only support it, after it.
- Reports and explanations of a concept open with two or three sentences; details only when asked. An internal
  variant or check id only with a plain explanation next to it.
- Holdout findings (`FINDINGS.md`) stay apart from `dev` signals; a `dev` signal is never called a finding.
- `FINDINGS.md` is generated by the engine: never edit it. `<package>/EXPERIMENTS.md` is yours to write.
- Quote `verdict.text` as it is; never round or retell its numbers.
- Before you report a comparison, open lost and won cases with their inputs (step 4).
- Spend in the journal is measured: `spend.usd` of `series_get` per series, `series_list` `stats` for totals; an
  estimate appears only before a run and is labelled as one.
- Summaries and statistics for the owner are a Markdown file in `<package>/`, next to `EXPERIMENTS.md`, unless
  the owner asks for another format.
- An experiment whose question is answered gets `archived: true` in its `experiment.yaml`, the only edit allowed
  on an experiment that has series; it is never deleted.

## Procedure

| # | Step | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | After every series that ends `done`: an entry in `<package>/EXPERIMENTS.md` with the date, experiment and series id, the question, `verdict.text` quoted, the numbers with n, Scope (what the claim covers and what it does not), Read (the cases you opened and what you saw), Spend (measured) and Changes | the entry exists with every part |
| 2 | Sections of the journal: Now (the owner's goal and the stage), Planned (the owner's hypotheses and data policies not run yet), What holdout settled (pointing at `FINDINGS.md`), What works (`dev` signals), What does not, How we measure (checks, ground truth, controls), Open questions, Spend (per series, and totals from `series_list` `stats`: spend with judge checks, requests, tokens, wall time) | a reader without the conversation knows what is known and what is planned |
| 3 | A refuted belief is marked with the date, the series and its scope, never deleted. The failure of one implementation is "implementation-level" until a strong version also fails | the history of decisions stays visible; no general lesson rests on one weak implementation |
| 4 | Before reporting a comparison, open 5 to 10 cases the new variant lost and won against the current best, side by side with their inputs: `series_outputs` `{series_id, split: "dev", variant: <candidate>}` and the same for the current best, with `fields` for the decision field and the node the claim is about; `run_get_node` for a prompt. Held-out outputs are not read per case (`series_outputs` and `series export` leave them out), so a holdout entry cites the `dev` reading. A breakdown by tag or label shows n in every cell; a cell under 10 cases shows a direction, not a result | the Read line says in one sentence what you saw |
| 5 | Report to the owner: two or three sentences with the real-data result, the delta in points and n; then the next step and the measured spend | the report passes the MUST rules |
| 6 | Autonomy limit: count experiments since the owner's permission; at the limit, report, update the journal and stop | the owner is called in time |
| 7 | After a compaction, read the journal and invoke the skill of the current stage before any action | facts of the summary checked against the journal |
| 8 | An experiment whose question is answered (a holdout verdict, or the owner closed the question): `archived: true` in its `experiment.yaml`, and the journal entry says so | Studio's Research lists only open questions; the folder and its findings stay |

Entry format:

```markdown
### 2026-09-24 · intent_escalation_agents · series 0199f1e2-… · holdout

Question: does Qwen as the escalation agent get the intent right at most 0.1 less often than DeepSeek, with no more invalid first outputs and at most 25% slower at p95?
Verdict: "<verdict.text as the series wrote it>"
Numbers: intent right 0.86 against 0.89 (-3 points, within the 10-point margin), valid first outputs 0.97 against 0.95, p95 12% faster, n = 40 support cases.
Scope: the `escalate` node only, with the recorded triage as its input; another agent or another triage needs its own series.
Read: on dev series 0199e5c4, 7 lost and 4 won cases side by side; the losses are messages whose defect claim the rest of the message takes back.
Spend: $0.42 measured.
Changes: the `escalate` node points at qwen; the experiment is archived.
```

## Pitfalls

| What goes wrong | Do instead |
|---|---|
| Asked for progress in a few sentences with percentages, the first answer opened with a synthetic sweep and mixed ranges from different tests | open with real data: the number, the delta in points, n |
| A two-line question about a concept got a four-section answer | two or three sentences, then offer details |
| The owner had to ask for one written conclusion that a person or a model can read outside the conversation | keep `EXPERIMENTS.md` current after every series |
| The owner had to ask whether anyone had read the model answers | open lost and won cases before every report (step 4) |
| One intermediate record failed (a call summary without speaker turns), and "intermediate steps do not help" went into the journal | a Scope line; one implementation's failure is "implementation-level" until a strong version also fails |
| A per-tag breakdown put 100% on 3 invoices of one layout next to 72% on 60 others, and it was read as a winner | n in every cell; under 10 cases is a direction, confirmed by an experiment on that tag |
| The journal carried pre-run estimates as spend | measured `spend.usd` from `series_get`, totals from `series_list` `stats` |
| A statistics summary was built as an HTML page | a Markdown file next to `EXPERIMENTS.md` |
| The journal was rewritten by a script, and refuted entries and hand-written context were lost | Edit or Write, one change at a time |
| A compaction summary carried a false rule for hours | the journal is the memory; re-read it after a compaction |
| Answered experiments left among the open ones, so the owner could not tell what was still in question | `archived: true` once the question is answered |
| A good pattern: allowed a set number of experiments with a report at the end, the agent stayed within it and wrote a journal that opens with the main lesson and keeps the one finding apart from `dev` signals | do the same |

## Tools and commands

- Read, Edit and Write for `<package>/EXPERIMENTS.md` and owner summaries; never a script or a heredoc. Edit for
  `archived: true` in `experiments/<id>/experiment.yaml`.
- `aqven` MCP `series_get` for the verdict, the matrix and `spend.usd` (`view: "summary"` first); with
  `include_cases: true` for the case rows of a `dev` series. `series_outputs` (`split: "dev"`, `variant`, `case`,
  `fields`) for what each variant answered. `series_list` (`experiment_id`) for the history and `stats`.
  `run_get_node` for the prompt and the answer of one attempt.
