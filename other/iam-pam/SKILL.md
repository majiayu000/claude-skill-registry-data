# LeeWay IAM & PAM
Govern joiner/mover/leaver and workload identities, credentials, federation, MFA/passkeys, roles/attributes, least privilege, JIT/JEA elevation, privileged-session controls and rapid revocation. Identity changes with high blast radius require explicit authorization and evidence.
