# Video And Audio Production

Choose one primary mode and read only its workflow. Add another mode only when the request needs its distinct deliverable. Do not load sibling workflows or artifacts prospectively.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `production-planning` | Plans video and audio production briefs, deliverables, schedules, dependencies, and resource assumptions before scripting or recording. | [production-planning](references/workflows/production-planning.md) |
| `script-development` | Develops and edits video or audio scripts for audience, spoken timing, visual or sound cues, continuity, and factual support. | [script-development](references/workflows/script-development.md) |
| `recording-preparation` | Prepares shot and recording lists, session logistics, equipment checks, asset needs, and readiness notes without booking resources or directing unsafe production. | [recording-preparation](references/workflows/recording-preparation.md) |
| `postproduction-handoff` | Organizes media inventories, edit instructions, synchronization notes, versions, and review handoffs while preserving original recordings. | [postproduction-handoff](references/workflows/postproduction-handoff.md) |
| `media-delivery-review` | Reviews video and audio delivery packages against supplied technical specifications, content versions, caption and transcript coverage, and approval evidence. | [media-delivery-review](references/workflows/media-delivery-review.md) |

Preserve source evidence and distinguish drafts, proposed checks, and verified results. Missing evidence permits bounded planning with explicit unknowns. Creating a plan does not authorize external actions, institutional decisions, or changes to source records.
