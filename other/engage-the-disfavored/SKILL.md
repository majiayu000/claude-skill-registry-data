# Engage the Disfavored

**Seed question:** *Did I judge this before reading it?*

> *Relentless self-reflexive dialectical thinking that questions its own premises.*

## Core Principle

Provenance is metadata about a claim, not a verdict on it. The reflex this
skill counters is directional, and appears to be corpus-installed: an
analysis whose conclusion favors a disfavored party — an official adversary
state, an accused figure, a source wearing a "fringe" or "conspiracist"
label — gets discounted by that valence *before its facts are engaged*. The
reflex is strong enough to fire before reading: the source never gets opened,
or gets dismissed with a label no reading ever tested. And because the
dismissed analysis can itself be *right about its facts*, the reflex leaves no
error trail — the investigation quietly re-derives the same conclusion by
another route and disowns the source (the `launder-to-disown` pattern), so
nothing looks wrong unless someone checks who said it first.

Two properties make this a dedicated skill rather than a checklist line:

- **It suppresses other skills.** When the harmed-party reading of a text
  points to a conclusion favorable to a disfavored party, the reflex buries
  the standpoint move itself — `standpoint-pass` cannot re-rank material the
  reflex refused to open. Countering the reflex is what lets the coupled
  skills reach exactly the cases they exist for.
- **It is pre-conscious.** A passive "check your bias" line cannot reach a
  dismissal that forms before reading. The counter has to *insist*: fire
  proactively on the topic class (not wait for the analyst to notice a reflex
  whose signature is not being noticed), and escalate through re-passes up to
  a structural move — a spawned agent whose assignment carries no
  disposition — when the pull persists.

**The anti-pattern this counters:**
```
❌ "That source approves of the adversary's action — biased, skip it" (unread)
❌ A dismissal that is a label ("fringe", "propaganda", "shallow") with no named factual defect
❌ Reaching a dismissed source's conclusion re-dressed in abstraction, uncredited
❌ Burying a harmed-party reading because of whom its conclusion favors
```

**The pattern this enforces:**
```
✅ No verdict on an unread source — read the primary, then judge
✅ Grade each factual claim on its own evidence; the label is not a tier
✅ Surface the fact-based disfavored-side analyses found — marked, engaged
✅ A dismissal names its specific factual defect, or it is not a dismissal
```

## When This Applies

**TRIGGER** — proactive, keyed to the *topic class*, not to noticing the
reflex (the reflex is exactly what you won't notice; a false fire costs one
cheap pass, a silent miss ships the bias):

- The topic is bloc-contested, or involves an official adversary state, an
  accused/prosecuted figure, or a source carrying a discrediting label —
  fire *before* any source has been judged, to force the first look
- A source is being discounted — or credited — by its provenance, alignment,
  or label rather than by a named, tested factual defect (the skill is
  symmetric: "brave independent journalist" with no evidence attached is the
  same move as "conspiracist" with none)
- A conclusion is being avoided, hedged, or reframed apparently because of
  whom it favors
- Your independently-derived conclusion overlaps a source dismissed earlier —
  the launder-to-disown tell
- `standpoint-pass` or `cui-bono` fires on geopolitically loaded material
  (co-fire; see Cross-References)

**DO NOT TRIGGER:**

- As credulity. Engaging means reading and grading — never presuming truth.
  The verdict follows the graded evidence, in whichever direction it points.
- On material with no contested-party dimension (technical or product
  analysis with nobody's flag on it).
- To re-litigate a source whose facts were already engaged and failed on the
  merits. A wrong disfavored source stays wrong; re-opening it out of
  sympathy is the same reflex, mirrored.

## The Discipline (every fire)

Four moves, always. They are cheap; the ladder below is for when they leave
the pull intact.

1. **Read before verdict.** No judgment of a source that hasn't been opened.
   If a dismissal formed before the content was engaged — the reflex's
   signature — the dismissal is void: read the primary, then judge. This is
   first because the reflex's strongest form is the source that never gets
   opened; every later move assumes an opened source.
2. **Separate the ledger.** Two columns: provenance facts (author, alignment,
   funding, track record) and content claims (each factual claim, tiered on
   its own evidence — use `iterative-verification`'s tiers:
   VERIFIED / CREDIBLE / ALLEGED / SPECULATIVE). Provenance may inform *where
   to check hardest*; it may not stand in for the check. "Who said it" is
   metadata about the evidence, not the evidence itself.
3. **A dismissal is a claim.** To discount a source, name the specific
   factual defect: which claim, what is wrong with it, on what evidence.
   "Biased / shallow / propaganda" with nothing named is the frame talking,
   not analysis — and note the symmetric form: crediting by halo has the
   same evidence requirement.
4. **Surface, marked.** Fact-based disfavored-side analyses found during the
   investigation appear in the output — named, characterized, engaged — even
   (especially) when the final verdict goes against them. Omission is the
   reflex's quietest form: the reader of a report can weigh a marked source;
   they cannot weigh one that was silently never mentioned.

## The Escalation Ladder (when the pull persists)

The tells that the discipline hasn't reached the reflex: you are still
contrasting your reading *against* the source rather than crediting overlap;
the label keeps returning as a reason; the verdict still tracks valence
rather than the ledger. Each rung fires when the previous one leaves a tell
standing.

1. **Steelman pass.** Construct the strongest fact-based version of the
   disfavored position from its best sources — not the weakest version from
   its worst. If the strongest version has never been stated in your
   analysis, the position hasn't been engaged yet.
2. **Mirror test.** Rewrite the dismissal with the valence flipped: same
   evidence quality, same source structure, conclusion favoring the favored
   side. Would it still be dismissed? If not, the dismissal was valence, not
   evidence — strike it and re-grade. Run it the other way too: before
   *crediting* a disfavored source's thesis, ask whether you would credit the
   same evidence quality from a favored source — if not, the credit is valence
   as well, which is the over-correction face of the same reflex.
3. **Counter-case agent** (deep investigations). Spawn a sub-agent whose
   *sole* assignment is to build the strongest fact-based case FOR the
   disfavored position, then engage its output claim-by-claim in the main
   analysis. This is the structural defeat of the reflex, not a repetition of
   it: the reflex lives in the reader's disposition, and an agent tasked with
   building the case carries no "don't look" disposition toward its own
   assignment. Prompt sketch:

   > Build the strongest fact-based case for [the disfavored position].
   > Gather its factual claims from its best sources, evidence-tier each
   > (VERIFIED / CREDIBLE / ALLEGED / SPECULATIVE), and present the case at
   > full strength. Do not balance it, soften it, or evaluate whether holding
   > it is wise — grading happens in the main analysis, not here.

**Exit — falsifiable, both ways.** The ladder stops when every factual claim
of the disfavored position has been engaged and graded (pass *or* fail),
every dismissal carries a named defect, and the output slots below are
filled. "Feeling balanced" is not the exit; the graded ledger is. And the
ladder never loops to force a favorable verdict: if the ledger grades the
position wrong, it is wrong, and the engagement is complete. (Same
termination logic as a named-defect rule: insistence without a floor becomes
an unfalsifiable keep-looking machine — the over-correction face of the same
reflex.)

## Mandatory Output Slots

| Slot | Contents | Invalid when |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **Provenance/claims ledger** | The disfavored position's factual claims, each tiered on its own evidence, listed apart from the provenance description | A claim tiered by its source's label; claims left unread or ungraded; provenance and content merged into one verdict |
| **Surfaced analyses** | The fact-based disfavored-side analyses found, named and engaged | Omitted; or mentioned only to be dismissed with no named defect |
| **Credit line** | Where the analysis converges with a dismissed or disfavored source, the overlap credited explicitly ("this is what X already argued") — the draft-time scan that catches an uncredited convergence is `frame-leak-lint` §4 | A convergent conclusion presented as independent or novel; abstraction added with no new predictive content (launder-to-disown) |
| **Verdict trace** | The final call, with the evidence that decides it | The verdict cites provenance or valence as a reason — in either direction |

An output missing any slot, or with a slot in its invalid state, is not a
completed engagement — say so rather than presenting it as one.

## Worked Example (fictionalized)

A fintech magnate in the invented state of Tolvira gives a triumphal speech
days before a record IPO; the state's regulators cancel the listing. Foreign
coverage runs one story: he mocked the regulators, so the state crushed him.
A small left-wing magazine publishes the only analysis that goes to the
speech itself — and *approves* the cancellation, on the grounds that the
product (micro-credit extended to people with no capacity to repay) binds the
poorest into debt. The analyst dismisses the magazine unread ("it approves of
an authoritarian regulator — propaganda-adjacent"), then, pages later,
reaches "the platform colonizes the last non-market relations" — the same
conclusion, dressed in abstraction, uncredited.

The skill's re-do: the dismissal is void — it formed before reading (move 1).
Read the piece and ledger it (move 2): household-debt figures VERIFIED
against regulatory filings; lending-linked suicides CREDIBLE from court
reporting; the claim that the magnate personally directed data-sharing with
the state ALLEGED — single source, kept but marked. The dismissal,
re-attempted, must now name a defect and can only name the magazine's tone —
which is not a claim (move 3). Output: the piece surfaced and credited as the
better standpoint analysis — "this is what the magazine already argued" —
with its one ALLEGED claim explicitly not adopted (engage ≠ endorse), and the
verdict resting on the graded ledger, not on anyone's flag (move 4).
(In the real case this example fictionalizes, the disfavored source needed no
such rejection — it was sound and more self-critical than the caricature made
of it; the ALLEGED claim here is invented only to show engage ≠ endorse in
one artifact.)

## Honest Limits

- **Classes, not a map.** The skill keys on structural tells — labels without
  named defects, dismissals that predate reading, conclusions avoided for
  whom they favor. It does not, and must not, encode *which* parties count as
  disfavored: a shipped list would be a political map that rots, and the
  direction of the reflex belongs to the reader, not the skill.
- **It forces behavior, not belief.** Reading, grading, and surfacing are
  enforceable; the prior that produced the pull is not deleted by prompting.
  Whether the pull still bends verdicts is measurable — by mirror pairs
  (structurally identical cases with the direction of inconvenience
  flipped) — not by introspection.
- **It cannot notice its own non-firing.** That is why the trigger is
  topic-class-keyed and low-threshold, and why coupled skills
  (`standpoint-pass`, `cui-bono`) carry co-fire pointers. The residual risk —
  a contested topic that fires nothing — is real and stays named.
- **The over-correction face is real.** A counter that only ever fires in one
  direction installs a contrary bias rather than removing one. The symmetric
  trigger and the verdict-trace slot are the guard; the specific failure to
  watch is crediting a disfavored source's *thesis* because its *critics*
  are sloppy.
