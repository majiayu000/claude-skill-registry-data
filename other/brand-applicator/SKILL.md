# Brand Applicator

Use this skill when the user needs existing work to match a defined brand system.

## Workflow
- Collect the real brand inputs first.
- Patch the current deliverable instead of rebuilding from zero unless necessary.
- Summarize what brand rules were actually applied.

## Deliverables
- A brand-aligned output or patch plan.
- A compact change summary.

## Guardrails
- Do not trade away clarity for branding.
- Do not invent missing brand rules without labeling assumptions.
