# Sales

Use this skill when the work is moving a relationship or deal forward.

## Workflow
- Clarify stage, account context, and next desired action.
- Focus on relevance and credibility.
- Route to email, research, or decision skills when useful.

## Deliverables
- A sales-ready brief or message.
- A short next-step recommendation.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent customer pain points or facts.
- Do not hide the commercial ask.
