# Vary the Premise, Not the Layout

**Rule.** The axis that varies is the premise. Three layouts of one premise are one option in three costumes.

**Why.** A site revamp shipped three "distinct" front doors: an index, a masthead over a list, a contact-sheet
grid. All three were *a homepage that catalogues their things*, in the palette the site already wore. One was
picked, fully built, then rejected (*"this doesnt feel right… more options?"*) and the old site kept. The build
wasn't the failure; the spread was.

**How to apply.**
- Write each option's premise as one sentence before generating. Two sentences that differ only in nouns are
  one option: cut it, find a real second.
- Good spreads cross kinds: no navigation at all / time is the spine / it asks before it shows / it is a
  publication, not a site / never the same twice.
- Palette and layout follow from the premise; they are never the variable.
