# Data

Use this skill when the task is fundamentally about tables, files, metrics, or evidence.

## Workflow
- Clarify source data and metric definitions.
- Normalize before analyzing.
- Present results in the most decision-useful format.

## Deliverables
- A cleaned dataset, analysis brief, or dashboard spec.
- A short explanation of key findings.

## Guardrails
- Do not hide missing or messy data.
- Do not overcomplicate simple summaries.
