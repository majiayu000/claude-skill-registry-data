# Total Audio Promo — Indie Artist Pack Strategy

Release promotion framework for **Total Audio Promo pack** (£59, one-shot radio + playlist + blog submission).

## Product

**£59 pack includes:**
- 200 UK radio stations (BBC Introducing, community, online)
- 400 independent playlist curators (vetted, no botted playlists)
- 50 music blogs
- 1-week turnaround

**What it's NOT:**
- Not a 6-week radio campaign
- Not a £5k plugger replacement
- Not a Submithub alternative (no pay-per-review)
- Not botted playlist farms

## ICP

Artists who **already invested four figures** in PR/radio:
- Hired a plugger before (£1k–5k)
- Paid for Submithub premium
- Worked with PR agency (Plus, Liberty, Focus)
- Got radio play but want more reach
- 10k–100k monthly listeners (past bedroom-producer stage)

## Timing (artist's release timeline)

**Best timing**: 2 weeks before release

- Artist submits track + press kit
- TAP submits to radio/playlists within 1 week
- Radio stations playlist 2–4 weeks after release
- Playlist curators add 1–2 weeks after release

**Not ideal**: Day-of-release submissions (radio producers need lead time)

## What artists should have ready

- **Track**: WAV or high-quality MP3
- **Press kit**: 150-word bio, genre tags, previous support (radio/press)
- **Links**: Spotify pre-save or release link
- **Social proof**: previous radio play, monthly listeners, or press coverage

**What artists DON'T need:**
- Professional PR photoshoot (iPhone photo is fine)
- EPK website (press kit doc is enough)
- Music video (nice to have, not required)

## Playlist vetting (no botted playlists)

TAP pack excludes:
- Playlists with stream-to-follower ratio over 100:1
- Playlists that wipe all tracks every 48 hours
- Curators requesting direct PayPal for "guaranteed streams"
- Geographic anomalies (80%+ streams from one server-farm city)

See `botted-playlist-auditor` skill in skills/spotcheck/ for full red flags.

## Radio submission targets

- **BBC Introducing** (regional shows)
- **Community radio** (200+ UK stations)
- **Online radio** (Soho Radio, NTS, Worldwide FM)
- **Student radio** (university stations)

**Not included**: National BBC Radio 1/2/6 Music (those need dedicated plugging, not pack submission)

## Success metrics (what artists should expect)

- **Radio plays**: 5–15 plays across community/online stations (realistic)
- **Playlist adds**: 2–5 independent playlist adds (10k–50k followers)
- **Blog coverage**: 1–3 blog features

**Not realistic from £59 pack**: BBC Radio 1, major Spotify editorial, 100k+ playlist adds
