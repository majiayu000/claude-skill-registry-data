# Nick's Demand Report

Measure whether Nick's is creating and capturing more qualified local demand, not whether vanity metrics moved.

## Core outcomes

Prioritize evidence that connects to:
- phone calls and qualified call outcomes
- direction requests and local discovery
- tire/service inquiries and orders
- booked appointments
- cars arriving at the shop
- repeat customers
- review volume/quality
- revenue and gross-profit proxies when available
- durable local brand recognition

## Workflow

1. Define the exact reporting period and an equal prior comparison period. Add year-over-year when available and comparable.
2. Inventory data sources before analysis. Use connected systems, exports, files, dashboards, or verified production receipts. Mark each source as available, stale, partial, or missing.
3. Build the demand chain:
   - visibility -> engaged demand -> lead/contact -> booked/committed -> arrived/completed -> revenue -> repeat/review.
4. Reconcile channels rather than summing incompatible metrics.
   - Organic search and GBP can overlap.
   - Calls may include existing customers, vendors, spam, or non-marketing traffic.
   - Platform conversions can be modeled or duplicated.
5. Find the biggest positive and negative movements, but weight them by business value and confidence.
6. Identify leakage between stages: high visibility with weak clicks, strong calls with weak booking, bookings with weak arrivals, traffic with poor conversion tracking, or revenue changes unsupported by demand metrics.
7. Surface local competitive or platform changes only when current external evidence materially explains the numbers.
8. Distinguish causation, plausible contribution, and simple correlation.
9. Read `references/report-contract.md` and follow its output structure.

## Rules

- Never call growth successful based on followers, impressions, traffic, or calls alone.
- Never combine counts from different systems unless definitions and deduplication are understood.
- Use exact dates and identify timezone/cutoff mismatches.
- Prefer a small number of high-leverage actions with measurable receipts.
- Flag any area where tracking uncertainty is large enough to undermine the report.
