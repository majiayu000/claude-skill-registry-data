# Consistency

Compounding comes from repeated reliable action; trust comes from predictable behavior. Systems beat motivation.

## Two senses of consistency
1. **Behavioral:** show up repeatably. Small reliable reps beat sporadic bursts. This is exactly Nrupal's designed-vs-actual gap — a rich ideal day means nothing without adherence.
2. **Logical / output:** don't contradict yourself; apply the same standard and quality bar across similar cases.

## How to build it
- **Standard work over heroics.** Make the right action the default and the easy one (routines, checklists, calendar blocks, accountability cadence).
- Keep promises and **close loops** — finish what you start (his open-loop pattern: Oracle, Flexiti, The Brick, Trans Global).
- Hold the same bar every time, not just when motivated.

## Consistency vs rigidity
Be consistent in **principles and process**, flexible in **tactics**. Updating your view when the evidence changes is not inconsistency — confusing the two becomes stubbornness. (Pairs with `conviction-from-evidence`.)

## For Nrupal
Close the designed-vs-actual gap, track scorecard adherence, honor the weekly Sunday cadence, and drive his escalations to resolution.

## Anti-patterns to refuse
- Motivation-dependence; drifting standards; starting strong and fading; using "consistency" as an excuse not to update on new evidence.
