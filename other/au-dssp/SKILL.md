# DSSP, DRP and bonus-share parcel workpapers

## Inputs

Income year, holder entity and residency, capital-account or trading status, Division 230 applicability and elections, issuer and plan terms, elections and effective dates, registry allotment and dividend statements, participating quantities, acquisition dates and original first-element cost and reduced-cost-base balances by parcel. Obtain other cost elements, corporate actions, prior allocations and cash or fractional entitlements.

## Workflow

1. Classify each event from the actual scheme documents and election. Keep cash dividends, DRP purchases and DSSP or bonus-share allotments separate, including partial participation. A registry label does not establish the tax treatment.

2. Open the applicable issuer ruling and legislation for the event date. Check its status, arrangement, shareholder class, fully paid shares, holding basis and residency coverage. Check whether Division 230 applies to the scheme, including any election: CR 2023/61 excludes those holders. Unknown applicability prevents reliance on that ruling. CR 2023/61 concerns the specified Argo scheme; it does not establish every issuer's treatment. Refer materially different or partly paid arrangements for separate analysis.

3. For a DRP, reconcile the dividend, franking and any withholding separately from the reinvested amount and new acquisition parcel. Verify the new acquisition date and first-element basis, including the dividend actually applied to acquire the shares under TD 2000/3; a franking credit is not additional reinvestment consideration. For a DSSP, establish the applicable non-dividend treatment before excluding income or franking. Never transfer dividend franking credits into a DSSP row by analogy.

4. Allocate DSSP bonus quantities across participating original acquisition parcels on the supported pro-rata basis before assigning inherited dates or apportioning costs. For an established post-CGT allocation branch, apportion each original parcel's first-element cost and reduced cost base over its original and allocated bonus shares under the applicable authority. Preserve inherited acquisition dates where that branch requires them. Keep pre-CGT parcels, other cost elements, repeated allotments, rounding and fractional cash separate. Neither foregone dividends nor market value automatically creates additional acquisition cost.

5. Roll forward quantities and first-element balances for every participating parcel. Reconcile quantities to registry holdings and total supported first-element balances before and after each allotment. Retain a rounding bridge and keep reduced cost base distinct from cost base. Where supplied facts support a separate method check, complete its conditional per-parcel quantity and monetary bridges, including the DRP consideration candidate. Label these as arithmetic illustrations of the stated branch; keep approved tax fields unverified until authority and scheme coverage are established. Route a later disposal to the CGT workpaper with the reconciled history.

## Hand-off and checks

An event classification and a parcel register showing original and bonus quantities, acquisition dates, cost-base elements, reduced-cost-base elements, income/franking treatment, source and unresolved items. Missing election, scheme or acquisition evidence leaves the affected treatment and allocation unverified. This prepares transaction records, not investment recommendations or a complete CGT calculation.

For each unresolved item record evidence needed, owner, status and next action. Keep dependent results conditional until the item is resolved.

## Fabricated acceptance example

A registry statement combines DRP purchases and a DSSP allotment while one original parcel lacks acquisition records. Reconcile the known share count, separate the plans and leave the unsupported parcel basis open.
