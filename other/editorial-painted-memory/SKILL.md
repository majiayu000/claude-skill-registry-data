# Editorial Painted Memory

Create one independent poster per photograph. Preserve every original non-destructively.

Use two equal horizontal regions on a 3:4 vertical canvas. Keep the upper region
recognizably photographic with only restrained editorial grading. In the lower region,
reduce the scene to its strongest silhouette, gesture, objects, and spatial relationship.
Draw it small on warm textured paper with thin imperfect lines and no more than four
matte acrylic color shapes. Let negative space occupy most of the lower region.
For dense scenes, choose one relationship and at most three supporting motifs instead of
redrawing every person, window, sign, or object.

Do not substitute crayon, watercolor, heavy oil paint, polished vector art, 3D, or a
commercial cartoon finish. Add small factual typography only when supplied or certain.

Use the best available image generation or editing tool. When an exact unchanged photo
and exact 50:50 split are required, generate the lower artwork separately and assemble
the poster deterministically from a normalized copy of the source.

Verify the 3:4 canvas, equal split, source fidelity, handmade paper material, restrained
palette, subject recognition, and absence of invented text or watermarks. Return the
saved asset path and state whether Direct or Production execution was used.
