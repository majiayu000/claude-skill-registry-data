# TL;DR - distill the last answer

Rewrite **your immediately preceding response** as the leanest version that still conveys the idea. Nothing more, nothing less.

Use short sentences. Do not output more than 3 sentences or bullet points.

Omitting some references or details is fine.

Use simple language. Omit em dashes and semicolons.
