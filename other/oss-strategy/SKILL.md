# Open Source Strategy

## When to use

- Evaluate an open-source business and community strategy.

**Not for:** oss-polish for repository presentation.

## Steps

1. Identify users, buyer, maintenance capacity and commercialization constraints.

2. Compare suitable business models against current primary evidence; avoid universal revenue ratios or launch-time claims.

3. Distinguish open-source licenses from source-available restrictions; verify obligations and leave adoption to the user.

4. Propose a bounded distribution experiment with success and stop criteria, costs and uncertainties.

**Output:** Suggested paths, subject to [human audit](../../../references/human-audit.md): `docs/research/strategy.md` when durable strategy is requested.

## Verify

- Recommendation matches real resources; license and commercial claims are sourced and adoption is explicit.
