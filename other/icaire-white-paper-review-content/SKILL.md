# ICAIRE White Paper: Review Content

Review an ICAIRE white-paper draft before it moves to expert review, approval,
or design and formatting.

## Contract

- Keep scope to ICAIRE white papers. Scientific-paper review, academic peer
  review, journal compliance, and research-integrity review are out of scope
  unless separately requested under a scientific-paper workflow.
- Use the proposal, approval notes, source set, and ICAIRE context as the review
  baseline.
- Check whether the draft is grounded, complete, legitimate, coherent, and ready
  for the next phase.
- Treat missing criteria, templates, citation rules, Arabic support, grammar
  checks, and design standards as explicit review gaps.
- Separate blockers from minor edits and optional improvements.

## Workflow

1. Establish the review baseline.
   - Read the draft, proposal, approval notes, source material, and any
     supplied criteria or templates.
   - Retrieve ICAIRE context when internal claims, stakeholder language, or
     prior decisions need checking.
2. Check scope and structure.
   - Confirm the draft matches the approved white-paper purpose, audience,
     outline, and out-of-scope boundaries.
   - Flag scientific-paper drift such as journal framing, unapproved academic
     claims, or formal hypothesis language.
3. Check evidence and legitimacy.
   - Map major claims to sources.
   - Flag unsupported claims, weak sources, missing citations, overclaims,
     sensitive stakeholder language, and claims needing expert review.
4. Check readability and language.
   - Review grammar, sentence clarity, terminology, abstract structure,
     translation or Arabic-language needs, and duplicated content.
5. Check readiness for formatting.
   - Identify missing figures, diagrams, tables, image permissions, citation
     style decisions, template needs, and design-standard gaps.
6. Produce a decision.
   - Mark the draft as ready for next phase, ready with minor edits, needs major
     revision, or blocked.

## Output

Default to:

- `Review Decision`
- `Major Blockers`
- `Evidence And Citation Issues`
- `Scope Issues`
- `Structure And Argument Issues`
- `Grammar And Language Issues`
- `Arabic Support Needs`
- `Design And Formatting Readiness`
- `Recommended Edits`
- `Questions For Israa Or Leadership`

## Guardrails

- Do not rewrite the whole paper unless the user asks for a revision pass.
- Do not approve unsupported claims because the prose sounds polished.
- Do not invent missing source details, citation metadata, approval criteria, or
  design standards.
- Do not apply scientific-paper review criteria to a white paper by default.
