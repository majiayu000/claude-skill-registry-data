# LeeWay Film Continuity Graph
Track shot adjacency and continuity dimensions explicitly. A cut may intentionally break one dimension; record intent. Use graph queries to identify missing coverage, axis/screen-direction conflicts, state mismatches and shots invalidated by upstream changes.
