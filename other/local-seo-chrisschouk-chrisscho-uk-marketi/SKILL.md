# chrisscho.uk Local SEO

Local SEO for chrisscho.uk — £750 AI workflow audit for Brighton and UK owner-run businesses.

## Strategy (not programmatic clones)

**One service-area page** — Brighton + surrounding areas (Hove, Lewes, Worthing — 20-mile radius)

**Four industry pages** — not cities:
- /for/agencies — marketing, PR, creative agencies
- /for/studios — production, design, architecture
- /for/consultancies — business, finance, ops consultants  
- /for/professional-services — accountants, solicitors, recruiters

**No city-clone factories**: Do not generate 50 "[service] in [city]" pages. That's spam.

## Google Business Profile (GBP)

- **Primary category**: Business Consultant
- **Service areas**: Brighton, Hove, Lewes, Worthing (20-mile radius, not nationwide)
- **Photos**: Chris, office, Brighton landmarks (NO Total Audio Promo logo or music industry branding)
- **Posts**: Weekly 150-word case study or tip (cafe voice, specific to Brighton business scene)

## Page structure (service-area page)

```
H1: AI Workflow Audit for Brighton Businesses
- Intro: 2–3 sentences, cafe voice, Brighton-specific observation
- Who it's for: Owner-run, 2–20 people, stuck in spreadsheets
- What you get: £750, 1-week audit, 30-minute walkthrough
- Examples: 3 Brighton business types (cafe, studio, consultancy) with specific pain points
- CTA: Book 15-minute intro call
```

## Page structure (industry pages)

```
H1: AI Workflow Audit for [Industry]
- Intro: specific to industry workflow (e.g., agencies juggling client Slack threads + Trello boards)
- Pain points: 3 industry-specific workflow breakpoints
- What you get: £750, 1-week audit
- Examples: 2 case studies (NO invented customers — if no real examples yet, write as "common scenario")
- CTA: Book 15-minute intro call
```

## Writing style

- **Cafe voice**: conversational, specific, no corporate fluff
- **UK spelling**: optimise not optimize, favour not favor
- **No em dashes**: use commas or full stops
- **Real examples only**: if you don't have a real customer quote, write "common scenario" not a fake testimonial

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Generate 50 "[service] in [city]" pages
- ❌ Keyword-stuff "AI workflow audit Brighton" 20 times
- ❌ Add Total Audio Promo branding or music industry case studies
- ❌ Invent customer testimonials
- ❌ Write generic "we help businesses transform their operations" fluff
