# Genre Skill: Shooter

Gunplay is the core: aim, fire, clear feedback, fair spawns, readable arenas.

## Map types (use as the variant)

- **team-deathmatch**: two or more teams fight for kills
- **gun-game**: a weapon ladder, advance on each kill
- **objective**: capture points or a payload with shooting
- **box-fight**: small arenas for 1v1 or a few players
