# Product and Author Truth

## Method

1. Verify every material product capability against the current product, approved documentation, or a documented test.
2. Create a Feature Fit Matrix for each material brand mention: reader job, live feature, proof, best link, and useful visual.
3. Remove any brand mention with no fit-matrix row. Use pricing links only for a pricing, plan, or cost job.
4. Select an author; record CMS identity, public profile, topical permission, and three to five examples or a justified house-style fallback.
5. Extract style patterns—opening, BLUF rhythm, examples, product restraint, and banned moves—without copying wording.

## Hard failures

No verified feature, no author identity, missing author profile, wrong CMS author, or unsupported safety/privacy/legal/product claim.

## Output

Feature Fit Matrix, product-proof packet, author-style packet, and byline verification plan.
