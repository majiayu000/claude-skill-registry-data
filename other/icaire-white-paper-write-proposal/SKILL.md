# ICAIRE White Paper: Write Proposal

Create a proposal for an ICAIRE white paper that can be reviewed by leadership
before full drafting begins.

## Contract

- Keep scope to ICAIRE white papers. Scientific papers, journal articles, and
  academic manuscript workflows are out of scope unless ICAIRE later defines
  separate criteria for them.
- Start from the proposed topic, purpose, audience, owner, deadline, and
  intended use.
- Use BigBrain or the ICAIRE MCP connector for current ICAIRE context, related
  initiatives, prior meetings, source material, and stakeholder sensitivities.
- Treat Israa's white-paper criteria, templates, citation rules, abstract
  guidance, Arabic requirements, grammar rules, and design standards as
  placeholders until supplied. Mark missing items clearly.
- Produce a proposal that leadership can approve, reject, or send back with
  specific questions.

## Workflow

1. Define the proposal frame.
   - Identify title, topic, audience, purpose, desired impact, owner, review
     date, and expected output format.
   - If any field is missing, propose a reasonable placeholder and label it.
2. Gather context.
   - Read supplied notes, meeting pages, source pages, prior reports, and
     relevant initiative pages.
   - Retrieve ICAIRE context when stakeholders, priorities, or evidence are
     unclear.
3. Draft the proposal.
   - Write a concise title, working abstract, problem statement, relevance to
     ICAIRE, proposed structure, key claims, evidence needs, and expected
     outputs.
   - Include methodology or benchmarking only when it fits a white-paper
     purpose; do not turn the proposal into a scientific-paper plan.
4. Prepare for approval.
   - List decision points, risks, dependencies, missing sources, review roles,
     and explicit approval questions.
   - Identify where leadership, subject-matter expert, Arabic-language, grammar,
     citation, or design review will be needed.
5. Check readiness.
   - Verify that claims are grounded in source material or marked as proposed.
   - Flag unsupported commitments, vague audience definitions, and missing
     criteria.

## Output

Default to:

- `Working Title`
- `Purpose And Audience`
- `Proposed Abstract`
- `Why This Matters To ICAIRE`
- `Scope`
- `Out Of Scope`
- `Proposed Structure`
- `Evidence And Citation Needs`
- `Review And Approval Questions`
- `Missing Inputs`

## Guardrails

- Do not imply ICAIRE or leadership has approved a proposal unless the source
  material says so.
- Do not fabricate criteria, citations, templates, reviewers, deadlines, or
  design standards.
- Do not include scientific-paper requirements such as journal fit, formal
  hypothesis testing, peer-review strategy, or academic author contribution
  statements unless the user explicitly asks for a separate scientific-paper
  workflow.
- Do not proceed as if placeholder criteria are final.
