# Outline and Article Promise

## Method

1. Start with an opening that answers the reader’s dominant job immediately.
2. Turn the Beat Spec into MECE H2/H3 sections with clear BLUFs.
3. Give the information-gain asset a specific section, not a passing reference.
4. Place at least two relevant internal links and the conversion path where they serve the reader.
5. Reserve visual slots by role: cover/OG, product proof, concept explanation, comparison/decision support, and evidence.
6. Brainstorm at least five honest H1 options; choose the strongest combination of search fit, reader value, and click reason.
7. Use the Oversubscribed lens only if promotion, demand creation, a partner handoff, or link-building is genuinely required.

## Output

Annotated outline, Article Promise, link plan, visual plan, headline decision, and conditional promotion handoff.
