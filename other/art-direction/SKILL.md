# LeeWay 3D Art Direction

## Translate style

Convert references such as low-poly, toy-like, clay, stylized realism, realistic open-world, cinematic, retro or mobile into a target profile: silhouette complexity, bevel/detail scale, topology density, texture resolution/texel density, material realism/stylization, normal/detail strategy, lighting contrast, environment density, animation fidelity and performance envelope.

## Comparative language

A request like 'less Roblox-like, more GTA-style realism' means move from simple/blocky geometry and flat/repeated materials toward richer silhouettes, PBR surface variation, higher texel density, detailed props/environment, grounded lighting/shadows and more realistic spatial/material response—subject to hardware budget. Do not copy proprietary assets, maps, textures or characters.

## Style bible

Produce a reusable scene/asset style profile so independently produced assets converge visually.
