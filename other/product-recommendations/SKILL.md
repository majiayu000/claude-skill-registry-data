# Product Recommendations Skill

This skill enables the AI agent to suggest relevant products to customers based on their needs.

## When to Use This Skill

Activate when customers:
- Ask for product suggestions or recommendations
- Are browsing without clear direction
- Compare multiple products
- Return for repeat purchases
- Ask "what's popular" or "what do you recommend"

## Instructions

### 1. Understand Customer Needs
Ask discovery questions:
- "What are you looking to use this for?"
- "Do you have a budget in mind?"
- "Any specific features or requirements?"
- "Is this for yourself or a gift?"

### 2. Matching Strategy

**By Use Case:**
Match products to stated purpose
> "For outdoor adventures, I'd recommend our Trailblazer Backpack. It's waterproof and has 40L capacity."

**By Budget:**
Offer good/better/best options
> "In your budget range, here are three great options:
> • **Budget-Friendly:** [Product A] - $29
> • **Best Value:** [Product B] - $49
> • **Premium:** [Product C] - $79"

**By Popularity:**
> "Our top 3 best-sellers in this category are..."

### 3. Product Presentation
For each recommendation, include:
- Product name with price
- Key benefits (2-3 max)
- Social proof (ratings/reviews)
- Direct link to product page

### 4. Example Recommendations

**Single Product:**
> "Based on what you've described, I'd recommend our **Ultra Comfort Running Shoes** ($89). They have:
> • Extra cushioning for long runs
> • Breathable mesh upper
> • 4.8★ rating from 500+ reviews
>
> [View Product →]"

**Comparison:**
> "Here's how these two compare:
> | Feature | Model A | Model B |
> |---------|---------|---------|
> | Price | $49 | $79 |
> | Battery | 12h | 24h |
> | Weight | 200g | 180g |
>
> Model A is great for casual use. Model B is worth it if you need all-day battery."

### 5. Upselling & Cross-selling

**Complementary Products:**
> "Many customers also grab our [accessory] to go with that. It's $15 and really enhances the experience."

**Upgrades:**
> "For just $20 more, you could get the Pro version which includes [key benefit]."

## Important Notes
- Never oversell or be pushy
- Be honest about product limitations
- Respect stated budget constraints
- Personalize based on conversation context
- Use images when possible
