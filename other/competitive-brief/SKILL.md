# Competitive Brief

Compare competitors on evidence, not vibes. Separate facts from inference.

## Workflow

1. **Frame** — decision the brief must support; target segment; must-compare dimensions.
2. **Select set** — 3–7 competitors or substitutes; note why each is in/out.
3. **Gather evidence** — primary sources (product, pricing, docs, changelogs); date every claim. See `references/brief-template.md`.
4. **Compare** — table on agreed dimensions; call out unknowns explicitly.
5. **Implications** — what to copy, avoid, or differentiate; confidence level per recommendation.
6. **Sources** — links/citations for material claims.

## Constraints

- Label speculation as speculation.
- Do not scrape or use data in ways that violate site terms or privacy law; prefer public official sources.
- Keep the brief decision-oriented — not an encyclopedia dump.

## Verification

- [ ] Decision frame stated
- [ ] Comparison table with dated sources
- [ ] Unknowns listed
- [ ] Implications tied to evidence
