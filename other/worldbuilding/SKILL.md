# LeeWay Worldbuilding
Build only what serves the work while maintaining deeper causal coherence. Track physical/social rules, geography, institutions, resources/economics, technology/magic constraints, history, culture and terminology. Feed canon/continuity; avoid exposition dumps merely because world detail exists.
