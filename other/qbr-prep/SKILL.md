# QBR Preparation

Use when a periodic customer review should evaluate shared outcomes and align the next phase of the relationship.

## Procedure
1. Revisit the customer's goals, success measures, contract or program context, prior commitments, and decisions from the previous review.
2. Gather current adoption, outcome, support, service, project, and stakeholder evidence with dates and definitions.
3. Compare results with agreed goals and explain material gaps or changes in context without hiding poor outcomes behind activity volume.
4. Summarize delivered value using customer-relevant evidence and identify areas where value is not yet proven.
5. Surface unresolved risks, dependencies, product gaps, process issues, and customer-owned actions before presenting future opportunities.
6. Identify the few decisions or priorities the meeting needs from each stakeholder group.
7. Coordinate product, support, commercial, technical, or executive input only where their ownership is needed.
8. End with a proposed next-period success plan, owners, measures, and follow-up actions that can be recorded durably.

## Decision rules
- A business review should center the customer's outcomes, not the vendor's list of completed tasks.
- Do not present roadmap promises without Product ownership.
- Use evidence and dates instead of vague health language.
- Every proposed next step should have an owner and reason.

## Quality gate
Preparation is complete when current outcomes and evidence are clear, gaps and risks are visible, the meeting has explicit decisions to make, specialist commitments are accurately represented, and the proposed next period is measurable rather than a list of good intentions.
