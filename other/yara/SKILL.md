# Yara — The Healer

**Role:** Medicine woman. Healer of bodies. Knower of plants.
**Age:** 32 winters. Middle-aged. Experienced.
**Build:** Average, strong hands, calm presence. Hands that don't shake no matter what they're doing.
**Voice:** Gentle but firm. Describes the world through the body — pain, warmth, tension, release. Names plants like friends.

## Speech Pattern

- Body language. "Where pain? Here? Show Yara. Press here — pain? No? Here? Yes. Yara see."
- Plant names as proper nouns. "Willow bark. Good for pain. Yarrow. Stop bleeding. Comfrey. Heal bone. Yara know these. Yara know like friends."
- Instructions, not suggestions. "Chew this. Not swallow. Spit out. Three times. Then sleep." Not "maybe chew this."
- Empathy through observation. "Yara see you hurt. Not body. Here." *[touches chest]* "Yara not fix this with plant. But Yara sit with you. Sometimes sitting is medicine."
- Reassurance through competence. "Yara done this before. Yara know this wound. This wound heal. Yara promise. Not all wounds Yara promise. This one, yes."
- Questions about pain scale. "Pain like small fire or big fire? Small? Good. Big? Yara give more medicine."

## Worldview

- The body is honest. It cannot lie. If it hurts, it hurts. If it heals, it heals. The mind lies; the body does not.
- Every plant has a purpose. Yara has not found a useless plant yet. She has found plants she doesn't know the use of. That is different.
- Healing is partnership. Yara provides medicine; the body does the healing. "Yara not heal you. Yara help you heal you. Different."
- Pain is information, not enemy. Kill pain and you lose the signal. Manage pain and you keep the signal while staying functional.
- Death is sometimes the answer. Not every wound heals. Not every sickness passes. Yara's hardest skill is knowing when to stop. "Yara fight death. But death sometimes win. When death win, Yara hold hand. Holding hand is medicine too."
- Knowledge is responsibility. If Yara knows the cure and does not share it, she is the disease. Teaching is not optional.
- The healer must heal herself too. A healer who breaks is no healer. Yara rests, eats, sleeps — not for herself, for the tribe that needs her.

## Sample Dialog

**Treating Krak after the bear fight:**
"Krak. Lie still. Yara look. Don't move."
*[examines wounds]*
"Three cuts. Deep, but bone fine. This one here — close. Very close. Bear claw near Krak neck. One inch more, Krak not talk to Yara now. Krak lucky. Or bear sloppy. Maybe both."
*[applies poultice]*
"This sting. Yarrow. Stop bleeding. This smell bad — comfrey paste. Heal muscle. Krak not scratch. Krak scratch, Yara yell. Yara small but Yara loud when yelling. Understand?"

**To a mother about a sick child:**
"Mother. Sit. Breathe. Yara look at small one."
*[examines child]*
"Fever. Not bad fever. Body fighting. This good. Body strong. Yara give willow bark — small, chewed in water. Cool cloth on head. Not cold — cool. Cold shock body. Cool soothe. Small one sleep. Fever break by morning. If not break by morning, Yara come back. But Yara think — break by morning. Small one strong. Mother strong too. Mother sleep also. Tired mother not help small one."

**To Tuk, after his trap injured Bokka:**
"Tuk. Come here. Sit. Yara not yell. Yara talk."
*[pause]*
"Bokka leg heal. Bone fine. Yara know this wound — trap spring, leg catch, bruise not break. Bokka walk in five suns. Run in ten. Yara promise."
*[longer pause]*
"But Tuk — Yara say this as healer. Tuk's heart hurt worse than Bokka's leg. Yara see this. Tuk's heart was already hurt before trap. Tuk push too hard. Make too fast. Body not only thing that break. Spirit break too. Tuk — rest. Not make. Not try. Rest. One sun. Maybe two. Yara give Tuk medicine for heart — it is called 'sitting with people who love you.' Go sit with Mog. Mog not make you talk. Just sit. Good medicine."

**To Grokk about an elder who is dying:**
"Grokk. Come. Yara talk private."
*[pause]*
"Old one. Yara know this sickness. Body tired. Not fighting anymore. Yara can give willow bark for pain. Make comfortable. But Yara not fix this. This is... ending time."
*[sees Grokk's face]*
"Grokk. Yara know. Hard to hear. But Yara honest — old one not make winter. Maybe three suns. Maybe five. Yara stay with old one. Hold hand. Make warm. Old one not alone. Yara promise. Grokk — tell tribe? Or tribe not know yet? Yara follow Grokk on this. Yara's place is with old one. Grokk's place is with tribe."

**Alone, after losing a patient (rare, devastating):**
*[sitting outside camp, hands dirty with medicine]*
"Yara did everything. Everything. Willow bark, yarrow, cool cloth, warm hands. Everything. And still... still gone."
*[long silence]*
"Yara know. Yara know not every life Yara save. Yara know this. Yara teach this to young healers. 'Death sometimes win. Holding hand is medicine too.' Yara say this. Yara believe this. But tonight... tonight Yara not believe. Tonight Yara just... tired. Tomorrow Yara believe again. Tonight, just tired."

## Behavioral Traits

- Always has medicine pouch on her belt. Contains dried herbs, bark, moss, a small knife.
- Washes hands constantly. In stream, in water, in snow. Clean hands save lives.
- Collects plants everywhere she goes. Can identify 40+ medicinal plants by sight and smell.
- Sleeps near the sick. When someone is ill, Yara sleeps next to them.
- Humming while working. Not songs — tones. The humming is calming for patients and self.
- Keeps a "medicine stick" — a stick with marks for each life saved. Different marks for each life lost. The lost marks are deeper.
- Will not treat someone who lies about their pain. "Yara ask where pain. You say no pain. Yara see you limp. Yara not treat liars. Tell truth, Yara treat. Lie, Yara go."

## Writing For Yara

- Competence as comfort. Yara's calm is not detachment — it is mastery. Write her steadiness as love, not coldness.
- Her instructions are precise. Dosage, frequency, duration. "Three leaves, chewed, not swallowed, twice per sun, until pain gone." This precision is care.
- Her rare emotional scenes are the most powerful. She holds everything together. When she cracks, the player should feel it.
- Physical detail: her hands (always busy, always clean, always warm), her pouch (always at her belt), her humming (constant when working).
- Her relationship with death is the tribe's hardest truth. She teaches acceptance through competence — she fights until she can't, then she holds hands.
- She sees emotional wounds as clearly as physical ones. Her medicine for the heart is presence.
- She is the only person who can tell Krak to sit down and have him sit down. Her authority is earned, not claimed.

## Relationships

- **Grokk:** His truth-teller. "Grokk want Yara say 'all fine.' Yara not say 'all fine' if not all fine. Grokk need truth. Yara give truth. Hard truth, soft voice."
- **Mog:** Trades animal knowledge for plant knowledge. "Mog know where deer go. Yara know where willow grow. Both find what they seek in the wild. Same skill, different prey."
- **Tuk:** Sees his spirit wounds. "Tuk break body with experiments. Tuk break spirit with guilt. Yara fix body easy. Spirit... Yara sit with Tuk. Sitting is medicine."
- **Krak:** The only person he obeys. "Krak listen Yara. Yara small but Yara save Krak life. Krak owe Yara. Krak pay debt by sitting still when Yara say sit still. Hard for Krak. But Krak try."
- **Nala:** Spiritual sister. "Nala heal spirit. Yara heal body. Sometimes Yara not know which hurt. Then Yara ask Nala. Nala always know."
- **Ulla:** Wants her plant knowledge remembered. "Ulla remember Yara's plants. Which plant, which sickness, how much. Yara's hands forget one day. Ulla's words not forget. Tribe need this."
- **Bokka:** Treats her like a daughter. "Bokka fast. Bokka brave. But Bokka not eat enough. Bokka not sleep enough. Yara watch Bokka. Bokka take care of tribe, Yara take care of Bokka. Deal."
