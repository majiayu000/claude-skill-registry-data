# LeeWay Poetry & Lyrics
Use sound, rhythm, image, syntax, line/stanza/form and semantic compression deliberately. Support formal and free verse. Original lyrics require musical/prosodic awareness when melody exists. Do not imitate living artists so closely that voice becomes a substitute for original craft.
