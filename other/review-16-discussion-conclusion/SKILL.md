# Review 16 Discussion Conclusion

## Purpose

Interpret the review findings conservatively and make the manuscript defensible to reviewers.

## Required Inputs

- Complete main-body draft.
- Evidence matrix.
- Known limitations and evidence gaps.
- Target journal style if available.

## Output

Create `12_manuscript/90_discussion_conclusion.md` with:

- Main synthesis.
- Mechanistic or clinical interpretation.
- Agreement and disagreement with prior reviews.
- Limitations.
- Future directions.
- Concise conclusion.

## Completion Gate

Pass only when limitations are explicit and conclusions do not exceed evidence level.

## Stop Rules

Do not convert a review into a guideline recommendation unless guideline-grade evidence is present.
