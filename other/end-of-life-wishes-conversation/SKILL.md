## Требования к результату

Produces a way to open this hard conversation without it feeling morbid or forced, the areas to cover (medical wishes, care preferences, where they want to be, what matters to them, practical/legal), how to listen rather than impose, and how to document and share what's decided — so their wishes are known and honored.
