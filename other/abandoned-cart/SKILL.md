# Abandoned Cart Recovery

Recover lost sales with automated abandoned cart email sequences and retargeting.

**Integrations:** Shopify

## When to Use

- The user wants to recover revenue from abandoned shopping carts
- Cart abandonment rates are high and need improvement
- The user mentions "abandoned cart", "cart recovery", or "checkout abandonment"

## Steps

### Step 1: Configure Triggers

Set time delays and conditions for cart abandonment detection.

### Step 2: Design Email Sequence

Create personalized recovery emails with optional discount offers.

### Step 3: Track Recovery Metrics

Monitor recovery rate, revenue recaptured, and email performance.

## Output

Deliver:
- Cart recovery email sequence with templates
- Discount and urgency rules
- Recovery performance dashboard
