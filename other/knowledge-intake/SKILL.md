# Knowledge Intake

## Workflow

1. Identify source, date, format, scope, sensitivity, and whether the original must be preserved.
2. Preserve or reference the input before summarizing it.
3. Classify extracted statements with [references/evidence-labels.md](references/evidence-labels.md).
4. Search existing notes by title, aliases, keywords, and meaning.
5. Use [references/intake-classification.md](references/intake-classification.md) to choose Raw, Wiki, Outputs, Inbox, update, or no write.
6. Handle disagreements with [references/conflict-handling.md](references/conflict-handling.md).
7. Preview writes when authorization is absent or disputed.
8. Report sources, created or updated paths, unresolved items, and excluded material.

Use [assets/intake-template.md](assets/intake-template.md) when a raw intake record is useful. Do not invent missing abbreviations or context.
