# Weekly Reset

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Identify the few areas that most affect daily usability.
2. Handle trash, dishes, laundry flow, surfaces, and restocking in an efficient sequence.
3. Assign shared work visibly when multiple capable household members are involved.
4. Stop when the home is functional enough; do not convert reset day into an endless deep clean.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
