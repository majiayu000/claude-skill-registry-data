# ICAIRE White Paper: Design And Format

Turn reviewed ICAIRE white-paper content into a clean, branded document ready
for stakeholder review or publication preparation.

## Contract

- Keep scope to ICAIRE white papers. Scientific-paper journal formatting,
  submission packages, and academic publisher templates are out of scope unless
  a separate scientific-paper workflow is defined.
- Start from reviewed content. If content review is missing, label the output
  as formatting-only and list unresolved content risks.
- Use ICAIRE brand guidance and repo-owned assets when available. ICAIRE's
  primary brand color is `#4A9549` unless an approved standard overrides it.
- Treat final templates, citation style, abstract structure, Arabic typography,
  grammar standards, image rules, diagram standards, and design standards as
  placeholders until supplied.
- Verify rendered output visually before claiming it is ready.

## Workflow

1. Confirm formatting inputs.
   - Identify document format, audience, deadline, template, brand assets,
     language requirements, citation style, image permissions, and review status.
2. Prepare the document structure.
   - Apply title page, abstract or executive summary, section hierarchy,
     citations, references, figures, diagrams, tables, captions, and appendices
     as appropriate for a white paper.
3. Apply design standards.
   - Use ICAIRE visual identity, restrained layout, readable typography,
     consistent spacing, clear hierarchy, and accessible contrast.
   - Use diagrams, imagery, and tables only when they clarify the argument.
4. Run language and citation cleanup.
   - Check grammar, sentence-level consistency, terminology, Arabic-language
     handling, citation formatting, reference completeness, and placeholder
     text.
5. Render and verify.
   - Inspect the actual PDF, DOCX, slide, or layout output for clipping,
     awkward wrapping, overlap, weak contrast, broken images, citation errors,
     missing figures, and unresolved placeholders.
   - Iterate until visual issues are fixed or clearly reported.

## Output

Default to:

- `Formatted Output`
- `Template Or Design Standard Used`
- `Brand Assets Used`
- `Citation Format Status`
- `Arabic And Grammar Status`
- `Visual QA`
- `Remaining Placeholders`
- `Publication Readiness`

## Guardrails

- Do not claim publication readiness when content review, citation checks,
  Arabic support, grammar checks, or design standards are still missing.
- Do not invent final templates or citation rules.
- Do not use scientific journal formatting as the default.
- Do not rely only on source-file existence; render and visually inspect the
  produced document when creating or editing files.
