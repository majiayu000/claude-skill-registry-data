# Business model

Status: Draft. Not yet verified in a documented practical example.

Input: product, audience, possible revenue streams and public or synthetic assumptions. Do not request or store personal financial information, private company forecasts or customer records. Unknown costs stay unknown, not zero.

## Procedure

1. **Models.** Compare credible revenue models and pricing hypotheses. Record evidence of willingness to pay separately from interest. Estimate a reachable market from explicit counts and prices; distinguish total market, serviceable market and plausible initial reach. Do not manufacture precision.
2. **Costs.** Separate one-time development costs from recurring fixed and variable expenses. Include hosting, storage, AI usage, payment fees, support, acquisition and maintenance when relevant. Keep units, currency and time period consistent.
3. **Scenarios.** Calculate conservative, base and optimistic cases with labelled assumptions. Show revenue, contribution, operating result, cash burn and runway where meaningful. Check sensitivity to retention, usage and support burden; flag missing inputs.
4. **Obligations.** List ownership, intellectual-property, licences, tax, privacy and consumer-law questions for qualified review. Draft commercial, refund and service-policy options, never compliance conclusions or binding terms. Do not select a repository licence.
5. **Decision.** Recommend validate, revise or stop with the next evidence needed. The human owner decides price, investment, commitments and contracts. Do not purchase, bill, register a company or accept terms.

## Calculation checks

For a synthetic subscription example, 100 paying accounts at 8 currency units per month produce 800 units of monthly revenue. At 2 units variable cost per account, contribution is 600; with 700 fixed monthly costs, operating result is -100 before any omitted items. This is arithmetic, not a forecast.

- Contribution per account = net revenue per account minus variable cost per account.
- Break-even accounts = fixed recurring costs divided by positive contribution per account, rounded up.
- Runway = available business cash divided by positive monthly net cash burn, assuming constant burn. Otherwise explain why this shortcut is inapplicable.

## Output

Return assumptions with sources, scenario calculations, omitted costs, risk register, policy questions and decision owner. Do not claim profitability from revenue alone or legal readiness from an AI-generated checklist.
