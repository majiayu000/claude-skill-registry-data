# DataQ Filing for Crash Classification Errors

Per § 390.5, a DOT-recordable crash requires: fatality, bodily injury requiring medical treatment off-scene, OR disabling damage requiring tow-away. Crashes missing these triggers are NOT recordable. Challenge them.

## Common misclassification scenarios
- **Crash with no injuries** but tow-away (this IS recordable; not a challenge target)
- **Crash with minor injuries** where first aid was given on scene only — NOT recordable
- **Crash with damage** but vehicles were drivable — NOT recordable (tow-away requires inability to drive)
- **Crash with passenger pre-existing condition** — depends on what triggered medical treatment
- **Crash where parties drove away** but later sought medical treatment — typically NOT recordable (treatment must be away from scene immediately)

## Evidence needed
- Police report (often shows injury status, tow-away status)
- Photos of damage
- Witness statements
- Medical records (if available, can show no immediate off-scene treatment)
- Vehicle inspection results
- Insurance claim records

## Filing steps
1. Go to fmcsa.dot.gov/DataQ
2. Search for the crash record
3. Challenge category: "Crash Classification"
4. Subcategory: "Not DOT-recordable"
5. Narrative explaining why crash does not meet § 390.5 triggers
6. Upload evidence

## Success rate
- Documentation clearly shows no qualifying trigger: ~70%
- Officer reasonably classified given on-scene info: ~30%
