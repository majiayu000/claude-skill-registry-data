# Sleep Routine

Use when this capability directly owns the requested outcome.

## Procedure
1. Work backward from the required wake time and realistic sleep opportunity.
2. Reduce stimulating tasks and bright-light exposure near bedtime when practical.
3. Use a short repeatable sequence that signals the end of the day.
4. Prefer consistency over elaborate sleep rituals.

## Output standard
Give a concrete result, decision aid, or next action. Keep assumptions visible and hand off work outside this skill's domain.
