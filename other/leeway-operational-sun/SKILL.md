# LeeWay Operational Sun

## Five-ray v1 register
1 ADMIN_SERVICE
2 LOGISTICS_FULFILLMENT
3 SALES_PIPELINE
4 MARKETING_GROWTH
5 PEOPLE_HR

For evidence-bound x_k, SPD W_k and theta_k: r_k=sqrt(x_k^T W_k x_k); b_k=1 iff r_k<=theta_k. Omega=sum(b_k*2^(5-k)); Omega_max=31; Phi=product(b_k).

## Target law
Zero means zero deviation from the declared operating profile, not literal zero queue, zero turnover or one universal ratio. rho=0.80 and LTV:CAC=3 may be profile defaults/heuristics only when justified.

## Hard extensions
Finance/accounting integrity, compliance/legal/privacy, authorization, payroll correctness, safety and mandatory controls can independently block action even when Omega=31.

## Breach law
b_k=0 dispatches validation/diagnosis to the owning capability. It does not authorize a business mutation. Data quality is checked before operational remediation.

## Green law
Omega=31 means the five-ray profile is inside tolerance at the measured window. Record evidence and continue event/time-based observation; do not infer permanent health.
