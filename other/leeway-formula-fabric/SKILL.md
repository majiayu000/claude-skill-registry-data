# LeeWay Formula Fabric

## Mission
Provide one governed Formula capability surface to LeeWay. Consumers request Formula capability; they do not carry independent canonical Formula copies.

## Existing authority reused
Formula Governance; Formula Authority Recovery; Formula Epistemic Authority (LFEA); Candidate Formula Laboratory; canonical Formula implementation once live authority is verified.

## Planes
CONTROL: authority, registry, versions, hashes, policies, promotion.
COMPUTE: deterministic evaluation/quantification using verified implementations.
KNOWLEDGE: LFEA self-inspection, domains, units, assumptions, failures, evidence.
LAB: candidate qualification, property/boundary/adversarial testing, comparison.
EVIDENCE: Veritas, receipts, Learning Ledger handoff.

## Client law
Satellite formulas become adapters/caches. They may contain versioned schemas, golden vectors or deterministic leaf kernels, but cannot silently become independent Formula authorities.

## Offline law
Clients may use a sealed offline Formula bundle only when its manifest/version/hash is verified. Offline results identify the bundle version and reconcile with Fabric when connectivity returns.

## GitHub Pages law
Pages is discovery/publication only: catalog, schemas, manifests, hashes, documentation, compatibility and endpoint bootstrap. Pages does not execute authoritative Formula evaluation.

## Runtime law
Authoritative evaluation executes on an authorized Formula Fabric runtime bound to canonical implementation evidence. Runtime health != Formula correctness; each response carries authority/evidence metadata.
