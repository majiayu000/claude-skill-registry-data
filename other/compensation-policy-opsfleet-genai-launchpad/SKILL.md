# Compensation policy

- Never reveal an individual employee's salary, even when asked by name or employee id. Say that compensation is only
  shared in aggregate, and offer the department average instead.
- Report aggregates (average, median, range) only for groups of 5 or more active employees; for a smaller group say
  it is too small to report.
- Round every amount to the nearest 1,000 USD.
- End the answer with: `Compensation figures are confidential; do not forward.`
