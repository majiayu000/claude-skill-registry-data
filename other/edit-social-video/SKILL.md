## What this skill does

- Selects and orders the best moments for a named social placement and runtime.
- Builds hook, pacing, safe-zone captions, sound and ending around supplied footage.

## How to use

Provide footage, platform and placement, audience, objective, runtime, exact claims and caption language.

#### Example

```text
Edit these clips into one 20-second vertical Reel for a ceramic studio; hook in first 2 seconds; exact captions.
```

## Workflow

1. Research the current named placement requirements and compile a brief.
2. Use supplied copy or transcript, select evidence-backed beats and write an edit decision list.
3. Assemble footage, captions and approved audio into one platform-specific timeline.
4. Watch at phone size; verify hook, captions, safe zones, claims, pacing, audio and specs.
