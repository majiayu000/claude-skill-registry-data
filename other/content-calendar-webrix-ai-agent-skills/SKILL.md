# Content Calendar

Plan and manage content calendars with scheduling, assignments, and cross-channel coordination.

**Integrations:** Notion, Slack

## When to Use

- The user needs to plan content for upcoming weeks or months
- Content creation needs better organization and scheduling
- The user mentions "content calendar", "editorial calendar", or "content planning"

## Steps

### Step 1: Define Content Mix

Set channel frequency, content types, and themes.

### Step 2: Schedule Content

Assign dates, authors, and deadlines for each piece.

### Step 3: Coordinate Channels

Ensure cross-channel consistency and timing.

### Step 4: Track Progress

Monitor creation progress and publication status.

## Output

Deliver:
- Content calendar with all channels
- Author assignments and deadlines
- Progress tracking board
