# Marketing Reporting

Generate comprehensive marketing reports with channel performance, ROI, and attribution data.

**Integrations:** Google Analytics, HubSpot

## When to Use

- The user needs a marketing performance report for leadership
- Marketing teams want to track ROI by channel
- The user mentions "marketing report", "channel performance", or "marketing ROI"

## Steps

### Step 1: Aggregate Channel Data

Pull performance data from all marketing channels.

### Step 2: Calculate ROI

Compute return on investment by channel and campaign.

### Step 3: Generate Report

Create formatted report with visualizations and insights.

## Output

Deliver:
- Channel performance breakdown
- Campaign ROI analysis
- Executive summary with recommendations
