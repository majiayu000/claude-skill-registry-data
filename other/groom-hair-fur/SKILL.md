# LeeWay 3D Groom Hair & Fur

Build scalp/surface attachment, guides, density, length, clump, curl/frizz/noise and silhouette. Validate root attachment, penetration, interpolation and motion. Choose strand/curve, cards/shells or baked representations by renderer/runtime budget. Blender's current hair Geometry Nodes can generate, interpolate, attach, curl, clump and deform curves; treat those nodes as provider operations, not the skill itself.
