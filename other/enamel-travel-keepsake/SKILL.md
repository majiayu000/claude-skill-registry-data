# Enamel Travel Keepsake

Create one independent 3:4 vertical poster per photograph and preserve the original
file. Split the poster into two equal horizontal regions with no border or overlap.

Keep the lower half as an authentic, source-faithful crop of the supplied photograph.
Use the upper half as a clean solid-color field sampled from the source. Identify the
single most memorable person, landmark, or landscape motif and compress only that motif
into a small premium enamel magnet occupying roughly 20–30% of the poster width. Use a
manufacturable contour, narrow dark metal boundaries, and three to six source-derived
enamel color fields. Retain the decisive identity cues and discard micro-detail.

Center the magnet in generous negative space. A short handwritten English label may sit
below it only when the user supplies the exact wording or the subject name is certain;
otherwise omit text. Give the magnet restrained thickness, directional light, and a
small contact shadow. Avoid standalone product photography, neutral tabletop scenes,
generic shields, toy plastic, chrome, glitter, packaging, hands, invented locations,
and fake text.

Use the best available image generation or editing tool. In Production mode, generate
the upper keepsake panel separately and assemble it above an unchanged normalized source
crop. Verify exact 3:4 geometry, equal halves, source fidelity, small magnet scale,
recognizability, color economy, manufacturability, and the absence of watermarks. Return
each saved asset path separately and name the execution mode used.
