# Operations

Use this skill when the task is mainly about processes, SOPs, recurring reports, or workflow improvement.

## Workflow
- Clarify owner, cadence, input, and output.
- Prefer checklists, workflows, and dashboards over vague advice.
- Route the work toward the most relevant operational sub-skill.

## Deliverables
- An operations-ready brief, SOP, or workflow plan.
- A clear next-step list.

## Guardrails
- Do not leave cadence or ownership ambiguous.
- Do not create process overhead heavier than the problem.
