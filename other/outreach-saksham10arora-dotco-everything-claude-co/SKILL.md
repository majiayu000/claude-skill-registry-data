# /outreach: networking that actually happens

Read `personal/networking_tracker.md`. Show recent contacts, who is due a follow-up, and progress against the weekly target in hot.md (default 3 to 5 contacts a week).

## New message
Find out who the person is and why they matter. Hook with one real proof point from the "Strongest signals" section of hot.md, the one most relevant to them. Short, specific, references their actual work, never cringe.

## Follow-up
Reference the last message and add new signal (a shipped project, a sharp question). Never "just checking in".

## Log it
After sending, add to the tracker: name, company, role, date, channel, what was said, next action.
