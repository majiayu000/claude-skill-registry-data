# Product Tracking: Business Case

Frame telemetry as a decision-quality investment, not a promise that data automatically creates value.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience, decision, product outcome, current evidence gaps, and existing analytics capability.
2. State the questions the organization cannot answer reliably today, with known evidence and uncertainty.
3. Link a minimum telemetry scope to decisions such as activation, retention, adoption, reliability, or support prioritization.
4. Describe required work, ownership, privacy review, maintenance, risks, and a bounded pilot.
5. Define success evidence and a stop condition; avoid invented ROI or unsupported time savings.

## Deliverable

Write a five-minute decision brief:

```text
Decision requested
Current blind spots
Minimum viable telemetry scope
Expected decisions enabled
Cost, ownership, privacy and operational risks
Pilot, success measures, and next decision
```

## Guardrails

- Separate facts, estimates, and assumptions.
- Do not claim that AI, a vendor, or instrumentation alone guarantees business outcomes.
- Do not use customer data or internal financial information outside the authorized scope.
