# LeeWay 3D CAD BIM & Manufacturing

Use exact units, constraints, reference geometry, assemblies and tolerance semantics when engineering/manufacturing/BIM intent exists. Distinguish visual mesh from analytic/parametric geometry. Validate clearances, interfaces and fabrication constraints. Rendering cannot certify manufacturability or structural safety.
