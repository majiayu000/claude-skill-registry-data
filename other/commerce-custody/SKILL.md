# LeeWay Skill House — Commerce & Custody

## Mission
Prove that contractual/commercial obligations, money, product structure and physical custody reconcile across the full order-to-cash and procure/build/ship/receive lifecycle.

## Core invariant
Contract/Order → Rated Charge → Invoice/Credit → Cash/Application must reconcile financially.
Product/BOM/PO → Build/Ship/BOL → Receipt/Inspection → Vendor Invoice must reconcile physically/commercially.
Exceptions remain explicit; authorized credits/write-offs/scrap/tolerances are governed states, not hidden leakage.

## Legal/financial boundary
Tax, freight classification, bills of lading, Incoterms/FOB terms, accounting treatment and statutory invoice requirements depend on jurisdiction/contracts. Use current authoritative evidence and human/professional approval where consequential.
