# LeeWay People & HR Operations
Track staffing demand/capacity, recruiting funnel, time-to-fill, onboarding, attendance/leave, payroll input integrity, retention and development. Workforce transition models may support planning but must not become automated adverse-employment decisions. Hiring, firing, discipline, compensation and protected-class-sensitive decisions remain governed human processes.
