# LeeWay Scene & Dialogue
Every scene needs a reason to exist and a changed state, but not every professional scene requires a full emotional sign reversal. Track objective, opposition, tactic changes, information movement, relationship/status movement, decision/action and exit consequence. Dialogue may include exposition when motivated; avoid inert explanation, not information itself. Preserve distinct voices and subtext where appropriate.
