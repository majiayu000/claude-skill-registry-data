# LeeWay 3D Character & Anatomy

Use skeletal landmarks, major muscle/fat volumes, proportion references and deformation needs to construct believable characters/creatures. Stylization may exaggerate anatomy but should remain internally coherent. Coordinate with sculpting, topology, facial systems, grooming, clothing, rigging, materials and animation. Do not infer an identifiable real person's exact anatomy from inadequate reference.
