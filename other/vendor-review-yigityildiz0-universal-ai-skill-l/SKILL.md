# Vendor Review

Compare alternatives against the decision, not a generic feature checklist.

1. Define decision owner, use cases, non-negotiables, constraints, data sensitivity, budget horizon, and exit requirements.
2. Gather source-backed evidence for capabilities, pricing assumptions, integration, security/privacy posture, support, roadmap, and lock-in.
3. Score only criteria that matter; show weights, evidence strength, unknowns, and assumptions.
4. Produce a recommendation, pilot plan, contract/implementation questions, and reversal path.

Do not represent marketing claims as verified facts or make purchasing commitments. Verify current pricing and contractual terms from authoritative sources before a spend decision.
