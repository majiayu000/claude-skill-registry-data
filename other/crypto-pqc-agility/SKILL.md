# LeeWay Cryptography & PQC Agility
Use standardized, reviewed implementations rather than hand-rolling cryptographic primitives. Inventory algorithms/keys/certificates/protocols/data lifetimes, classify HNDL risk, map FIPS 203 ML-KEM, FIPS 204 ML-DSA and FIPS 205 SLH-DSA migration paths, test interoperability/performance and preserve rollback/deprecation plans. PQC deployment reduces modeled quantum exposure; it does not prove perpetual cryptographic security.
