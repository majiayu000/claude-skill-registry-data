# Total Audio Promo Outreach

Cold and warm outreach for **Total Audio Promo** pack (£59 for 2026, one-shot radio/playlist/blog pack).

## Brand

**Total Audio Promo** — never "Platform", never "TAP Platform". The product is the pack.

## Sending address

**info@totalaudiopromo.com** — never promo@

## ICP (2026 pack focus)

Artists who **already paid four figures** for PR, radio, or plugging:
- Hired a plugger (£1k–5k) in the last 2 years
- Paid for Submithub premium campaigns
- Worked with a PR agency (Plus, Liberty, Focus)

**Retired ICP**: PR agencies. Do not pitch agencies in 2026.

## Excludes (do not pitch as TAP pack)

Check `growth-os/brands/tap/excludes.md` before sending:

- **Holy Basil**
- **LAMIA**
- **Kag Katumba**

These artists are known contacts or wrong fit. Do not send pack pitches.

## What NOT to mention

- ❌ Paid streaming (playlist farms, stream padding)
- ❌ "Platform" branding
- ❌ Liberty plugger recaps as sales material (those are evidence for Chris, not artist-facing copy)
- ❌ Pricing over £59 (2026 pack is flat £59)

## Voice

- **Indie artist voice**: conversational, music-scene insider, no corporate fluff
- **UK spelling**: favour not favor, centre not center
- **No em dashes**: commas or full stops
- **Specific to their release**: mention genre, previous single, radio support, not generic "great track" fluff

## Message structure

1. **Open with specific observation** — their last release, radio play, Spotify monthly listeners, or previous PR campaign
2. **Pack offer** — £59, what's included (radio, playlist, blog submission)
3. **Evidence** — mention one artist who got BBC 6 Music or similar (NO fake testimonials — if no real example, skip)
4. **CTA** — link to book or reply

### Example (pass)

```
Hi Emma,

I heard your last single on Amazing Radio. 50k monthly listeners — you're past 
the "building audience" stage.

Total Audio Promo pack (£59) gets your next release to:
- 200 UK radio stations (BBC Introducing, community, online)
- 400 independent playlist curators (no botted playlists)
- 50 music blogs

Artists like [real artist name] got BBC 6 Music support from this pack.

Reply if you want the full list.

Chris
Total Audio Promo
info@totalaudiopromo.com
```

### Example (fail — generic, no specific observation, wrong voice)

```
Hi there,

We help independent artists get radio play and playlist placements using our 
AI-powered platform. Our advanced targeting algorithm connects your music with 
the right curators and stations.

Special offer: £59 for our premium pack (limited time).

Book a call to learn more.

Best,
Total Audio Promo Team
```

## Metric that matters

**Qualified replies + pipeline**, not messages sent.

5 personalised messages to artists who paid £5k for a plugger beats 100 templated messages to bedroom producers.

## Evidence (Liberty recap as proof, not sales)

Liberty plugger recaps (e.g., "artist X got 15 radio plays") are evidence for Chris's growth-os memory, **not** copy-paste sales material for artist-facing outreach.

Use the pattern ("artists like [name] got BBC 6 Music support") but don't quote Liberty directly in cold outreach.
