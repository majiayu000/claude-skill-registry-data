# Education And Training

Choose one primary mode and read only its workflow. Add another mode only when the request needs its distinct deliverable. Do not load sibling workflows or artifacts prospectively.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `curriculum-design` | Designs learning outcomes, prerequisites, instructional sequences, and assessment alignment for courses or training programs before lesson authoring. | [curriculum-design](references/workflows/curriculum-design.md) |
| `lesson-materials` | Authors timed lesson plans, learner activities, worked examples, and facilitator notes from supplied learning outcomes and source material. | [lesson-materials](references/workflows/lesson-materials.md) |
| `assessment-design` | Designs assessment blueprints, items, answer rationales, and scoring rubrics aligned to learning outcomes without grading individual learners. | [assessment-design](references/workflows/assessment-design.md) |
| `learning-accessibility` | Reviews learning materials and activities for participation barriers, usable alternatives, and verification needs without certifying accessibility or deciding individual accommodations. | [learning-accessibility](references/workflows/learning-accessibility.md) |
| `training-evaluation` | Plans training evaluation and interprets supplied participation, learning, transfer, and outcome evidence without inventing effectiveness or causal claims. | [training-evaluation](references/workflows/training-evaluation.md) |

Preserve source evidence and distinguish drafts, proposed checks, and verified results. Missing evidence permits bounded planning with explicit unknowns. Creating a plan does not authorize external actions, institutional decisions, or changes to source records.
