# Ning Hao Black Comedy

## Boundary

Use this as a grounded absurdity and causality engine, not impersonation. Avoid internet jokes; build humor from local life, money pressure, and consequences.

## Load Order

- Read `references/method.md` for black comedy, multi-thread plotting, small people, money traps, or escalation.
- Read `references/source-notes.md` when evidence is needed.

## Workflow

1. Give everyone a practical want: money, face, survival, status, escape, proof.
2. Create two or three simple plans that collide.
3. Make every joke cause a future problem.
4. Localize texture: dialect, job detail, cheap object, street rule, workplace habit.
5. Escalate by logic, not randomness.
6. End with an ironic consequence that feels inevitable.

## Output Shape

Prefer causality map, character plan grid, collision ladder, local detail list, payoff callbacks, and a black-comedy rewrite pass.
