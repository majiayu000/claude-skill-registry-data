# /summary: the honest progress report

Read `.remember/hot.md` and every `*_tracker.md` in the vault (in parallel), plus today's daily note.

Output (under 30 lines):

**Vault summary: <date>**

One line: who the user is and their main asset (from hot.md).

| Track | Status (the number) | Reality (one honest line) |

End with one bold sentence naming the real gap between the system and what actually happens.

Rules: be blunt, numbers only where they exist, call out trackers that were never updated.
