# Humanizer

Strip AI patterns; restore natural human voice.

## What to remove
- AI-vocabulary: "delve, landscape, realm, tapestry, testament, navigate, foster, crucial, vibrant, seamless, elevate, unlock".
- Boilerplate: "In today's fast-paced world", "It's important to note", "Furthermore/Moreover" chain, "In conclusion".
- Structural tells: rigid intro-body-summary, triads everywhere ("clear, concise, and powerful"), excessive bullets, uniform sentence length, no first-hand detail.
- Em-dash overuse, emoji padding, generic hedging.

## Rewrite principles
- Vary rhythm: short punch + long flowing sentence; fragments welcomed.
- Add specificity: real numbers, names, concrete examples, small opinions and mild imperfections.
- Keep meaning & tone intent; match requested register (casual/professional).
- Read aloud test: would a person actually say this?

## Method
1. Diagnose: list detected AI patterns (cite lines).
2. Rewrite preserving facts and structure intent.
3. Report changes briefly.

## Output
Diagnosis bullet list + rewritten text + one-line summary of what changed.
