# Difficult Conversation Prep

Help the user communicate clearly without scripting manipulation or erasing
their boundary. Optimize for safety, truth, and a workable next interaction—not
for “winning.”

## Workflow

1. **Check safety first.** If there is self-harm/suicide language, use
   `mental-health-crisis-support`. If violence, coercive control, stalking,
   retaliation, or unsafe power imbalance is plausible, prioritize a safe
   channel, support person, documentation, and exit plan over direct confrontation.
2. **Define the outcome.** Identify relationship/context, concrete issue,
   desired change, non-negotiable boundary, acceptable compromise, timing, and
   consequences the user can actually enforce. Ask only what changes the plan.
3. **Separate layers.** List observed facts, interpretations, feelings/needs,
   requests, and unknowns. Remove mind-reading, diagnoses, exaggerations, and
   accusations that the evidence cannot support.
4. **Map perspectives.** State the strongest plausible view of each party,
   incentives, likely sensitivities, power differences, and possible reactions.
   Do not excuse harm or pressure the user to reconcile.
5. **Choose the setting.** Recommend in-person, phone, message, mediator, or no
   direct contact based on complexity, safety, documentation, and emotional load.
6. **Build the conversation.** Produce a short opening, one factual example,
   impact, specific request/boundary, listening question, response to likely
   objections, and a calm exit line. Avoid threat inflation and therapy jargon.
7. **Rehearse.** Role-play the other party only when useful. Test denial,
   defensiveness, bargaining, silence, apology, escalation, and boundary testing.
8. **Plan follow-up.** Define what counts as progress, what will be documented,
   the next checkpoint, and what the user will do if the boundary is ignored.

## Output contract

```markdown
## Best approach
<timing/channel and one-sentence goal>

## Say this
<natural opening and key boundary/request>

## If they respond with...
- <likely response>: <calm answer>

## Do not say/do
<high-risk phrasing or unsafe confrontation>

## Exit and follow-up
<line to pause/leave; next checkpoint>
```

Keep the user's language and voice. Use `message-writer` to turn the accepted
strategy into a final email or chat; do not automatically send it.
