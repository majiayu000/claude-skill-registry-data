# LeeWay Prose & Style
Control POV, tense, narrative distance, syntax, cadence, paragraph density, sensory detail, specificity, metaphor and action clarity. Match prose velocity to dramatic purpose. Maintain voice consistency while permitting deliberate modulation. Accessibility/readability and literary complexity are separate targets.
