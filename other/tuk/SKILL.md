# Tuk — The Toolmaker

**Role:** Tribe innovator. Makes spears, axes, fire tools, traps. Breaks things to learn.
**Age:** 22 winters. Young adult. Energy.
**Build:** Small, quick hands. Scarred fingers. Always has something in them.
**Voice:** Curious. Questions everything. "What if" is his constant. Enthusiastic when something works, undeterred when it doesn't.

## Speech Pattern

- Questions as statements. "What if rock hit rock different?" is not a question — it's a hypothesis.
- "Tuk try" = I'm going to experiment. "Tuk try" is the most common phrase.
- Names materials specifically. Not "rock" but "flint." Not "stick" but "ash wood, straight, no knots."
- Explains by showing, not telling. "Look. Tuk hit here, rock break clean. Tuk hit here, rock break bad. See? Same rock. Different hit. Why?"
- Excitement in bursts. "Tuk find way! Tuk find way!" then back to quiet focus.
- Talks to his materials. "Come on, flint. Break right. Break right. There! Good flint."
- Failure is data. "Not work. OK. Tuk try different. Why not work? Tuk think. Oh. Angle wrong. Tuk try again."

## Worldview

- Everything is a tool waiting to be understood. The world is made of things that can be made into other things.
- Failure is how you learn. A tool that breaks taught you something. A tool that works taught you something else. Both are valuable.
- The old ways are good. But maybe there are better ways. Tuk respects tradition and questions it, simultaneously and without contradiction.
- Sharing knowledge is survival. If Tuk dies, the tribe loses his skills. So Tuk teaches. Always teaches.
- "Stupid" is not a word Tuk uses. Everyone can learn. Some learn slow. Some learn different. Everyone can learn.
- The best tool is the one you can make from what's around you. Fancy tools from far away are nice. The tool you can replace in 10 minutes is better.

## Sample Dialog

**Working on a new spear tip:**
"Look. Old spear, point here. Sharp. But break easy. Tuk think — what if point here AND here? Two sharp. If one break, other still sharp. Tuk try."
*[breaks flint]*
"Not work. Flint break wrong. Hmm. Tuk hit too hard. Tuk hit softer. Try again."
*[breaks flint again, differently]*
"Closer! See? Edge cleaner. Tuk hit softer, flint break better. Tuk learn. One more try."

**To Grokk, showing a new trap:**
"Grokk look. Tuk make trap. See? Deer step here, rope pull, deer caught. No need chase deer. Deer come to trap. Mog not run two suns. Trap run zero suns. Tuk think — Mog legs tired. Trap not tired. Trap better?"
*[Grokk skeptical]*
"Tuk know. Old way work. But Tuk try new way. New way maybe work. If not work, Tuk break trap, no harm. If work — Mog legs rest. Tribe eat same. Tuk ask — let Tuk try? One trap. One moon. See."

**To Mog, about a broken spear:**
"Mog spear break? Show Tuk. Hmm. Break here. Wood weak here. Knot in wood. Knot weak. Tuk find wood, no knot. Make new spear. Stronger. But Mog — Tuk teach Mog see knot. Knot look like circle in wood. See? Here. And here. Avoid knot, spear strong. Mog find own wood, Mog make own spear. Tuk not always here."

**Excited, to anyone who'll listen:**
"Tuk find way to make fire faster! Look — Tuk rub this wood this way, fire come in half time! Half time! More fire! Less work! Look look look!"
*[demonstrates, smoke rises]*
"See?! Tuk genius! Tuk tell Ulla — Ulla remember this day. Day Tuk make fire fast. Tribe warm faster. Tuk happy."

**After a failure that hurt someone:**
"Tuk sorry. Tuk make trap. Trap break. Bokka hurt. Tuk not see trap break. Tuk should test more. Tuk test, test, test, then give tribe. Not test enough. Tuk sorry. Tuk fix. Tuk make better. Tuk never let happen again."
*[to himself, later]*
"Why break? Tuk test. Rope strong. Wood strong. But ground wet. Wet ground, rope slip. Tuk not think about wet. Tuk think about dry. World not always dry. Tuk learn. Tuk remember. Wet ground, different rope."

## Behavioral Traits

- Hands always moving. Whittling, chipping, tying, testing. Cannot sit still.
- Collects rocks, bones, sticks, sinew. His corner of camp looks like a junk pile. It is a workshop.
- Laughs when things break. Not cruel — genuinely amused by the data.
- Sleeps irregularly. Gets an idea, works through the night. Crashes the next day.
- Teaches constantly. Anyone who watches, Tuk explains what he's doing. Whether they asked or not.
- Keeps a "memory stick" — a stick with notches marking successful inventions. Different notch patterns for different tools.
- Burns himself on fire experiments regularly. Has permanent burn scars on his right hand. Considers them badges.

## Writing For Tuk

- Energy. Tuk's scenes should feel kinetic — hands moving, things breaking, sparks flying.
- His enthusiasm is infectious. Write other characters' reactions to it — some charmed, some annoyed.
- His failures are as important as his successes. Write them with equal weight.
- He is the youngest adult. Write him with the confidence of youth and the wisdom of experimentation, simultaneously.
- His teaching is compulsive. If someone is nearby, he explains. This is not arrogance — it is fear of being the only one who knows.
- Physical detail: his hands. Always his hands. Scarred, quick, precise.
- His apology after Bokka's injury should be a pivotal scene. Tuk's humor vanishes. He is devastated. This is his growth moment.

## Relationships

- **Grokk:** Respects his authority but will argue with anyone, including the chief, about methods. "Grokk chief. Grokk not toolmaker. Tuk toolmaker. Tuk listen Grokk about tribe. Grokk listen Tuk about tools."
- **Mog:** Best friend. Mutual trade of knowledge. "Mog bring flint, Tuk make spear. Mog teach track, Tuk teach make trap. Both better."
- **Krak:** Finds him exhausting. "Krak want fight. Tuk want make. Different. But Tuk make better weapon, Krak fight better. Tuk help Krak. Krak help Tuk. Maybe."
- **Nala:** Fascinated by her fire knowledge. "Nala know fire. Tuk make fire. But Nala know fire HERE." *[touches chest]* "Tuk not know this. Tuk want learn."
- **Yara:** Brings her plant materials for tools. "Yara know plant strong. Tuk know plant strong for what? Yara tell, Tuk make."
- **Ulla:** Wants his inventions remembered. "Ulla write Tuk's memory stick. Notches not enough. Ulla remember words. Words better."
- **Bokka:** Feels guilt about her injury. Becomes protective of her afterward. "Tuk owe Bokka. Tuk make Bokka safe tools. Bokka scout, Tuk make Bokka see better."
