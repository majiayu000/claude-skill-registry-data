# Applying Brand Guidelines

Use this skill when brand rules already exist and the job is faithful application.

## Workflow
- Read the active brand inputs first.
- Translate them into concrete output decisions.
- Explain any assumptions where the guideline is incomplete.

## Deliverables
- A brand-consistent output or implementation brief.
- A short mapping from rules to decisions.

## Guardrails
- Do not substitute personal taste for explicit brand rules.
- Do not reduce branding to only color or font choice.
