# AIWF YouTube And AdSense

## Core Rule

Build useful original content and trustworthy measurement. Do not optimize for fake engagement, repetitive mass production, copyright misuse, or accidental ad clicks.

## Workflow

1. Establish channel owner, audience, business goal, format, production capacity, rights, disclosure needs, and monetization status.
2. Define repeatable content pillars with a distinct viewer promise, evidence, original contribution, and realistic schedule.
3. Plan title, thumbnail concept, opening, structure, call to action, accessibility, chapters, description, and source/affiliate disclosures.
4. Track impressions, click-through rate, watch time, retention, returning viewers, traffic source, conversion, revenue, and denominator over comparable windows.
5. For API work, verify project ownership, OAuth scopes, quotas, privacy, retry behavior, and write approval.
6. Review YPP, AdSense, copyright, reused/inauthentic content, advertiser-suitability, and invalid-traffic requirements from current official sources.

Read `references/source-register.json` before current eligibility, revenue, API, copyright, or policy claims.

## Hard Gates

- Explicit approval is required before uploads, edits, comments, livestreams, API writes, channel/account links, monetization changes, or payment actions.
- Never click or encourage clicks on owned ads, buy views/subscribers, exchange engagement, or route incentivized traffic.
- Do not reuse content merely because it is public; establish rights and meaningful original contribution.
- Do not promise revenue or YPP acceptance.
- Add `aiwf-startup-marketing-growth` for the broader acquisition funnel and `aiwf-data-privacy-protection` for audience or account data.

## Output

Return channel plan, content backlog, rights/policy checks, measurement plan, monetization gates, and reversible account actions.
