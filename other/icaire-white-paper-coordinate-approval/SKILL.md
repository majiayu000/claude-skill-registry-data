# ICAIRE White Paper: Coordinate Approval

Prepare the human approval step for an ICAIRE white paper so leadership can
make a clear decision before drafting continues.

## Contract

- Keep scope to ICAIRE white papers. Scientific papers and academic publication
  approval workflows are out of scope until separately defined.
- Treat approval as human-owned. The skill prepares the package, questions,
  options, and follow-up record; it does not approve on leadership's behalf.
- Use BigBrain or the ICAIRE MCP connector for current stakeholders, prior
  decisions, meeting context, relevant initiatives, and known sensitivities.
- Keep criteria, approval template, required approvers, citation standards,
  Arabic review requirements, grammar checks, and design standards as explicit
  placeholders when not supplied.
- Separate confirmed facts, proposed positioning, missing inputs, and decision
  requests.

## Workflow

1. Identify the approval moment.
   - Record the proposal, requester, expected approvers, deadline, meeting or
     review channel, and decision required.
2. Gather context.
   - Read the proposal, source material, relevant meeting notes, initiative
     pages, and stakeholder context.
   - Retrieve ICAIRE context when approver roles, sensitivities, or prior
     decisions are unclear.
3. Build the approval package.
   - Summarize the proposed white paper, rationale, scope, out-of-scope items,
     audience, expected output, resource needs, risks, and unresolved questions.
   - Include placeholders for Israa's criteria, template checks, citation style,
     Arabic support, grammar review, and design standards.
4. Prepare decision options.
   - Offer practical options such as approve, approve with conditions, revise
     and resubmit, defer, or reject.
   - State the implication of each option and what follow-up work it triggers.
5. Capture next actions.
   - Draft follow-up notes, owner assignments, open questions, and timeline
     entries for the brain when the user asks for persistence.

## Output

Default to:

- `Approval Summary`
- `Decision Needed`
- `Recommended Option`
- `Decision Options`
- `Approval Criteria Placeholder`
- `Risks And Sensitivities`
- `Open Questions`
- `Follow-Up Actions`
- `Status Note`

## Guardrails

- Do not mark a paper approved unless leadership approval is explicit in source
  material or the user states it.
- Do not invent approvers, authority, dates, conditions, or decision outcomes.
- Do not route scientific-paper publication decisions through this white-paper
  approval skill.
- Do not hide missing criteria; make them visible in the approval package.
