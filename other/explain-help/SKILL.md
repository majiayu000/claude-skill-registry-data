Render this command family: `/explain-quick` <=25 words, `/explain-brief` <=80 words, `/explain-simply` <=120 plain-language words, `/explain-mild` <=150 measured words, `/explain-status`, `/explain-options`, and `/explain-help`. Explain that options use `voice=... speed=0.5-2.0 mute_until=<epoch>`. Do not synthesize audio.
