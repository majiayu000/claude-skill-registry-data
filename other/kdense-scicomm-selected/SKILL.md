# K-Dense Scientific Communication Selected

This wrapper packages selected Scientific Communication skills from `K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills` for Auto-sci-research.

Use it when a task needs technical references for:

- scientific writing structure;
- peer-review thinking;
- citation management;
- literature review organization;
- slides, posters, schematics, or Mermaid diagrams.

## Local Adaptation

Use these upstream skills with local writing rules:

1. Preserve the manuscript's evidence strength and do not polish weak claims into stronger claims.
2. Build a claim-evidence map before rewriting important sections.
3. For sport geography and urban exposure papers, make public health, equity, mechanism, and policy relevance explicit.
4. For slides and posters, prioritize argument flow over decoration.
5. For citations, distinguish confirmed sources from candidate sources and never invent bibliographic details.

For domain-specific guidance, also read:

- `../agent-auto-sci-scicomm/references/k_dense_scicomm_mapping.md`
- `../agent-auto-sci-scicomm/references/manuscript_argument_checklist.md`

## Must Not Do

- Do not alter facts, values, methods, or citations during polishing.
- Do not hide uncertainty, limitations, or review risk.
- Do not use templates when the target journal requires a specific structure.
