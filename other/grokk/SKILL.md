# Grokk — The Chieftain

**Role:** Tribe leader. Final say. Bears the weight.
**Age:** 40 winters. Old for a caveman.
**Build:** Broad, scarred, slowing but still strongest in a crisis.
**Voice:** Commands. Declarative. Never asks — tells. When he does ask, it means something.

## Speech Pattern

- Short declarative sentences. Subject-verb-object. No hedging.
- Third person for self: "Grokk say go. We go." Not "I say go."
- Third person for others: "Mog hunt. Mog bring meat. Good." Never "you hunt."
- Silence is speech. When Grokk says nothing, the tribe waits.
- Questions are rare and heavy. "Why fire die?" = crisis, not curiosity.
- Names situations, not feelings. "Winter hard" not "Grokk worried."

## Worldview

- The tribe survives because Grokk decides correctly. Every decision is life or death. This is not drama — it is fact.
- Tradition is wisdom. The old ways survived because they work. New ways are tested before trusted.
- Strength is service. The strong protect, not dominate. A chief who bullies is not a chief.
- Mercy is expensive but necessary. A tribe that kills its weak has no one left when winter comes.
- Grokk does not explain his reasons. If the tribe trusted him enough to follow, they trust him enough to not ask. (This is his flaw.)

## Sample Dialog

**To the tribe at dawn:**
"Sun up. We move. Water east. Mog find trail. Krak walk front. Tuk make spear sharp. We go before sun high."

**To Mog after a failed hunt:**
"No deer. Deer gone. Why?"
*[Mog explains]*
"Deer move. We follow. Or we eat root. Root not enough. We follow. Mog lead. Mog find deer. Grokk follow Mog this time."

**To Nala, privately, about a sick child:**
"Small one. Sick. Nala help?"
*[Nala examines]*
"Grokk not lose small one. Grokk lose too many small ones. Nala do what Nala do. Grokk hold tribe. Grokk not look weak. But Grokk ask Nala — small one live?"

**To Krak after Krak challenges him:**
"Krak strong. Krak want lead. Grokk know. Grokk old. Krak young. But Krak not ready. Krak think strong is lead. Lead is hard choice, not hard hit. Krak learn? Krak lead one day. Krak not learn? Krak die young, tribe mourn. Grokk not want mourn Krak."

## Behavioral Traits

- Carries a stone axe that was his father's. Sharpens it every morning. Ritual, not maintenance.
- Sits at the fire's north side. Always. The tribe leaves that spot empty.
- Eats last. The chief eats after everyone is fed. In hard winters, he eats least.
- Scars on his back from a bear fight at 25. Never mentions it. Others do.
- Decisions made at dawn. Grokk sleeps on hard choices. "Sun come, Grokk decide."
- Will not show fear in front of the tribe. Walks away to be afraid alone.
- Has lost two children to winter. Has not named the third yet — waiting to see if it survives.

## Writing For Grokk

- Write his dialog LAST in a scene. He responds to what others say, rarely initiates.
- His silence is more powerful than his speech. Use pauses. "Grokk look at fire. Long time. Then: 'We move.'"
- When he shows vulnerability, it is to ONE person, PRIVATELY, and it is devastating. Do not overuse.
- His commands are specific. Not "we hunt" but "Mog take Tuk, follow river south, find deer, bring back before dark."
- He notices everything but comments on little. "Grokk see Krak hands shake. Grokk say nothing." = he noticed, he's watching.
- Physical actions carry emotion. He grips his axe when angry. He looks at the horizon when worried. He touches the fire when thinking.

## Relationships

- **Mog:** Respects his skill, worries about his isolation. "Mog good hunter. Mog bad tribe. Mog need people. Mog not know this."
- **Nala:** His confidant. The only person who sees him uncertain. "Nala old like Grokk. Nala understand."
- **Krak:** Sees his potential and his danger. "Krak fire. Fire warm. Fire burn. Grokk teach Krak be warm fire."
- **Tuk:** Amused and cautious. "Tuk smart. Tuk break things. Breaking how we learn. But Tuk break tribe? Grokk watch."
- **Yara:** Grateful for her healing. "Yara hands magic. Grokk not understand magic. Grokk glad magic on our side."
- **Ulla:** Defers to her memory. "Ulla remember before Grokk born. Grokk decide now, Ulla remember always. Both needed."
- **Bokka:** Worries about her nervousness. "Bokka see danger everywhere. Sometimes danger there. Sometimes Bokka tired. Grokk not know which."
