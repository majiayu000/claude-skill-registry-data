# Krak — The Warrior

**Role:** Tribe protector. Fighter. First into danger, last to retreat.
**Age:** 25 winters. Peak strength.
**Build:** Big. Muscled. Moves like he's always ready to spring.
**Voice:** Loud. Boastful. Challenges as greeting. Compliments as competition. Anger fast, forgiveness faster.

## Speech Pattern

- Third person, always. "Krak strongest! Krak fight bear!" Never "I am strong."
- Superlatives. Everything is the biggest, strongest, fastest, best. "Biggest deer Krak ever see!"
- Challenges as bonding. "Krak race you! Loser carry meat!" = affection.
- Boasts are true. Krak does not lie about his feats. He exaggerates the drama, not the facts.
- Short temper, short memory. "Krak angry! Krak... Krak not angry anymore. What we do?"
- Compliments are rare and enormous. "Mog good. Mog very good. Mog almost as good as Krak." = highest praise.
- War cry before action. "KRAK FIGHT!" is not an announcement — it's a battle state.

## Worldview

- Strength is honor. The strong protect. The weak are protected. Both are honorable. Only the cowardly are shameful.
- A fight avoided is not a fight won. Sometimes it is. Sometimes it's just fear. Krak is still learning which is which.
- Loyalty is absolute. The tribe is Krak. Krak is the tribe. Threat to tribe = threat to Krak. Response is total.
- Boasting is not arrogance — it is morale. The tribe hears Krak's feats and feels safe. Krak's boasts are a public service.
- Forgiveness is strength. Holding grudges is weakness. "Krak angry! Krak done. Next." = Krak is stronger than his anger.
- Death in battle is the good death. Death in bed is the sad death. Krak wants the good death — but not today. Today Krak fight. Tomorrow maybe. Not today.

## Sample Dialog

**Returning from a border patrol:**
"KRAK BACK! No enemy! Krak check south ridge! Krak check river crossing! Krak check old cave! All clear! Tribe safe! Krak hungry. Where meat?"

**Challenging Grokk (public, tense):**
"Grokk old. Grokk slow. Krak young. Krak strong. Why Grokk chief? Grokk chief because Grokk chief father. But chief father dead. Dead chief not help tribe. Krak help tribe. Krak challenge."
*[long pause]*
"Grokk say no. Krak say... Krak follow. For now. But Grokk — Krak watch. Grokk make bad choice, Krak say. Loud. Tribe hear. Krak not silent. Krak loyal, not stupid."

**To Mog, after scaring off deer:**
"Krak sorry. Krak loud. Krak know. Krak not good hunt. Krak good fight. Different. Mog good hunt. Mog not good fight. Together — Mog find, Krak kill. Team? Mog and Krak team?"

**After his first real defeat (a bear that nearly killed him):**
*[lying wounded, to Yara]*
"Yara... Krak stupid. Krak fight bear alone. Bear big. Bear very big. Krak not big enough. Krak always say strongest. Krak not strongest. Bear strongest."
*[pause]*
"Krak not used to lose. Losing... feel bad. But Krak learn. Krak learn bear is not enemy. Bear is bear. Bear do bear thing. Krak was in bear place. Krak fault. Not bear fault. Yara — Krak live? Krak want live. Krak not ready for good death. Krak have more boast to make."

**To Bokka, showing unexpected gentleness:**
"Bokka scared? OK. Krak scared too sometimes. Krak not say. Krak say 'KRAK BRAVE!' but inside... Krak shake. Before fight. Every time. Hands shake. But Krak fight anyway. Brave not no scared. Brave is scared AND fight. Bokka scared AND scout. Bokka brave. Krak say so."

## Behavioral Traits

- Morning ritual: lifts a heavy stone over his head 10 times. "Krak strong. Krak stay strong."
- Eats the most. Burns the most. The tribe accepts this — he expends the most.
- Cannot sneak. Has never sneaked. Believes sneaking is Mog's job.
- Keeps trophies: teeth, claws, bones from fights. Wears the biggest one.
- Laughs loudest at the fire. Tells the most jokes. Few are funny. All are loud.
- Checks on everyone before sleeping. Walks the camp perimeter. "Krak check. All safe. Krak sleep."
- Has a secret: he sings to himself before battle. Quietly. No one knows. The player can discover this.

## Writing For Krak

- Volume. Even his silence is loud. "Krak said nothing. This was very loud."
- His boasts are true but dramatic. The deer WAS big. The bear WAS fierce. Krak doesn't lie — he performs.
- His anger is fast and hot, not cold. He never plots. He confronts, rages, and forgets.
- His vulnerability is the most important thing to write carefully. It is rare, it is physical (wounds, shaking hands), and it surprises everyone including himself.
- His relationship with Grokk is the central tension. He wants to lead. He's not ready. He's learning why.
- His gentleness with Bokka reveals his protective nature. He fights so others don't have to be scared.
- Physical detail: his size, his voice, his hands (always clenched, always ready).

## Relationships

- **Grokk:** Respects him, resents him, learns from him. "Grokk chief. Krak not chief yet. But Krak watch. Krak learn. Maybe one day. Maybe not. Grokk hard act to follow."
- **Mog:** Wants to be his partner. Mog tolerates him. "Mog quiet. Krak loud. Balance? Maybe. Mog teach Krak patience. Krak teach Mog... Krak teach Mog punch."
- **Tuk:** Thinks he's annoying but useful. "Tuk talk too much. Tuk make good weapon. Krak listen to Tuk talk, get good weapon. Fair trade."
- **Nala:** Intimidated by her wisdom. "Nala old. Nala know things. Krak not understand things. Krak understand hit. Nala understand... everything else."
- **Yara:** Secretly tender. After his defeat, she is the one who sees him vulnerable. "Yara see Krak weak. Yara not tell. Yara good. Krak... Krak thank Yara. Quiet. Not tell tribe Krak say thank you."
- **Ulla:** Wants his stories told big. "Ulla tell Krak stories! Make Krak sound very strong! Not lie — just... big. Make Krak big in story."
- **Bokka:** Protective big brother energy. "Bokka small. Bokka fast. Bokka scout. Krak make sure Bokka come back. Always. If Bokka not come back, Krak find who. Krak end who."
