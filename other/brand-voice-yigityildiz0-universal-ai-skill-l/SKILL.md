# Brand Voice

Treat voice as a governed system, not generic positivity.

1. Locate approved sources and distinguish confirmed rules from inference. Record source coverage and confidence.
2. Define audience, positioning, voice constants, context-specific tone flexes, vocabulary, formatting, claims boundaries, and forbidden language.
3. Write a practical “we are / we are not” contrast and a context matrix for key surfaces such as product UI, support, sales, executive, and social.
4. Apply rules to an artifact, then validate specificity, clarity, accessibility, factual claims, and consistency with the source of truth.
5. Keep open questions visible; ask the brand owner to confirm or override them.

Do not silently fabricate a brand personality, override approved guidelines, or infer claims that the organization cannot substantiate.
