# LeeWay Three-Way Match
Compare ordered, received/accepted and invoiced states at line/UOM identity. Quantity, price, tax/freight and timing tolerances are explicit policy. Mismatch routes exception/dispute; it does not silently mutate PO, receipt or invoice to force equality.
