# Reels Performance Report

Determine which creative patterns deserve more production and which only generate shallow reach.

## Workflow

1. Establish account, exact dates, posting cadence, sample size, and available metrics.
2. Use exports/connected insights/screenshots/database cohorts. Do not assume every account exposes the same current Instagram metrics.
3. Normalize content metadata when available:
   - format/franchise
   - topic/service
   - hook type
   - duration
   - real shop footage vs generated/stock
   - voiceover/narrator
   - structurePatternId or equivalent creative cohort
   - CTA, caption, audio, posting time
4. Analyze distribution and retention:
   - plays/reach where available
   - early retention or skip proxies
   - average watch time and completion proxies
   - replays when supported
5. Analyze value signals:
   - sends/shares
   - saves
   - comments with intent
   - profile visits
   - follows/follower conversion
   - local/non-follower reach where available
   - website/call/lead outcomes when joinable
6. Compare cohorts, not isolated viral posts. Require enough observations before declaring a format repeatable.
7. Identify false wins: high reach with weak retention/value signals, paid/reposted anomalies, or one-off topic spikes that do not generalize.
8. For Nick's, prioritize useful local automotive education and memorable brand-building that can plausibly increase shop demand, not generic national views alone.
9. Read `references/report-contract.md` and follow its output structure.

## Rules

- Do not infer watch-time metrics that Instagram did not provide.
- Use medians and per-reach/per-play rates when large outliers distort averages.
- Separate creative effect from topic, distribution, posting time, and sample-size confounds.
- Do not call a format proven from one or two posts.
