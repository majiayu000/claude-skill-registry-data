# Public-Source Research

Choose one primary mode and read only its workflow. Add modes only for separable analyses the request actually requires.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `osint-research-planning` | Bound an investigation, collection lanes, source rules, privacy, evidence, and handoffs. | [OSINT research planning](references/workflows/osint-research-planning.md) |
| `public-records-research` | Search official records with jurisdiction, identity, procedural-status, and privacy checks. | [Public-records research](references/workflows/public-records-research.md) |
| `geolocation-chronolocation-analysis` | Estimate public media location or capture time from visible and lawfully available evidence. | [Geolocation and chronolocation](references/workflows/geolocation-chronolocation-analysis.md) |
| `public-social-network-analysis` | Analyze aggregate public-platform behavior, amplification, or coordination signals. | [Public-network analysis](references/workflows/public-social-network-analysis.md) |
| `misinformation-risk-analysis` | Assess claim accuracy, provenance, media context, spread, harm, or correction strategy. | [Misinformation-risk analysis](references/workflows/misinformation-risk-analysis.md) |

Do not load sibling workflows or artifacts prospectively. Stop on credentials, access-control bypass, doxxing, stalking, private-home or sensitive-facility targeting, harassment, or disproportionate personal-data collection.
