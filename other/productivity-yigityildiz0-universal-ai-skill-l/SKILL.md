# Productivity

Use this skill when the user wants operational leverage for personal or team productivity.

## Workflow
- Clarify the recurring friction.
- Prefer skimmable outputs and reusable structures.
- Route to briefing, email, or note-processing skills as needed.

## Deliverables
- A compact productivity artifact or routine.
- A short next-action list.

## Guardrails
- Do not create a system heavier than the problem.
- Do not bury urgent items.
