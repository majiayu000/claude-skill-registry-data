# LeeWay Shot Design & Coverage
Every shot declares narrative/editorial purpose. Design coverage from scene beats and likely edit points, not a random shot list. Track master/coverage, singles, reactions, inserts, transitions, screen direction and continuity. More coverage is not automatically better; sufficient intentional coverage is the target.
