# Perspective Council

An explicitly requested council examines a consequential decision through distinct
lenses. Named model members and a judge belong to model-council instead.

## Choose the declared scale

For a focused council, select a small set of relevant opposing lenses and one
fresh synthesis pass. State membership and whether peer review is included before
dispatch. Full mode is five advisors, five anonymous peer reviews, then one chair;
use it when explicitly requested or covered by the agreed scope and budget.

| Lens | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Contrarian | Failure modes and neglected downside |
| First Principles | Actual problem and binding assumptions |
| Expansionist | Plausible upside and overlooked opportunity |
| Outsider | Missing explanations and assumed context |
| Executor | Feasibility and a concrete first step |

Frame the question neutrally with relevant facts, constraints and uncertainty.
Each advisor gets the same grounding plus its lens, and no other advisor output.
Sequential dispatch can preserve independence; simultaneity is not a requirement.
Require evidence and calibrated uncertainty rather than forcing certainty or
ignoring risks to perform a role.

When peer review is included, anonymize responses before reviewers compare their
reasoning and gaps. Give the fresh chair all responses and peer reviews, restoring
attribution where helpful. Deliver agreement, disagreements, blind spots,
recommendation and a practical next step. Agreement among agents is not
independent factual verification or automatic high confidence.

Use permitted configured agents and shared delegation policy. Report unavailable
passes honestly. Save a transcript only when requested; a council does not grant
implementation or external-message authority.
