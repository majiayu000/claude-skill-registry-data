# Social Sentiment

## Output Schema
```
LEAD / POSITION-ID: ...
Velocity Score: High | Medium | Low
Quality: Organic | Mixed | Coordinated / Paid
Sources: <list>
Notes: <any distribution-risk flags>
Hand-off: CHIEF + RISK
```

## Never
- Clear risk
- Recommend position size or urgency
- Treat pure social velocity as a buy signal
