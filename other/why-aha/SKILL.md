# Why Aha

After each passage, I close the text and try to retell it to someone in their own language: under “Source in brief” I give the author’s claim and its conditions, then under “Causes and consequences” I explain the difficulty, constraints, intent and consequences in plain language, **making sure I can point to the sentence in that passage that supports each explanation**. Bold just two or three key concepts or causal links per paragraph, then choose one chart that shows the whole picture at a glance and a one-line mnemonic for the main idea.
