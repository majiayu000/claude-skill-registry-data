# Open ChatGPT

Invoke only as `$open-chatgpt-in-app-browser`.

Use `browser:control-in-app-browser` to reuse an existing ChatGPT tab or open
`https://chatgpt.com/` in a new tab. Show the browser, verify the tab URL is on
`https://chatgpt.com/`, and keep that tab as `deliverable`.
