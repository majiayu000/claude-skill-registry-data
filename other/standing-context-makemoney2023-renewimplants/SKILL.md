# Standing Context

Reusable **standing context** for org seats: frameworks and playbooks that apply across ventures (not brand/SKU locks).

## Hard rules

1. Read the **child pack** `SKILL.md` listed on your seat before using its config/frameworks.
2. Apply frameworks to the **active venture** facts in `business-idea/` — do not invent product claims.
3. Standing context produces **Craft deliverables** (Layer A) unless a production pack also applies.
4. Never reintroduce excluded brand kits (product configs, personas, vendor-specific call pathways).

## Child packs

| Pack | Use for |
|------|---------|
| `buying-psychology/` | Ecommerce/funnel buying psychology (Cialdini, Fogg, means-end, etc.) |
| `content-persuasion/` | Content persuasion playbook (AIDA/PAS, style, templates) |
| `ai-detection-writing/` | Human-voice / anti-detection writing strategy |
| `humor-craft/` | Brand-safe sarcasm idioms + sketch comedy structure research |
| `sales-youtube-frameworks/` | Sales training frameworks + YouTube analyses (enablement/outbound) |

## Artifact class

Default: **Craft deliverable (Layer A)** under seat Outputs. Sales frameworks inform `07-sales-playbook.md` and channel scripts.
