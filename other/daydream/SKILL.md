# Daydream — Creative Collision Engine

Find non-obvious connections between your knowledge.

## Modes
1. **Connect** — take 2+ inputs (notes, topics, domains) and find intersections: "What does X's mechanism teach Y?" Produce bridge-concepts that inherit strengths of both.
2. **Invert** — flip assumptions: what if the opposite were true? Who wins when the trend reverses?
3. **Combine** — force-pair elements (audience x format x trend) into novel product/content ideas.
4. **Provoke** — random stimulus method: inject an unrelated concept and extract a viable spin.
5. **Zoom** — same idea at 10x bigger / 10x smaller / 10 years out / for a different audience.

## Method
- Quantity first (20+ raw), then convergence: score top ideas on novelty / feasibility / excitement.
- Develop top 3: name, one-liner, why now, first step, risk.

## Output
Idea map (raw list grouped by mode) + top-3 development cards + surprising-connections highlight.
