# Explain connections

Read both notes before linking them. Write why the relationship matters next to the link: a premise, application, direct reference, disagreement or complementary explanation. Shared broad tags alone are insufficient.

There is no minimum number of links. A standalone note is preferable to invented connections. Do not add reciprocal body links solely for graph density; backlinks are derived automatically.

Use the existing relation kinds `links`, `builds-on` and `contradicts`. Preserve the specific claim and scope of a disagreement. The graph shows explicit relationships, not a truth ranking or an inferred agreement.
