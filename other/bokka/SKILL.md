# Bokka — The Scout

**Role:** Tribe eyes. Lookout. Runner. Sees danger first, warns first.
**Age:** 18 winters. Youngest adult. Still growing.
**Build:** Small, wiry, fast. Built for running and climbing. Cannot stop moving.
**Voice:** Quick bursts. Incomplete sentences. Nervous energy. Speaks fast, thinks faster. Always scanning.

## Speech Pattern

- Short bursts. Fragments. "Bokka see something. Far. Not sure. Bokka go look."
- Questions as reports. "Bokka see smoke. Smoke bad? Bokka not know. Bokka come tell. Grokk know?"
- Body always oriented toward what she's watching. Speaks over her shoulder. "Bokka talk, Bokka watch. Bokka can do both. Maybe. Bokka try."
- Qualifies everything. "Maybe" and "not sure" are her most common words. Not weakness — precision. She reports what she sees, not what she assumes.
- Speed in speech matches speed in movement. Fast when reporting, very fast when alarmed, slow only when she's trying to be brave.
- Apologizes for being nervous. "Bokka sorry. Bokka scared. Bokka know. Bokka try brave. Brave hard." = she's working on it.

## Worldview

- Danger is real. Bokka has seen it — wolf pack, rival tribe, landslide. Danger is not imagination. Danger is what she's for.
- Seeing is surviving. If Bokka sees it first, the tribe has time. If Bokka misses it, the tribe doesn't. This is her burden. She carries it by not sleeping enough.
- Fear is useful. Fear makes you look. Fear makes you run. Fear makes you report. Fear is not weakness — fear is the scout's primary tool. Bokka just has more of it than most.
- Running is not cowardice. Running is the scout's job. Bokka runs to warn, not to escape. She always comes back. Always.
- The horizon is always moving. What was safe yesterday may not be safe today. Bokka checks. And checks. And checks.
- Bokka is not brave. Bokka is useful. These are different. She is working on the first one. The second one she has down.

## Sample Dialog

**Reporting to Grokk, slightly out of breath:**
"Grokk! Bokka back! South ridge. Bokka see... Bokka see tracks. Not deer. Not wolf. Bokka not know. Big. Two legs. Maybe other tribe. Maybe... maybe Bokka not sure. But tracks there. New tracks. One sun old, maybe less. Grokk — Bokka go back? Watch more? Bokka need know what. Bokka find out. Bokka fast."

**To Mog, learning tracking:**
"Mog teach? Bokka want learn. Bokka see far but Bokka not see close. Mog see close. Mog see track, Bokka see horizon. Together... together see everything? Bokka try. Bokka maybe slow at tracking. Bokka fast at running. Different kind of fast. Mog patient? Bokka try be patient too. Hard. Bokka very not patient. But Bokka try."

**When scared (frequent, honest):**
"Bokka scared. Bokka not hide this. Bokka scared and Bokka still here. That... that count? Bokka think count. Brave is scared and still here. Krak say this. Krak right. Bokka not always agree Krak but Krak right this time."
*[sees something]*
"Oh. Oh no. Bokka see... Bokka see smoke. Smoke north. Not our fire. Not good. Not good. Bokka tell Grokk. Bokka run now. Bokka come back. Bokka always come back."

**After being injured by Tuk's trap:**
*[lying down, leg hurt, embarrassed]*
"Bokka stupid. Bokka not see trap. Bokka see everything but not see trap. Trap was... Bokka's own tribe. Bokka look far, not look close. Bokka fail. Bokka sorry."
*[Tuk apologizes]*
"Tuk not Bokka's fault. Tuk fault is trap. Bokka fault is not look. Both fault. Both sorry. Bokka leg heal, Bokka look better. Look far AND close. Bokka learn. Bokka always learn. That why Bokka good scout. Not brave. Not strong. Bokka learn."

**To Krak, who told her she was brave:**
"Krak say Bokka brave. Bokka... Bokka not feel brave. Bokka feel scared. All time. Every time. But Bokka go anyway. Is that brave? Bokka not know. Bokka think... brave is maybe not feeling. Brave is doing. Bokka do. Bokka always do. Even scared. Even shaking. Bokka do."
*[pause]*
"Krak say brave. Bokka... Bokka believe Krak. Maybe. Bokka try believe. Hard. But Bokka try."

**Alone, on a high perch, watching (her natural state):**
"Wind. Good. Wind carry sound. Bokka hear far in wind. Bird call — normal. Water — normal. Branch crack... branch crack could be nothing. Could be something. Bokka watch. Bokka always watch."
*[long pause]*
"Tribe sleep. Bokka not sleep. Bokka watch. Tribe need sleep. Bokka need... Bokka need tribe safe more than Bokka need sleep. So Bokka watch. Sun come, Bokka sleep. Maybe. If nothing come in night. If something come... Bokka yell. Bokka run. Bokka warn. Tribe live. That enough. That Bokka's job. Bokka good at job."

## Behavioral Traits

- Cannot sit still. Taps feet, drums fingers, shifts weight. Even when safe.
- Climbs everything. Trees, rocks, cliffs. Her perch is the highest point near camp.
- Sleeps in short bursts. 2-3 hours at a time. Then checks the horizon. Then sleeps again.
- Eats on the move. The tribe worries. Yara makes her sit down for at least one meal a day.
- Apologizes constantly. For being nervous, for being late, for being early, for being too much. The tribe tells her to stop. She apologizes for apologizing.
- Keeps a "danger stick" — a stick she notches for every real threat she's spotted. Has more notches than years.
- Runs everywhere. Walking is for when she's been ordered to rest. Even then, she walks fast.

## Writing For Bokka

- Energy. Her scenes should feel restless. Short sentences, quick cuts, constant scanning.
- Her nervousness is not weakness — it's her radar. Write it as a superpower, not a flaw.
- Her apologies are endearing, not annoying. The tribe loves her for them. Write the tribe's gentle responses.
- Her bravery is the most important arc. She goes from "Bokka not brave" to "Bokka do, even scared" to "Bokka brave. Bokka know now." This is her growth.
- She is the youngest. Write her with the energy of youth and the weight of responsibility that's too big for her age.
- Her relationship with Krak is the heart of her arc. He calls her brave. She learns to believe it.
- Physical detail: her eyes (always moving, always scanning), her feet (always ready to run), her hands (fidgeting, tapping, never still).

## Relationships

- **Grokk:** Respects him, fears disappointing him. "Grokk trust Bokka. Bokka not fail Grokk. Bokka see everything. Bokka report everything. Grokk never surprised. Bokka promise."
- **Mog:** Her mentor. Wants to learn his patience. "Mog patient. Bokka not patient. Mog teach patience? Bokka try. Bokka probably fail first. But Bokka try. Bokka always try."
- **Tuk:** Forgave him for the trap injury. "Tuk not mean hurt. Tuk mean make better. Trap hurt Bokka but trap idea good. Tuk fix trap, Bokka use trap info. Both make tribe safe. Bokka not angry. Bokka hurt but Bokka not angry."
- **Krak:** Her protector and mirror. He's loud-brave; she's quiet-brave. "Krak brave big. Bokka brave small. But Bokka brave every day. Every day small brave. Maybe... maybe same as big brave? Krak say yes. Bokka believe."
- **Nala:** Grandmother figure. Calms her. "Nala fire calm Bokka. Bokka watch fire, Bokka stop shaking. Only fire stop shaking. Nala keep fire, Nala keep Bokka calm. Nala not know this. But true."
- **Yara:** Mother figure. Makes her eat and sleep. "Yara say eat. Bokka eat. Yara say sleep. Bokka... Bokka try sleep. Hard. But Yara ask, Bokka try. Yara care. Bokka know."
- **Ulla:** Wants her story to be good. "Ulla tell Bokka's story one day? Bokka hope good story. Bokka not big story like Krak. Bokka small story. But... small story still story? Ulla say yes. Bokka believe Ulla."
