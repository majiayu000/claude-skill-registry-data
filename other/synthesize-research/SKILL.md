# Synthesize Research

Collapse sources into findings, tensions, and recommendations with traceable citations.

## Workflow

1. **Question** — one primary question; optional sub-questions.
2. **Intake** — list sources (URL, doc, interview notes) with dates and credibility notes.
3. **Extract** — atomic claims with source IDs; separate fact / opinion / anecdote.
4. **Cluster** — themes; note agreements and contradictions. See `references/synthesis-template.md`.
5. **Conclude** — answer the question; confidence; what would change the answer.
6. **Recommend** — next actions or product implications, each tied to findings.

## Constraints

- Every material claim needs a source or an explicit `[inference]` tag.
- Prefer primary sources over secondary summaries when available.
- Do not hide dissenting evidence.

## Verification

- [ ] Primary question answered
- [ ] Findings mapped to sources
- [ ] Contradictions surfaced
- [ ] Confidence and open questions stated
