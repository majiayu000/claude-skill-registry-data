# LeeWay 3D Terrain & Geospatial

Preserve coordinate reference system, units, vertical datum and origin strategy when real geography matters. For fictional terrain, declare scale and generation seed. Build/ingest heightfields/meshes, roads, water and biome layers; tile/LOD/stream large worlds. Never invent surveyed/geographic accuracy from artistic terrain.
