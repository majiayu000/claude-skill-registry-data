# /opps: the opportunity pipeline

Read `personal/opportunities.md` and `personal/applications.md`, then render the FULL table (not a top 3):

`Priority | Program | Role | Opens | Deadline | Fit | Eligibility | Status | Notes`

Buckets, in order: Urgent (deadline within 7 days), Apply now, Watch (opens later), Pending (submitted), Missed/Ineligible (kept at the bottom, never deleted).
Within a bucket: deadline, then fit, then prestige. Target roles come from hot.md.

Then a "This week's moves" block, 3 to 5 bullets.

## Before anything goes in Urgent / Apply now / Watch
- Check today's date. Expired deadlines go to Missed.
- Verify eligibility against hot.md: work authorization, location, graduation year, institute or demographic filters. If unsure, read the official page first.

## Updating
New opportunity: program, role, deadline, source, fit 1-5, eligibility, notes.
Application stages: Applied, OA, Interview, Offer/Reject. Flag anything with no update in 2+ weeks.
