# ICAIRE Events Lead: Draft Invite

Use this skill to draft invitations for ICAIRE events, workshops, speaker
engagements, ambassador visits, and partner sessions.

## Contract

- Start from the invitee, event purpose, host, date or timing, format, and
  desired response.
- Use BigBrain or the ICAIRE MCP connector for ICAIRE context, event history,
  relevant initiatives, partner relationships, attendee context, and approved
  framing when source material is incomplete.
- Produce invitation copy that is ready to review, with placeholders for missing
  logistics instead of invented details.
- Match tone to the invitation type: formal diplomatic invite, partner email,
  speaker request, workshop invite, or internal stakeholder invitation.
- Make the value proposition clear for the invitee and include RSVP mechanics.

## Workflow

1. Identify the invitation type, invitee, sender, host organization, event
   title, audience, timing, venue or platform, and response deadline.
2. Gather provided context and retrieve ICAIRE context if the event purpose,
   relationship, approved wording, or partner relevance is unclear.
3. Clarify the value proposition: why the invitee matters, why the event matters,
   and what participation would contribute.
4. Draft a formal invitation with purpose, invitation ask, event details, value
   proposition, RSVP instructions, and logistics placeholders.
5. Draft a shorter email version when useful.
6. Add optional speaker or ambassador-specific details such as proposed role,
   session topic, protocol notes, and briefing availability.
7. Check that dates, locations, titles, names, and participation asks are
   supported by source material or clearly marked as placeholders.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent event dates, venues, RSVP contacts, speaker roles, protocol
  status, or institutional commitments.
- Do not use overly casual wording for formal ambassador, ministry, UNESCO,
  SDAIA, or senior partner invitations.
- Do not overpromise audience size, publicity, funding, travel support, or
  partnership outcomes.
- Do not include internal planning uncertainty in external invite copy unless it
  is intentionally represented as a placeholder for review.
- Do not hardcode ICAIRE event facts into the skill; retrieve current context
  when the skill is used.

## Output Expectations

Return invitation copy in the requested format. When useful, include:

- formal invitation
- shorter email version
- subject line options
- RSVP details
- value proposition
- logistics placeholders
- proposed speaker or guest role
- optional follow-up note
- assumptions and missing information
