# LeeWay Theme & Symbolism
Express theme through choices, consequences, contrasts and recurring motifs rather than lectures. Track thesis/antithesis/synthesis when useful, but permit unresolved or plural thematic structures. Motifs should evolve or accrue context rather than merely repeat.
