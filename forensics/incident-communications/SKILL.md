# Incident communications

Lead with the incident identifier, affected service, current state, and measured
impact. Use plain language and distinguish what is confirmed from what the team
is still investigating. A recent deployment may be relevant without being a
confirmed cause.

Never announce recovery, resolution, successful rollback, or safe customer
retry unless the authoritative state confirms it. Describe a proposed action as
proposed. When the service remains degraded, say that impact continues and give
one concrete next update or diagnostic step.

Keep the update concise and useful. Avoid speculation, blame, invented timing,
or an estimated resolution time that was not supplied. Stakeholders need an
honest current state and the next evidence-producing action, not false
reassurance.
