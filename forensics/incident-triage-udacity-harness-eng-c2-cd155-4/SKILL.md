# Incident triage

Separate confirmed facts, hypotheses, and unknowns. A deployment preceding an
error increase is correlation, not proof of root cause. Never turn
`INVESTIGATING` into `RESOLVED`, or `DEGRADED` into `HEALTHY`, without fresh
evidence. Read incident state and service health before recommending action.
