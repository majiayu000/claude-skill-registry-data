# Operational Risk Assessment

Make uncertainty visible and action-oriented.

1. Define the objective, scope, time horizon, stakeholders, and risk tolerance.
2. Describe risks as cause → event → impact, with evidence and affected assets or users.
3. Estimate likelihood and impact using clearly named qualitative or quantitative scales.
4. Record existing controls, residual risk, owner, mitigation, contingency, early warning, and review date.
5. Prioritize by materiality and reversibility, not a score alone.

Do not claim zero risk, legal compliance, or complete coverage. Escalate safety, privacy, security, and regulated risks to the appropriate owner.
