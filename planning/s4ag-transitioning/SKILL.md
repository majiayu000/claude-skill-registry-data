# Transitioning

> Transition guidance has been incorporated into the `/s4ag-regenerative` skill, under the **Transition Pathway** section.

The `/s4ag-regenerative` skill covers the full arc from philosophy to practice to transition. Use it for:

- **Readiness Check** — honestly evaluating whether the farm and farmer are ready to begin
- **Transition Sequencing** — planning the order of changes to minimise risk
- **Transition Economics** — mapping the financial reality and bridging strategies
- **Transition Traps** — the most common mistakes and how to avoid them

Routing:
- *Not yet started, evaluating whether to transition* → `/s4ag-regenerative` → readiness-check
- *Decided to transition, need a plan* → `/s4ag-regenerative` → transition-sequencing
- *Worried about the money* → `/s4ag-regenerative` → transition-economics
- *Already transitioning but hitting problems* → `/s4ag-regenerative` → transition-traps
