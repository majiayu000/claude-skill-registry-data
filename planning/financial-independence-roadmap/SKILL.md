## Требования к результату

Produces an educational read on your rough FI number (and why the savings rate matters more than income), the timeline math at different savings rates, the levers and lifestyle tradeoffs, the different flavors (lean/coast/full), and the traps — turning a vague dream of freedom into a directional plan.
