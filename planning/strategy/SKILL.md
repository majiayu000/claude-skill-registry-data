# Genre Skill: Strategy

Planning-driven maps: defenses, units or points, an economy, and waves or opponents that push back.

## Map types (use as the variant)

- **tower-defense**: waves of enemies on a fixed route against placed defenses
- **wave-defense**: co-op waves around one base or objective
- **base-control**: capture and hold points with an economy
