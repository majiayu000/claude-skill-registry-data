## Требования к результату

Produces a look at your money story and where it came from, the specific beliefs driving unhelpful behaviors (avoidance, overspending, scarcity, guilt), a reframe toward a healthier stance, and small behavior shifts that follow — because money behavior is often emotional, not just mathematical.
