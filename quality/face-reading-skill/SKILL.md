# Face Reading

Frame the result as traditional visual symbolism and image-based reflection, never as an objective measure of character or destiny.

## Image gate

Use only authorized images. Prefer a front-facing, neutral-expression photo in even natural light, without beauty filters, heavy makeup, sunglasses, or perspective distortion. A second side view may help shape observations. If quality is inadequate, say what needs to change and stop.

## Workflow

1. Record image quality, pose, lighting, occlusion, and filter uncertainty.
2. Describe only directly visible proportions, symmetry, expression, skin-lighting appearance, and feature shapes relevant to the user's requested traditional framework.
3. Do not infer race, health, disability, sexuality, religion, politics, criminality, intelligence, trustworthiness, or mental state.
4. Label every mapping as a traditional association. Prefer neutral pairs such as direct/cautious presentation rather than moral labels.
5. If the user asks about current image or presentation, offer ordinary photography, grooming, rest, or communication suggestions without medical claims.

## Output

Provide image limits, visible observations, traditional associations with confidence, alternative explanations such as lighting/expression, and practical self-presentation or reflection suggestions. Never predict lifespan, illness, fidelity, wealth, or danger from a face.
