# Demo audit

Use this skill after capture or when a demo needs an independent quality verdict. Compare the actual video and audio with the approved story and current plan. The person or agent that wrote the narration cannot be the sole reviewer.

Sample the screen and speech at regular intervals and around every active action and scene boundary. Inspect the final third separately for pacing and action density. Check product truth, full-frame legibility, spoken coverage, captions, cursor continuity, transitions, accessibility, and privacy.

## Verdict

Report concrete evidence and a location for every finding. A missing recording, unavailable audio, stale approval, or unverified transition is inconclusive—not a pass. Escalate P0/P1 findings for operator review and stop publication until resolved. Do not invent a passing result when evidence is absent. A render that clears the media audit waits for review; it is not ready to publish. Mirror the audit's automated checks in a silent rehearsal so they fail before voice time is spent.

Read [the audit contract](references/audit-contract.md) for sampling, severity, bounded repair, and the final report. Before calling a video ready for a course platform or public channel, run [the delivery checks](references/delivery-checks.md) for phone-size legibility, a muted watch, loudness, caption reading limits, flashes, length, and sidecar captions, transcript, and chapters. Return findings to the earliest input that owns them; rerun the affected downstream checks after repair.
