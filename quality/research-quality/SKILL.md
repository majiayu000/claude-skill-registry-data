# Research Quality

Choose one primary mode and read only its workflow. Add another mode only when the requested review genuinely spans it.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `academic-literature-review` | Search, screen, grade, and synthesize scholarly evidence and gaps. | [Academic literature review](references/workflows/academic-literature-review.md) |
| `research-methods-review` | Review design, sampling, measurement, validity, ethics, analysis, or reproducibility. | [Research-methods review](references/workflows/research-methods-review.md) |
| `citation-integrity-review` | Check claim support, quotations, provenance, bibliographic identity, and correction needs. | [Citation-integrity review](references/workflows/citation-integrity-review.md) |

Do not load sibling workflows or artifacts prospectively. Never invent sources, hide inaccessible evidence, overstate certainty, or substitute this work for required peer, statistical, ethics, or owner review.
