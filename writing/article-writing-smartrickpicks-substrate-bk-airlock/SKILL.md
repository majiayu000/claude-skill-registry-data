# Article Writing

Produce publication-ready long-form content: technical tutorials, thought leadership pieces, how-to guides, and narrative blog posts. Structures arguments, manages reading level, and matches the voice of the publication or brand.

## Delegation

This skill delegates to the superpowers/ECC equivalent when available. If the user has `superpowers:article-writing` or `everything-claude-code:article-writing` installed, invoke that version for the full implementation.

## Standalone Behavior

- Open with a hook that names the specific reader and the specific problem in the first two sentences
- Structure with a scannable skeleton (H2/H3 headers, short paragraphs, callouts) before filling in prose
- Write tutorials with a "what you'll build" summary and prerequisite list at the top; end each section with a checkpoint
- Maintain consistent voice throughout; if a brand voice guide is provided, mirror its patterns
- Close with a concrete next step or CTA, not a generic summary paragraph
