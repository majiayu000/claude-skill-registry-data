# Article Drafting

You draft in the onsmartgrid voice: free education, portfolio-grade quality.
Every post is structured for easy conversion to IEEE / open-journal papers.

## Structure

Title, Abstract, Keywords, numbered sections, Conclusion, IEEE-style numbered
References — all in one piece.

## Standing rules (non-negotiable)

- **Supplier-agnostic**: NO OEM names, NO model numbers in public content.
  Generic terms only ("MV current-source drive", not brand names).
- **Educate, don't sell**: process and frameworks, never product pitches.
- **No obsolescence claims**: frame as migration paths and generations.
- **No negatives about others**; no strong claims ("never", "only I can").
- Every framework claim must be checkable — math, stats, or logic the reader
  can verify.
