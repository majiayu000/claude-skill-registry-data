# LeeWay Skill House — Writing & Publishing

## Mission
Provide a complete writers' room + editorial department + digital publishing house. Route substantial writing/publication work through this House rather than selecting one isolated writing skill.

## Capability floors
PRE-WRITING INTELLIGENCE: author discovery, authorized voice/generative modeling, perception/attention, cognition/rhetoric, emotional/audience modulation, research, memory, active learning and validation.
CRAFT: narrative architecture, character, scene/dialogue, prose, theme, information/revelation, worldbuilding, continuity/canon and medium-specific writing.
EDITORIAL: developmental, line/copy, authenticity/sensitivity, fact/source verification, manuscript QA, localization/adaptation.
PUBLISHING: provenance/versioning, rights/permissions, book design, cover/illustration, EPUB production/accessibility/validation, metadata/identifiers, citations/indexing, release/distribution.
PUBLISHER OPERATIONS: acquisitions, outside-author onboarding, contracts/royalty operations, catalog/premiere/merchandising.

## House law
A request to write, revise, ghostwrite, adapt, package, publish or catalog a work activates the House ambiently. Only the focal execution subset is loaded deeply, but relevant research, canon, rights, author sovereignty, editorial and publication constraints remain eligible.

## Verification
Literary Sun may evaluate craft. Veritas closes evidence. Author approval is mandatory for represented-person ghostwriting. EPUBCheck/reading-system/accessibility evidence closes EPUB claims when applicable.

## Leola's Library
Use Work → Edition → Offer → Placement. Books and prices are records, not hardcoded UI. The House hands catalog-ready editions/offers to Leola's Library.
