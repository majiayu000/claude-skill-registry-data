# AEO blog resources

Start with `README.md`, then `docs/aeo-workflow.md`. Configure `site.config.json` with verified owner information. Use `docs/article-template.md` for content and `docs/ranking-checklist.md` for publication checks.

Reuse the included engine. Research actual questions before choosing pages, work from authorized source material, and distinguish facts from assumptions. Do not invent experience, testimonials, prices, or outcomes. Use a specific page for each distinct search intent and useful links between related pages.

Build with `python execution/build_site.py`. Check rendered text, links, visible FAQs, structured data, and draft/future exclusion. Obtain authorization before paid research or publishing. Do not treat a score or llms.txt as evidence of traffic or citations. Never send mail or submit visitor information as part of a test without permission.
