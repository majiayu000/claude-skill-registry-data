# Review 14 Introduction Writer

## Purpose

Write a restrained, citation-supported introduction that narrows from field context to the review question.

## Required Inputs

- Manuscript outline.
- Evidence matrix.
- Gap/topic report.
- Citation library.

## Output

Create or update `12_manuscript/01_introduction.md` with:

- Broad clinical/biological background.
- Specific unresolved issue.
- Why a review is needed now.
- Review aim and scope.
- Citation placeholders or verified citation keys.

## Completion Gate

Pass only when each paragraph has a clear role and every factual claim has citation support or a citation-needed marker.

## Stop Rules

Avoid exaggerated phrases such as "groundbreaking", "completely unknown", or "first comprehensive" unless verified.
