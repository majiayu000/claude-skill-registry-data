# Email Sequences

Use this skill when working on email sequences tasks.

## Workflow

- Define audience, trigger, promise, cadence, exit conditions, and measurable outcome before drafting.
- Keep each email focused on one job with clear subject, preview text, body, CTA, and suppression logic.
- Check consent, unsubscribe, segmentation, deliverability, and brand voice before launch.
