# Common Room

Use this skill when the task is about interpreting relationship or engagement signals over time.

## Workflow
- Clarify who is being monitored and what signal matters.
- Group signals into intent, risk, or opportunity.
- Route to research or messaging skills when needed.

## Deliverables
- A signal summary or account brief.
- Recommended follow-up actions.

## Guardrails
- Do not mistake weak activity for strong intent.
- Do not invent certainty from sparse signals.
