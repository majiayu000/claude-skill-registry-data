# Brand Voice

Use this skill when the task is about tone systems, messaging consistency, or editorial voice.

## Workflow
- Clarify audience, tone boundaries, and forbidden language.
- Route toward the most relevant brand and copy sub-skill.
- Keep voice rules concrete and reusable.

## Deliverables
- A voice-aligned output or messaging brief.
- A short set of voice rules if needed.

## Guardrails
- Do not confuse tone with generic positivity.
- Do not override explicit brand rules.
