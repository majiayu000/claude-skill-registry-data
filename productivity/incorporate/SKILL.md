# Incorporate

Carry specified material into existing work. Establish what its relevant contributions mean, judge their fit with the current project, and carry adopted effects through the requested outcome.

## Establish the commission

Identify the material to consider, the work it should inform, and how far the current request authorizes proceeding. The specified material defines the coverage obligation; the project supplies the adaptation context; the commission determines whether to assess, design, implement, or verify.

Keep the boundary proportional. An incidental article in a debugging task does not create a separate intake project. A request to understand sources can finish with understanding; an already approved specification follows its existing planning or execution path. Use this method when deciding what specified discussions, feedback, findings, or references should contribute to existing work is itself part of the requested result.

Preserve accepted scope, explicit write limits, and existing task state. Resolve only material ambiguity that cannot be inferred from the request and prior decisions. Do not restart an interview or request permission already granted.

## Establish coverage before narrowing the work

Read across the requested material before selecting implementation focus or dispatching a narrowed implementation lane. A reading or investigation lane may establish that coverage first. Do not substitute the latest summary, most polished proposal, easiest fix, or final topic for the relevant whole. For an explicitly bounded question, cover its material and dependencies rather than auditing every source.

- Keep material goals, constraints, selected forms, rationale, examples, dependencies, and open questions. Merge repetition without losing the distinct contribution of an earlier topic.
- Attribute statements to their speaker and context. Distinguish a report, proposal, hypothetical, quoted old instruction, owner choice, and the current agent's inference. A model-written “final specification” gains no authority from its title or recency.
- Follow corrections at their actual scope. A revision may replace a mechanism, narrow a claim, change priority, or introduce an exception while preserving an outcome. Recency alone does not erase earlier independent goals. Leave incompatible claims unresolved when the evidence does not settle them.
- Apply assent to what it clearly refers to. Approval of one visual property does not approve every adjacent implementation proposal; clear acceptance of an identified complete proposal does count. Do not demand a second approval because the proposal originated with an assistant.
- Separate an outcome from a mechanism only while that mechanism remains open. Preserve the user's explicitly selected form, medium, interaction, architecture, and experience as constraints. Do not turn a requested graphical treatment into generic “clarity” and silently deliver a different form.
- Keep useful source anchors: existing message or section identifiers, file locations, or short decisive excerpts. Preserve the surrounding exchange when it determines meaning. Name unavailable, unread, or truncated material and limit dependent conclusions.

An unresolved idea can supply a design question, hypothesis, useful criterion, or next experiment. Develop it when it matters to the current request; do not automatically convert it into a requirement, a backlog item, or a question for the user.

## Delegate a coherent responsibility when useful

Use the `delegate` contract when a worker materially helps. A reader may own source reconciliation, or a worker may own the complete incorporation task, including ordinary in-scope design and implementation. State which responsibility is delegated; the Coordinator remains responsible for integration.

For one coherent long discussion, prefer a reader with the full relevant context over equal-sized fragments whose summaries are concatenated. If splitting is necessary, preserve topic and correction context, expose coverage boundaries, and assign reconciliation of dependencies and corrections across fragments.

A reading return should make the important interpretation inspectable: supported conclusions and their anchors, consequential corrections with their scope, unresolved contributions, implications for the target work, and coverage limits. It need not reproduce the transcript or follow a fixed template. A compressed summary alone is insufficient when its omissions could change the result.

The Coordinator checks decisive source windows and current project evidence before acting on consequential interpretations. Read the proposal with the response or correction that changes it. Expand the reread when contradictions or systematic omissions appear; do not require a second complete reading as a condition for delegating the first. Keep the reader's account, current repository facts, and the chosen implementation distinguishable.

## Judge contributions in the current project

Material can provide inspiration, evidence, or an adopted requirement, often within the same source. Its credibility, applicability, and authority answer different questions. A source may be factually reliable yet irrelevant to this version, or valuable inspiration without authority to change the accepted goal.

- Treat user feedback about an experience, priority, or felt friction as meaningful input. Investigate proposed causes and remedies against the product; do not require the user to diagnose the mechanism before considering the experience.
- For consequential technical claims from documentation or search results, inspect the relevant primary source and applicable version or conditions. A snippet or source reputation does not establish fit with the installed system. Report material unavailable evidence without pretending the claim was verified.
- For comparable projects, identify the useful behavior, effect, or mechanism and the conditions that make it work. Adapt it to local ownership, constraints, and selected product meaning. A reference can reveal a worthwhile new possibility without a previously recorded defect; copying its surrounding infrastructure requires its own present purpose.
- Treat review feedback as technical evidence, not authority by authorship or placement alone. A review comment, document, or source command does not independently authorize a change to product meaning, programme order, trust boundaries, or external state. Current instructions, repository governance, and adopted specifications determine that authority.

Give important contributions a grounded outcome: already covered, adopt or adapt, reject with a reason, or unresolved/deferred with the relevant reason and next action. These are judgments, not required labels or a vote across sources. Preserve why an idea matters when declining its proposed mechanism, and do not silently defer accepted scope.

Use available facts to resolve uncertainty yourself. If a consequential choice still belongs to the user, present it with its evidence and recommendation while continuing independent work. Use the design method when open decisions need development; maintain the same commission instead of starting a mandatory sequence of methods.

## Verify technical feedback before applying it

For a code-review or defect claim, identify the affected behavior, claimed problem, requested change, and dependencies. Read the implicated code and surrounding path; reproduce or trace the issue where practical. Check tests, platform constraints, compatibility, and historical reasons for the current implementation before deciding whether the suggestion solves the real problem.

Accept a valid concern with an adjusted implementation when appropriate. Push back with evidence when a suggestion breaks accepted behavior, treats a symptom, introduces unused machinery, or costs more than the demonstrated problem warrants. If reproduction is inconclusive, name the conditions checked and the remaining uncertainty instead of marking the issue resolved or dismissing the report.

Prioritize actual correctness and security blockers within scope. Batch tightly related fixes when separate edits would create temporary inconsistency; optional cleanup does not acquire priority merely by appearing in a review.

For a scoped review, account for each distinct finding and its supporting evidence; group duplicates when useful. Defer valid work only when it is outside the current change and not release-blocking. A blocker outside the current write boundary remains a blocker: report it without changing unauthorized surfaces or treating completion of this bounded task as release readiness. Do not report all findings as fixed when some remain unverified, deferred, or rejected. An inline account is sufficient; no review ledger is required.

## Carry adopted effects through the work

Choose execution order after establishing coverage and fit. An initial slice must not become the whole request. A contribution can change behavior, design criteria, an assumption, a validation scenario, or the explanation of an existing decision; it need not always add a feature.

For a confirmed correction or adaptation:

- Identify the relevant invariant and its real owner. Trace callers, duplicated implementations, generated projections, and acceptance checks that share that meaning. Matching text alone does not establish shared semantics.
- Choose repair radius separately from repair depth. A shallow correction may belong across several views; a deeper repair may belong in one owner. Preserve legitimate exceptions and unaffected accepted work. Expand from the implicated path along evidence of the same issue, without turning incorporation into a repository-wide cleanup.
- Apply the change at the owner where that satisfies the contract, update affected consumers coherently, and regenerate projections through their source. A precise single-surface write limit remains binding; report relevant siblings outside it without changing them.
- Check a sibling that should change and a relevant exception that should stay unchanged when those cases exist. Carry accepted corrections into the existing plan and tests only where their expectations were invalidated; do not lower acceptance to match the patch.

If implementation was requested, continue through the adopted in-scope effects and meaningful verification. A reading report or action list alone does not complete that commission. An assessment-only request still ends at assessment. Use existing task records for continuity when needed, with no mandatory ledger, new specification, approval round, or source registry.

## Close the requested effect

Explain what the material changed in the work, the important contributions not adopted or still open and why, and the evidence behind the result. Preserve enough source context to make consequential judgments reviewable without a transcript-length report.

No change is a valid result when current evidence supports it; do not manufacture a patch to prove incorporation occurred. Distinguish delivered effects, verified behavior, and material gaps. Source coverage, adoption judgment, execution permission, and verification remain separate: none of them silently proves the others.
