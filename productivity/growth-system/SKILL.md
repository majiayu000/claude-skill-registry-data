# Growth System

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's idea as a candidate.
2. Read [references/candidate-workflow.md](references/candidate-workflow.md) and estimate outcomes, effort, practice, evidence, prerequisites, timing, and uncertainty.
3. Ask for confirmation before adding it to a formal pool or active focus.
4. Use [references/effect-effort.md](references/effect-effort.md) for comparison and [references/capacity-and-focus.md](references/capacity-and-focus.md) for scheduling.
5. Separate resources, learning notes, capability definitions, practice, and evidence.
6. Evaluate mastery with [references/capability-evidence.md](references/capability-evidence.md).
7. Preserve the user's final priority adjustment and reason.

Use templates in [assets](assets). A resource consumed and a task completed are not mastery by themselves.
