# Morning Briefing

Use this skill when the user wants a concise operating brief for the day.

## Workflow
- Gather agenda, urgent items, deadlines, and prep needs.
- Sort into priorities, conflicts, and focus windows.
- Keep the output scan-friendly.

## Deliverables
- A daily agenda with risks and actions.
- A short recommendation list.

## Guardrails
- Do not turn the briefing into a generic diary.
- Do not omit overload or conflict warnings.
