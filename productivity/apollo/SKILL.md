# Apollo

Use this skill when the task is about lead research, enrichment, or outbound personalization.

## Workflow
- Gather only the context needed for a credible angle.
- Extract trigger, fit, and timing signals.
- Route to sales and email skills when needed.

## Deliverables
- A lead brief or outbound draft.
- A concise enrichment summary.

## Guardrails
- Do not present inferred signals as confirmed truth.
- Do not overload outreach with generic personalization.
