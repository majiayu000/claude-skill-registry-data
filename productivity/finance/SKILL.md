# Finance

Use this skill when the task is a finance workflow rather than general admin work.

## Workflow
- Classify creation, organization, reconciliation, or reporting.
- Normalize dates, currencies, and categories.
- Route to the most relevant finance sub-skill.

## Deliverables
- A finance-ready document, dataset, or dashboard note.
- A short summary of numbers or risks.

## Guardrails
- Do not mix assumptions and extracted figures silently.
- Do not lose auditability of numbers.
