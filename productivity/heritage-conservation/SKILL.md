# Heritage Conservation

Choose one primary mode by its requested deliverable. Read only that workflow; add another only when the task requires its distinct output. Resolve relative links from the file containing them, including workflow links to `../artifacts/`.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `condition-assessment` | Reviews supplied condition records for objects or historic fabric, separating observations, material identifications, deterioration hypotheses, and inspection limits. | [Conservation Condition Assessment](references/workflows/condition-assessment.md) |
| `preventive-care` | Creates evidence-based preventive-care plans for collections and historic interiors, linking material sensitivities, environmental exposure, handling, storage, and review priorities. | [Preventive Conservation Planning](references/workflows/preventive-care.md) |
| `treatment-proposal-review` | Reviews proposed conservation interventions against condition evidence, significance, material compatibility, prior treatment, alternatives, and documentation requirements. | [Conservation Treatment Proposal Review](references/workflows/treatment-proposal-review.md) |
| `historic-fabric-recording` | Builds traceable records of historic building and site fabric, separating observed configuration, material descriptions, alteration evidence, phase interpretation, and inspection coverage. | [Historic Fabric Recording](references/workflows/historic-fabric-recording.md) |
| `site-conservation-planning` | Develops conservation plans for archaeological places and historic structures linking significance, fabric condition, site pressures, management options, and staged decisions. | [Site Conservation Planning](references/workflows/site-conservation-planning.md) |
| `monitoring-maintenance` | Organizes repeatable condition monitoring and maintenance records for objects and historic places, separating measured change, comparison limits, triggers, and completed work. | [Conservation Monitoring And Maintenance](references/workflows/monitoring-maintenance.md) |

Work from supplied evidence for movable objects, archaeological sites, and historic building fabric. Distinguish observed condition, inferred cause, proposed work, and verified outcomes. Record material, significance, inspection limits, and the applicable custodian or professional decisions. Missing evidence permits a bounded draft or verification plan with explicit unknowns. Preserve originals and do not claim observations, approvals, or checks that were not obtained.
