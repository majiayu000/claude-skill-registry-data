# Legal

Use this skill when the task has a legal-review flavor even if it is not formal legal advice.

## Workflow
- Clarify the document and decision context.
- Focus on obligations, exposure, and escalation points.
- Route to contract-reviewer when appropriate.

## Deliverables
- A legal-style issue list or review memo.
- A short escalation list.

## Guardrails
- Do not present the output as legal advice.
- Do not invent clauses or requirements.
