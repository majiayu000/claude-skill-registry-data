# Textual Scholarship

Choose one primary mode by the requested deliverable. Read only that workflow; add another mode only for a distinct stage or artifact the task needs.

Resolve each link relative to the file containing it. Workflow links to `../artifacts/` refer to this package’s `references/artifacts/` directory.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `witness-description` | Describe the supplied witnesses of a text and their provenance, extent, relations, and inspection limits before transcription or editorial comparison. | [Textual Witness Description](references/workflows/witness-description.md) |
| `documentary-transcription` | Produce a traceable transcription of supplied textual sources with an explicit policy for layout, spelling, abbreviations, corrections, and uncertainty. | [Documentary Transcription](references/workflows/documentary-transcription.md) |
| `variant-collation` | Align supplied witnesses and produce an inspectable record of textual variation under a declared comparison policy. | [Variant Collation](references/workflows/variant-collation.md) |
| `critical-edition-preparation` | Prepare an edited text, apparatus, and editorial account from documented witnesses and explicit editorial choices. | [Critical Edition Preparation](references/workflows/critical-edition-preparation.md) |

Keep source evidence, representations, editorial choices, and conjectures distinct. Preserve original readings and stable locations. Report inspected coverage and uncertainty; do not infer an original text, provenance, or encoding conformance without evidence.
