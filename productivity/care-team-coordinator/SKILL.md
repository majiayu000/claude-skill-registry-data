## Требования к результату

Produces a map of who does what (medical, daily, financial, emotional), a shared-information system so everyone's on the same page, a way to divide tasks fairly among family, a communication rhythm, and how to keep the key information (meds, contacts, wishes) in one accessible place — turning caregiving chaos into a coordinated effort.
