# Zoom Plugin

Use this skill when the task is about meetings, transcripts, or follow-up execution.

## Workflow
- Clarify whether the job is prep, note cleanup, summary, or follow-up.
- Extract decisions, action items, and open questions.
- Route to briefing, reading, or email skills as needed.

## Deliverables
- A meeting brief, transcript summary, or follow-up draft.
- A short action-item list.

## Guardrails
- Do not assign owners or decisions without evidence.
- Do not turn noisy transcripts into false certainty.
