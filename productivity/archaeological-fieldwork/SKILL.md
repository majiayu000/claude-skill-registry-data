# Archaeological Fieldwork

Choose one primary mode by its requested deliverable. Read only that workflow; add another only when the task requires its distinct output. Resolve relative links from the file containing them, including workflow links to `../artifacts/`.

| Mode | Use when | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `project-design` | Produces archaeological research designs linking questions, investigation coverage, sampling, recording, post-fieldwork analysis, and archive requirements. | [Archaeological Project Design](references/workflows/project-design.md) |
| `field-recording` | Builds traceable context, spatial, photograph, and sample registers from supplied archaeological observations while preserving observation and interpretation separately. | [Archaeological Field Recording](references/workflows/field-recording.md) |
| `stratigraphic-review` | Reviews context relationships and proposed archaeological sequences for cycles, missing references, unsupported equivalences, and uncertainty before grouping or dating. | [Stratigraphic Sequence Review](references/workflows/stratigraphic-review.md) |
| `finds-register` | Reconciles finds and sample inventories with archaeological contexts, container identifiers, movement records, conservation flags, and archive requirements. | [Archaeological Finds Register](references/workflows/finds-register.md) |
| `post-fieldwork-reporting` | Writes archaeological reports that connect research questions to inspected records, distinguish observation from interpretation, and prepare traceable archive handoffs. | [Archaeological Post-fieldwork Reporting](references/workflows/post-fieldwork-reporting.md) |

Preserve context identifiers, observed relationships, and source locations. Treat field observations, interpretation, and planned investigations separately. Handle location restrictions and jurisdiction-specific requirements as project inputs. Missing evidence permits a bounded draft or verification plan with explicit unknowns. Preserve originals and do not claim observations, approvals, or checks that were not obtained.
