# Gmail Intelligence

Use this skill when the user needs inbox analysis, thread prioritization, or reply planning.

## Workflow
- Classify threads by urgency and required reply.
- Extract asks, deadlines, and owners.
- Draft replies or summaries that reduce inbox friction.

## Deliverables
- A prioritized inbox view or reply set.
- A task list derived from email commitments.

## Guardrails
- Do not send, archive, or label mail without approval.
- Do not flatten nuance from sensitive threads.
