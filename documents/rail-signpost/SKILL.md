# Railway RAMS and Signalling Assurance Standards: Signpost (pointers only)

**This is a signpost, not a knowledge pack.** It carries **no standards-body content**:
no reproduced clauses, no normative text, no synthesised summaries of the standards.
EN 50126, EN 50128, EN 50129, and EN 50159 are paywalled with no redistribution grant
(Excluded under this repo's `docs/SOURCE-VETTING.md`), so they cannot be reconstituted
into a redistributable pack. What this skill does: tell you which document you want,
what it is for, who owns it, whether a free copy exists, and where to get the authentic one.

## When to use

You are working on railway RAMS, railway control software, or a signalling electronic
safety case and need to identify or cite the governing document: which standard covers
the RAMS process, which covers software, which covers safety-related electronic systems
for signalling, and which free text covers the risk-process need. Paywalled rows route
you to the owner. Free rows route you to the regulation or the ORR guidance. No row
names an in-repo pack.

**Prerequisites:** none, plain Markdown.

## How to use

Find your document below. The **Status** column says whether it can be packaged:

- **Excluded**: paywalled, no redistribution grant; buy from the owner. Cannot be packaged here.
- **Open**: free to obtain. No row in this signpost names an in-repo pack. Railway RAMS, railway software, and signalling electronic safety cases are not packaged in this catalogue.

CENELEC editions were confirmed on the EVS catalogue on 2026-10-14. No 2025 edition of EN 50128 was
found. EN 50129:2018 is withdrawn on EVS from 2026-06-02; EN 50129:2026 is the
incoming edition. Do not quote a date this file does not print.

## Scope & Limits

- No reproduced standards text. Rows carry designation, title, edition, owner, status, and URL only.
- No in-repo pack is the open path. Do not send a reader to `mil-std-882`, `faa-8110-49`, or `nhtsa-vehicle` as a railway equivalent.
- EN 50129 is safety-related electronic systems for signalling. EN 50159 is the communication standard.
- EN 50128 has no 2025 edition in this signpost. EN 50129 lists 2018 and 2026.
- CSM and ORR rows are the free risk-process and UK regulatory framing. They do not replace EN lifecycle, software, or safety-case content.
- RSSB full standards are not free. Catalogue summaries are.
- The IEC dual-number part designations for RAMS were not confirmed and are not cited.
