# Maritime Safety and Class Rules: Signpost (pointers only)

**This is a signpost, not a knowledge pack.** It carries **no instrument text**:
no reproduced clauses, no normative paraphrase, no synthesised summaries of any IMO
instrument or class rule. SOLAS, ISM, ISPS, and COLREGS as sold by IMO are paywalled
sales publications, so they are Excluded under this repo's `docs/SOURCE-VETTING.md`
and cannot be reconstituted into a redistributable pack. Class rules are free to read
on society portals and remain society copyright. What this skill does: tell you which
instrument you want, who sells it, and which free path covers the need. A free path is
not the IMO statutory text. No in-repo pack covers these instruments.

## When to use

You need to identify or cite a maritime safety instrument: which sales publication is
the current SOLAS consolidated text, which free path covers US flag implementation,
which free PDF covers collision rules, which UK notices cover flag guidance, and which
class portal covers hull and machinery practice. IMO sales rows route you to the owner.
US and UK rows route you to a government publisher. Class rows route you to a society
portal. None of those routes is a substitute for the IMO consolidated text, and none
names an in-repo pack.

**Prerequisites:** none, plain Markdown.

## How to use

Find your instrument below. The **Status** column says whether it can be packaged:

- **Excluded**: IMO sales publication or society-copyright class rule. No redistribution grant. Cannot be packaged here. Buy from the owner, or read on the owner's portal where the portal allows reading. Class-portal rows in table 4 are Excluded even though the portal is free to read. Free to read is not a redistribution grant.
- **Open**: free to obtain from the publishing body: a government publisher (US or UK), or the IMO's own free orientation pages. No row in this signpost names an in-repo pack. SOLAS, ISM, ISPS, COLREGS, and class rules are not packaged in this catalogue. An Open row is a pointer to freely readable material. It is not the IMO statutory text.

Sales titles were confirmed on the IMO current-publications list on 2026-10-14. Cite
SOLAS Consolidated Edition 2024 (IH110E), not 2020. Cite COLREGS 2026 Edition (IC904E)
as the sales title. The free collision-rules text is the USCG Navigation Rules PDF, not
the IMO book. Two USCG DCO URLs and the eCFR body were not retrieved by the research
client (HTTP 403 or a bot gate). Those Title cells say so. Do not quote a date this
file does not print. Reuse of IMO sales publications requires Organisation permission
and is subject to royalty agreements: https://www.imo.org/en/publications/Pages/PermissionRequests.aspx .
UK MCA notices default to Open Government Licence v3.0 unless a page says otherwise.
The licence is worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual, and non-exclusive, with the listed
exemptions (personal data, logos, third-party rights), and this pack still points
rather than excerpts: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/ .

A reader who will not buy IMO books goes (1) to flag law and circulars (US eCFR Title
46 plus NVIC and MSC; UK MSN, MGN, and MIN), (2) to the USCG Navigation Rules PDF for
collision rules, (3) to class-society portals for class practice, (4) to IMO free
explainers for definitions and chapter maps only.

## Scope & Limits

- No reproduced instrument text. Rows carry designation, title, edition, owner, status, and URL only.
- No in-repo pack is the open path. Do not send a reader to a NASA, rail, or space pack as a maritime equivalent.
- SOLAS Consolidated Edition 2024 (IH110E) is the paywalled statutory core. The 2020 consolidated edition is not current.
- ISM, ISPS, and the IMO COLREGS book are sales publications. IMO HTML explainers are orientation, not the codes.
- USCG Navigation Rules are the free collision-rules text. They are the US amalgam, not IC904E.
- eCFR Title 46 and USCG circulars are US flag implementation. They are not the SOLAS consolidated text. NVIC and MSC pages returned HTTP 403 to the research client. Re-check in a browser. Do not cite an NVIC number this file does not print.
- UK MCA notices are free under OGL v3.0 unless a page says otherwise. They are UK implementation and operational guidance, not the SOLAS consolidated text. This pack points at the collections. It does not excerpt notices.
- Class rules (DNV, ABS, Bureau Veritas) are free to read on society portals and remain society copyright. They are not public domain, not OGL, and not a SOLAS substitute.
- Lloyd's Register rules URL and download terms were not confirmed. This signpost does not cite an LR rules page and does not claim LR rules are free PDFs.
- No complete official SOLAS regulatory text is free from IMO.
