# Article Cover Adapter

Adapt a locked visual, not its message. Keep the source unchanged and write a separately named derivative.

## Workflow

1. Confirm ownership/permission, source image, exact text, protected artwork and credits, target dimensions, and permitted editing tools. Use [the adaptation brief](assets/adaptation-brief.md).
2. Read [the verification checklist](references/verification-checklist.md). Record protected regions before choosing a crop. Confirm target requirements from the user's brief or current primary documentation.
3. Prefer reframing or extending an existing background over cropping text, logos, credits, or important artwork. Use an available local editor. Do not invent replacement wording or redraw a logo.
4. If preserving the approved artwork requires paid generation, a new service, or external upload, stop for separate approval. Do not imply that an approved adaptation authorizes these actions.
5. Export a new derivative, inspect it at full size and likely display sizes, and check dimensions and file format. Treat [the target profile](assets/target-profile.json) as a fictional example, not a platform specification.
6. For generated imagery requiring metadata cleanup, use a free local, format-aware selective operation. Compare the adapted pre-clean export with its cleaned counterpart, not the differently sized original. Preserve rendering data and unrelated authorship/rights information. Keep source provenance separately.
7. Inspect an actual platform preview only when already available or separately authorized. Otherwise report platform preview pending. Local mockups do not establish platform acceptance or cropping.
8. Hand off the derivative, target profile, preserved-text/credit checklist, cleanup evidence, and unresolved preview items. Publishing requires approval of the exact finished package.

## Standalone checks

No other skill is required. An available local image inspector can verify dimensions, format, orientation, colour profile, alpha and frames; a format-aware metadata inspector can verify targeted fields. Compare decoded pixels before/after metadata-only cleanup and inspect the final rendering. If an inspector is unavailable, mark the affected checks unverified, not passed.

Where installed, media-spec-checker and remove-ai-image-metadata can help with these checks. Neither replaces visual review, rights review, or actual platform preview.

The example 1920 by 1080 canvas and 16:9 ratio are configurable. Do not convert a user's preferred dimensions or safe margins into universal platform rules.
