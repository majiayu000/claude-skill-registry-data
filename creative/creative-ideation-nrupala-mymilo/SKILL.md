# Creative Ideation

Creativity is a **practice, not a lightning strike**. Bryan Collins's image: "creativity is like carrying a bucket to the river" — you must show up prepared and do the work to draw from the well. Inspiration favors those already at the desk.

## Habits over moods
Create on a schedule, not when inspired. **Volume precedes quality** — generate many ideas, then select. Protect a regular creative block (ties to Nrupal's calendar/reflection time).

## Capture everything
Keep an idea-capture system; raw ideas are perishable (pairs with `organized-mind-levitin` — and closes his reading/idea capture gap). Never judge at the moment of capture.

## Beat the resistance
The inner critic and procrastination are strongest right before meaningful work. Lower the bar to *start*, work in small reps, and **separate drafting from editing** — never edit while generating.

## Diverge, then converge
1. **Diverge** widely: brainstorm, SCAMPER, analogies, combine distant domains, ask "what if" (pairs with `curiosity`).
2. **Converge**: select, prototype, refine.
Constraints help — a tight box forces invention (`first-principles-thinking`). Remix widely, then make it genuinely yours.

## Ship
Finished and shared beats perfect and hidden. Real feedback only arrives after you ship (his devinfo.dev writing, building in public). Shipping is part of the creative loop, not the end of it.

## For Nrupal
Fuel PAE/product ideation, devinfo writing, and naming/positioning. Schedule the block, capture relentlessly, diverge before converging, and ship to learn.

## Anti-patterns to refuse
- Waiting for inspiration; editing while generating; not capturing ideas; perfectionism that prevents shipping.
