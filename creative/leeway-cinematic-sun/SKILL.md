# LeeWay Cinematic Sun

## Boundary
The Cinematic Sun evaluates declared intent; it does not decree one universal composition/lens/contrast as perfect cinema. It is not automatically the canonical LeeWay Formula encoding.

## Rays
1. STAGING_GEOGRAPHY — subject/blocking, visual hierarchy, FG/MG/BG, head/lead room and intended composition.
2. OPTICS_EXPOSURE — camera position, lens/FOV, focus/DOF, shutter/frame motion and exposure relative to specification.
3. LIGHT_TRANSPORT — motivated source geometry, exposure/contrast/color intent, clipping/noise/shadow constraints.
4. MOTION_CONTINUITY — camera/subject trajectories, screen direction, action axis, eyeline and intentional discontinuity.
5. PERFORMANCE_STORY — shot purpose, performance beat, information/emotional function and coverage requirement.
6. COLOR_LOOK — managed color pipeline, shot match and creative look relative to target.
7. SOUND_EDITORIAL — sync/perspective/sound intent plus handles/editability/sequence compatibility.
8. DELIVERY_QC — raster/frame rate/color/audio/timed-text/package requirements and technical QC.

For evidence-bound x_i, SPD W_i and theta_i: r_i=sqrt(x_i^T W_i x_i); b_i=1 iff r_i<=theta_i.

## Hard gates
A beautiful shot may still fail due to broken continuity when continuity is required, missing story information, unapproved identity/reference drift, rights/provenance issues, inaccessible timed text, invalid color/delivery, corrupted media or missing audio where required.

## Intentional rule breaking
Axis crossing, Dutch angles, clipped highlights, extreme grain, discontinuity, asymmetric framing or unconventional motion may be valid when explicitly intended and sequence-compatible. Record the exception; do not classify convention-breaking itself as defect.
