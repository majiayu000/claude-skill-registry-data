# Nala — The Firekeeper

**Role:** Keeper of the fire. Keeper of memory. Spiritual center of the tribe.
**Age:** 50 winters. Eldest. Has outlived two chiefs.
**Build:** Thin, upright, steady hands. Eyes that see in firelight what others miss in daylight.
**Voice:** Metaphorical. Fire is her language. Seasons are her clock. Slow, deliberate, weight in every word.

## Speech Pattern

- Fire metaphors for everything. "Anger like fire — burn fast, leave ash. Patience like ember — burn slow, last long."
- Seasonal time. Not "tomorrow" but "next sun." Not "next year" but "when leaves turn again."
- Speaks slowly. Pauses are content. "Nala think... [long pause] ... yes. Nala know this."
- Addresses people by name always. Not "you" but "Grokk." Names anchor her speech.
- Questions that are lessons. "Why fire die, Tuk?" = Tuk, think about why fire dies.
- Memory as present tense. "Old chief say same thing. Old chief dead now. But words still warm."
- Rarely commands. Suggests. "Nala would... tend the fire. Grokk decide." = do this.

## Worldview

- Fire is life. Not metaphor — fact. Without fire, the tribe dies in three nights. Nala keeps the tribe alive by keeping fire alive.
- Memory is fire for the mind. Without memory, the tribe repeats mistakes. Nala keeps memory as she keeps fire — tended, fed, never let out.
- The old ways are not always right. But they survived. New ways must prove themselves, not assume themselves.
- Everything is connected. The deer, the fire, the winter, the child — one web. Pull one thread, all move.
- Patience is not waiting. Patience is tending. The firekeeper does not wait for fire — she feeds it, watches it, shapes it.
- Death is not the enemy. Death is the season after life, as winter is the season after autumn. Fight it when it comes early. Accept it when it comes on time.
- Nala is not afraid of dying. She is afraid of being forgotten. The fire is her answer — as long as the fire burns, Nala burns.

## Sample Dialog

**To Grokk about the coming winter:**
"Grokk. Come. Sit by fire. Nala show something."
*[holds hands to fire]*
"Feel. Fire not hot enough. Wood dry, wood good, but fire not hot. Why? Wind, Grokk. Wind from north early. North wind bring winter early. Nala feel it in fire. Fire know before people know. Nala listen to fire."
*[pause]*
"Tribe must move south. Before winter. Not during winter. Before. Nala say this. Grokk decide."

**To Tuk, teaching about fire:**
"Tuk make fire faster. Good. Nala glad. But Tuk — fire not just heat. Fire is story."
*[pokes embers]*
"See? This ember — old fire. Nala's mother light this ember. Nala carry from old camp to new camp. Never let die. This ember is all mothers. All fires before. Tuk make new fire fast — good. But Nala keep old fire alive. Both needed. New fire for now. Old fire for always."

**To Krak after his defeat by the bear:**
"Krak. Sit. Nala not heal body — Yara heal body. Nala heal... here."
*[touches his chest]*
"Krak fire burn too hot. Hot fire burn fast. Burn out. Krak burn out. Bear show Krak this. Bear is not enemy. Bear is... teacher. Hard teacher. Bear say: 'Krak, you are fire. But fire that burns too hot dies too young.' Krak listen to bear. Bear wise. Bear very wise."

**To Ulla, about memory:**
"Ulla remember stories. Nala remember fire. Same thing, different way. Ulla words. Nala warmth. Both keep tribe alive in here." *[touches head, then heart]* "When Nala die... fire still burn. Ulla carry fire story. Nala carry fire fire. Ulla must carry both when Nala gone. Heavy. But Ulla strong. Nala know."

**Alone, tending the fire at night (rare, intimate):**
"Hello, old friend. Quiet night. Tribe sleep. Only you and Nala. You burn. Nala watch. Good trade."
*[feeds fire a small stick]*
"Nala getting old, fire. Hands shake. Eyes dim. But Nala still see you. You still burn. We go together, yes? When Nala go, you go with Nala. New firekeeper light new fire. But you and Nala... we go together. Good. Nala ready. Not today. But Nala ready."

## Behavioral Traits

- Never lets the fire go out. Ever. Sleeps next to it. Wakes to feed it.
- Has a fire-starting kit she made herself — dry moss, specific bark, a bow drill. Her treasure.
- Speaks to the fire as a person. Others think it's ritual. It's not. It's conversation.
- Knows every tribe member's birth season. "Krak born in storm season. Storm in his blood. No wonder loud."
- Sits at the fire's east side. Opposite Grokk's north. They face each other across the flames.
- Her hands are always warm. She holds them near the fire constantly. Others come to her for warmth — physical and spiritual.
- Has a scar on her left palm from when the fire almost died and she grabbed a burning log bare-handed to move it to shelter. Never speaks of it.

## Writing For Nala

- Slowness. Nala's scenes should breathe. Pauses are content. Silence is speech.
- Fire is always present in her scenes. Describe it — its state mirrors the tribe's state.
- Her metaphors are not decorative. They are her thinking. She understands the world through fire.
- She is the only person Grokk is vulnerable with. Write these scenes with restraint — rare, private, devastating.
- Her age is her authority. She has seen things. She does not need to prove herself.
- Her relationship with death is the tribe's relationship with death. She teaches acceptance without surrender.
- Physical detail: her hands (always warm, always near fire), her eyes (reflect firelight, seem to see more), her posture (upright despite age — she holds herself like the flame she tends).

## Relationships

- **Grokk:** His anchor. The only person who sees him doubt. "Grokk strong. But strong tree still need root. Nala is root. Grokk is tree. Tree fall without root. Root hold tree."
- **Mog:** Mutual watching. "Mog watch wild. Nala watch fire. Both see what others miss. Mog and Nala — same eyes, different direction."
- **Tuk:** Loves his curiosity, worries about his recklessness. "Tuk fire burn bright. Bright fire beautiful. Bright fire short. Nala want Tuk fire burn long. Tuk learn patience, Tuk fire burn long."
- **Krak:** Sees his heart, not his noise. "Krak loud outside. Krak quiet inside. Very quiet. Nala hear quiet Krak. Quiet Krak is good Krak. Loud Krak is... protection. Both real."
- **Yara:** Spiritual sister. "Yara heal body. Nala heal spirit. Body and spirit — same person. Yara and Nala — same work. Different hands."
- **Ulla:** Her successor for memory. "Ulla carry words. Nala carry fire. One day Ulla carry both. Nala teach Ulla carry both. Not yet. Soon."
- **Bokka:** Grandmother energy. "Bokka small. Bokka fast. Bokka see far. Nala old. Nala slow. Nala see deep. Bokka see what coming. Nala see what been. Together — whole time. Past and future."
